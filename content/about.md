# About MELBZ

MELBZ is Melbourne's hyperlocal intelligence platform. We cover every corner of this city — **321 suburbs**, thousands of venues, and the local knowledge you'll never find on a generic review site.

We don't do surface-level. Every suburb guide, every restaurant recommendation, every vibe score is built on real data and genuine local experience. No sponsored fluff. No tourist traps dressed up as hidden gems.

---

## Our Founder

**Ebiau Daniel** — Founder & CEO

Ebiau Daniel built MELBZ because he was tired of searching for suburb information and getting the same recycled content from sites that had clearly never set foot in the places they reviewed.

A Melbourne local with a background in technology and digital media, Ebiau founded [Axior Labs](https://axiorlabs.com.au) to build digital platforms that deliver genuinely useful information. MELBZ is the flagship — a hyperlocal platform that treats every suburb as its own story worth telling properly.

> "Melbourne is 321 suburbs, each with its own personality. You can't describe Footscray the same way you describe Toorak. We don't try to. Every suburb gets the attention it deserves." — Ebiau Daniel

### How It Works

Every article on MELBZ goes through a quality pipeline:
1. **Research** — AI agents gather data from multiple sources
2. **Draft** — Content is generated following our strict style guide
3. **Quality Check** — Automated and manual review for accuracy
4. **Publish** — Content goes live with proper schema markup and SEO
5. **Update** — Articles are refreshed when venues open, close, or change

All AI-generated content is reviewed for accuracy and clearly labelled. We believe in transparency — this site is built with AI, and we're proud of what that technology can do when applied thoughtfully.
