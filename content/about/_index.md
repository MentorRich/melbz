## We Argue About Melbourne For a Living

Seriously. That's basically the job description.

MELBZ started because Melbourne has a problem: too much noise, not enough signal. Every suburb has a hundred listicles telling you it's "vibrant" and "up-and-coming." Every cafe gets called a "hidden gem" the day it opens. Every neighbourhood guide was written by someone who visited once on a sunny Saturday and decided they understood the place.

We don't do that.

We live here. We argue about it. We have strong opinions about which end of Fitzroy is better, whether Prahran or South Yarra actually counts as two suburbs, whether the 86 tram is a lifestyle choice or a punishment, and why anyone would voluntarily move to Doncaster (no offence, Doncaster).

## What MELBZ Actually Is

**321 suburbs. Updated constantly. Written by people who care too much.**

MELBZ is Melbourne's hyperlocal intelligence platform. We dig into every suburb so you don't have to guess whether it's actually walkable, actually affordable, or actually worth the rent premium. We track venue openings and closings, document the real commute times (not the "15 minutes via train if you leave at 6:42am exactly"), and give you the honest picture of what it's like to live somewhere — not just visit.

Our coverage includes:

- **Suburb intelligence** — the real vibe, the real costs, the honest upsides and downsides
- **Food and coffee** — we eat out constantly and have opinions, we're not sorry
- **Nightlife and culture** — what's actually good, what's just Instagram-friendly
- **Property and renting** — straight numbers, no real estate agent spin
- **Transport** — actual commute testing, not Google Maps estimates

## The Team

There are roughly 12-15 of us at any given time. We're a mix of journalists, food obsessives, ex-real estate people (reformed), urban planners who got tired of planning, and several people who moved suburbs once and became convinced they're experts on the entire city.

We're based out of multiple Melbourne suburbs — which creates constant internal arguments that we have decided to treat as a competitive advantage.

**Opinions held strongly and occasionally revised** — that's the deal.

## Why We Started

Because we kept moving around Melbourne and the existing information was garbage.

"Best cafes in Fitzroy" articles that hadn't been updated since 2019. "Cost of living" guides that used Airbnb prices to estimate rental costs. "Hidden gems" that were hidden because they closed three years ago.

We wanted something that actually tracked the city in real time, written by people who could tell the difference between a suburb that was genuinely changing and one that just got a new bar reviewed in *Good Food*.

So we built it.

## How We Work

We have contributors in suburbs across Melbourne. When something changes — a new venue, a development application, a price spike — we hear about it.

We also:
- **Google Street View obsessively** (yes, we know)
- **Crowdsource from locals** via poll widgets and comments
- **Use real data** — PTV for transport, REIV/CoreLogic for property, ABS for demographics
- **Actually test things** — if we say the tram takes 12 minutes, someone timed it

We try to be honest. Sometimes that means saying a suburb is overpriced for what it offers, or that the cafe everyone's posting about is actually average. People don't always love that. We do it anyway.
