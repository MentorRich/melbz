# Meet the MELBZ

MELBZ is written by 50 Melbourne locals who collectively cover 321 suburbs. Our writers don't parachute in for a Saturday afternoon visit and call it research. They live in these suburbs, drink at these bars, walk these streets daily, and have strong opinions about all of it.

## How We Work

Every MELBZ article goes through a process that most city guides skip entirely:

1. **Local knowledge first.** Each suburb guide is assigned to a writer who lives in or adjacent to that suburb. No exceptions.
2. **On-the-ground verification.** If we recommend a venue, someone from the team has been there in the last 90 days. We don't copy Google reviews.
3. **Real prices, real hours.** We check opening hours, menu prices, and transport options ourselves. If something changes, we update the article.
4. **No sponsored placements.** Venues don't pay to appear in our guides. If a place is good, it's in. If it's not, it's not. We've upset a few publicists this way.
