## Where the Money Actually Goes

**Groceries:** Standard supermarket territory. There's a Woolies or Coles accessible, plus a couple of smaller shops. Budget $80-$120/week for one person.

**Eating out:** The average meal out in Albert Park runs $18-$25 for lunch and $30-$50 for dinner. Check our [cheap eats guide](/albert-park/cheap-eats/) for the spots that don't punish your wallet.

**Transport:** A full-price Myki costs $10 daily cap, $50 weekly cap in Zone 1-2. If you're in Albert Park, you're firmly in Zone 1, which keeps your Myki costs predictable. An annual Myki pass is the smart move if you commute daily.

## The Hidden Costs Nobody Warns You About

1. **Parking permits** — If you drive, council parking permits run $50-$150/year depending on the area
2. **Brunch culture tax** — You will spend more on breakfast than you budget for. It's Melbourne law.
3. **The "quick drink after work" budget** — A pint in Albert Park averages $12-$14. Cocktails run $18-$24.
4. **Laundry** — If your apartment doesn't have a washing machine, coin laundry runs $8-$12 per load

## Is Albert Park Worth the Cost?

Albert Park is mid-range for Melbourne — not cheap, not eye-watering. You get a A- walkability score and B+ food scene for the price. It's a fair deal if those things matter to your daily life.
