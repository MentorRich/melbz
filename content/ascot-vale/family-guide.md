Thinking about raising kids in Ascot Vale? Here is what parents actually need to know. Sitting 6km northwest of the CBD with Showgrounds station for train access, Ascot Vale offers families a balance between inner-city proximity and a genuine residential feel. The Maribyrnong River, Flemington Racecourse, and Union Road village provide the backdrop.

**Family Friendliness Grade: B+**

## Schools

**Ascot Vale Primary School** on Ascot Vale Road is the main local primary and is well-regarded. **Ascot Vale West Primary** provides an additional option. **St Mary's Primary** (Catholic) on Union Road is also accessible. Enrolment zones matter — check your address against the Department of Education boundaries before making assumptions.

For secondary, **Buckley Park College** in Essendon and schools in Moonee Ponds are accessible by train or bus. Some families look at selective-entry schools in the CBD, which are about 15 to 20 minutes by train from Showgrounds station.

## Parks & Green Spaces

Ascot Vale's green space is one of its genuine strengths for families.

**Maribyrnong River trails** — The river runs along the western edge of the suburb, and the shared walking and cycling trail is flat, paved, and pram-friendly. Families use it daily for walks, cycling, and riverside picnics.

**Ascot Vale Reserve** and **Riverside Park** provide playground equipment, open grass, and BBQ facilities. The reserve hosts weekend sport — cricket, football, and junior club games.

**Flemington Racecourse parkland** — The areas around the racecourse precinct provide additional open space for walking and play outside of race events.

Smaller pocket parks throughout the residential streets off Union Road and Maribyrnong Road give families options for after-school play within walking distance.

## Safety for Families

Ascot Vale feels safe for families. Union Road is well-lit and populated during the day and evening. The residential streets off Maribyrnong Road and Ascot Vale Road are quiet and well-maintained. The community is a mix of young professionals and families, which keeps the streets active during school hours and weekends.

Standard Melbourne precautions apply at night, particularly near the Showgrounds precinct on event days.

## Kid-Friendly Eating

Union Road [cafes](/ascot-vale/best-cafes/) are family-friendly by default. High chairs are standard, outdoor seating gives kids space, and the general atmosphere is relaxed about children. The cafes along Union Road between Maribyrnong Road and Ascot Vale Road are the main options, with bakeries and brunch spots that work for Saturday morning family outings.

## Weekend Activities

**Maribyrnong River trail** is the headline weekend activity for families — cycling, walking, scootering, or just finding a spot by the river for a picnic. The trail connects south to Footscray and north through Essendon.

**Local sporting clubs** have strong junior programs for cricket, football, netball, and tennis. Ascot Vale Reserve hosts weekend matches.

**Showgrounds events** — The Melbourne Showgrounds (adjacent to the station) hosts markets, food events, and seasonal exhibitions that are often family-oriented.

**Ascot Vale Library** runs storytime sessions and school holiday programs.

## The Commute Factor

Showgrounds station is on the Sunbury line, with trains to the CBD taking about 15 to 20 minutes. School drop-offs add time to your morning, and the before/after-school care window matters. If both parents work, the fast train connection is a genuine advantage.

See our [Ascot Vale Transport Guide](/ascot-vale/transport-guide/) for the full breakdown.

## The Family Verdict

Ascot Vale works well for families who want inner-city proximity without sacrificing green space. The Maribyrnong River, multiple parks, and solid local primary schools create a practical family setup. You will share the suburb with a younger, social crowd — which is either a pro or a con depending on your stage. One-bedroom rents around $370 per week make it pricier than outer suburbs but reasonable for 6km from the CBD.

## FAQ

**Is Ascot Vale good for families?**
Yes. The Maribyrnong River trails, multiple parks, solid primary schools, and a 15 to 20 minute train to the CBD make it a strong option for families in the inner northwest.

**What schools are in Ascot Vale?**
Ascot Vale Primary, Ascot Vale West Primary, and St Mary's Primary (Catholic) are the main primary options. Secondary students typically travel to Buckley Park College in Essendon or schools in Moonee Ponds.

**Are there playgrounds in Ascot Vale?**
Yes. Ascot Vale Reserve and Riverside Park have playground equipment. Smaller pocket parks throughout the residential streets also have play areas.

**Is Ascot Vale safe for families?**
Ascot Vale is considered safe for families. Union Road is well-lit, residential streets are quiet, and the community is active. Standard precautions apply near the Showgrounds during major events.
