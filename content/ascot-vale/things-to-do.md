## What Is There to Do in Ascot Vale?

Ascot Vale sits 6km northwest of the CBD, with the Maribyrnong River along its western edge and Flemington Racecourse practically next door. Union Road provides the village-style commercial strip, and the river trails make this one of the better suburbs in the inner northwest for outdoor activities. There is more to do here than the suburb's quiet residential streets initially suggest.

## Outdoor Activities

**Maribyrnong River trail** — The main attraction for outdoor activity. A flat, paved shared path running along the river that connects south to Footscray and north toward Essendon. Running, cycling, walking, and dog walking all happen here daily. Weekend mornings on the trail are a social event in their own right.

**Ascot Vale Reserve** — Open grass, sporting ovals, and play areas. Weekend cricket and football matches bring the community together. The reserve is also good for casual exercise and picnics.

**Flemington Racecourse perimeter** — The roads around the racecourse (Epsom Road, Fisher Parade) provide a flat loop for running or walking. Outside of race days, the precinct is quiet and practical for exercise.

**Weekend Sport** — Local sporting clubs for cricket, football, netball, and tennis are active and have strong junior programs. Ascot Vale Reserve and nearby ovals host weekend matches year-round.

## Food and Drink Experiences

**Cafe Hopping on Union Road** — Start at the Maribyrnong Road end and work your way east. Union Road cafes serve Melbourne-standard coffee with brunch menus that range from classic to creative. Saturday morning on Union Road is Ascot Vale's social scene at its peak.

**Restaurant Discovery** — Italian, Modern Australian, and pub dining options line Union Road and the surrounding streets. The quality has improved noticeably in recent years as the suburb's demographics have shifted.

**Showgrounds Markets** — The Melbourne Showgrounds (adjacent to Showgrounds station) hosts regular markets including food markets, vintage markets, and seasonal events. These are a genuine drawcard and worth checking the calendar for.

## Culture and Creative

**Melbourne Showgrounds events** — Beyond the Royal Melbourne Show, the Showgrounds precinct hosts food and wine events, cultural festivals, trade shows, and community markets throughout the year. The event calendar is worth monitoring.

**Live Music** — Several Ascot Vale pubs book local acts. The scene is small but consistent.

**Maribyrnong River precinct** — The riverside area has a creative energy that attracts artists and makers. Warehouse studios and creative businesses operate in the light industrial areas near the river.

## Family-Friendly Activities

- **Ascot Vale Reserve playground** — Play equipment for different age groups
- **Maribyrnong River trail** — Pram-friendly, flat path for family walks and cycling
- **Ascot Vale Library** — Storytime sessions, school holiday programs, free WiFi
- **Showgrounds events** — Family-oriented markets and seasonal events
- **Flemington Racecourse open days** — Family-friendly race days with kids' activities

## Rainy Day Options

Melbourne weather means you need indoor plans. Ascot Vale has:
- **Ascot Vale Library** — Free WiFi, quiet space, community programs
- **[Cafes](/ascot-vale/best-cafes/) on Union Road** — Grab a book and settle in
- **Highpoint Shopping Centre** — A short drive west for cinema, retail, and food court
- **Melbourne Showgrounds** — Indoor events and exhibitions run regularly

## Free Things to Do

1. Walk or cycle the Maribyrnong River trail — flat, scenic, and kilometres long
2. Browse Union Road's cafes and independent shops
3. People-watch from an outdoor table on Union Road with a takeaway coffee
4. Explore the residential streets between Union Road and Ascot Vale Road — the heritage homes are worth seeing
5. Join a free community fitness group at Ascot Vale Reserve
6. Visit Ascot Vale Library for events and community programs

## FAQ

**What is there to do in Ascot Vale on weekends?**
The Maribyrnong River trail is the main outdoor activity. Union Road cafes and restaurants are busy on weekends. The Melbourne Showgrounds hosts regular markets and events. Sporting clubs run weekend matches at Ascot Vale Reserve.

**Is Ascot Vale good for cycling?**
Yes. The Maribyrnong River trail provides a flat, traffic-separated cycling route that connects south to the city and north to Essendon. The residential streets are also comfortable for cycling.

**What is Union Road Ascot Vale?**
Union Road is the main commercial strip — cafes, restaurants, shops, and essential services in a walkable village-style layout. It is the social hub of the suburb.

**How far is Ascot Vale from the Melbourne CBD?**
6km northwest. About 15 to 20 minutes by train from Showgrounds station, or 20 to 25 minutes by bike along the Maribyrnong River trail.
