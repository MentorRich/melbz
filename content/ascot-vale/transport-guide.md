Ascot Vale's transport game scored **B+** in our latest review. Sitting 6km northwest of the CBD, the suburb has train access via Showgrounds station, tram access along Mount Alexander Road, and the Maribyrnong River trail for cycling. Here is what that looks like in practice.

## Getting to the CBD

**By train:** Showgrounds station is on the Sunbury line. Trains to Flagstaff take about 15 to 20 minutes during peak, running every 8 to 12 minutes. This is the fastest commute option.

**By tram:** Route 57 runs along Maribyrnong Road through Ascot Vale and into the CBD via Flemington and North Melbourne. The tram takes about 25 to 35 minutes depending on traffic and stops. Route 59 along Mount Alexander Road in nearby Moonee Ponds is also accessible.

**By bike:** The Maribyrnong River trail provides a traffic-separated route toward the city. Cycling to the CBD takes about 20 to 25 minutes via the trail and connecting paths through Flemington.

## Train Details

**Showgrounds station** sits on the Sunbury (formerly Watergardens) line. It is located near the Melbourne Showgrounds precinct and serves the southern part of Ascot Vale.

**Peak frequency:** Every 8 to 12 minutes.
**Off-peak:** Every 15 to 20 minutes.
**Night Network:** Hourly all-night services on Friday and Saturday nights.

**Ascot Vale station** (also on the lines running through the area) and **Newmarket station** provide additional options depending on where you live within the suburb.

## Tram Routes

**Route 57** runs along Maribyrnong Road and connects Ascot Vale to the CBD via West Melbourne and North Melbourne. Stops along Maribyrnong Road in Ascot Vale provide access to the Union Road area.

**Route 59** runs along Mount Alexander Road in nearby Moonee Ponds, accessible from the eastern edge of Ascot Vale.

Trams are useful if your destination is along the route rather than in the City Loop. The train is faster for city-centre commutes.

## Bus Routes

Buses connect Ascot Vale to surrounding suburbs including Moonee Ponds, Essendon, Flemington, and Highpoint Shopping Centre. Frequency is variable — main routes run every 15 to 20 minutes during peak, less on weekends.

## Cycling

Ascot Vale is strong for cycling. The **Maribyrnong River trail** runs along the western edge of the suburb and provides a flat, paved, traffic-separated route toward the city. You can connect to the Capital City Trail near Flemington for a direct route into the CBD.

Heading north, the river trail connects to Essendon and the Moonee Ponds Creek Trail. The flat terrain and wide residential streets make local cycling comfortable.

## Driving & Parking

Parking in Ascot Vale is manageable. Most houses have driveways. Street parking near Union Road has time restrictions during business hours but residential streets are generally unrestricted.

**CityLink access** is practical — you can reach the airport in about 15 to 20 minutes, the western suburbs quickly, and the freeway network without going through the CBD.

## The Verdict

Ascot Vale gets a **B+** for transport because it offers train, tram, and cycling options that all work. The 15 to 20 minute train to the CBD is solid, the tram provides an alternative, and the Maribyrnong River trail makes cycling a genuine commute option rather than just recreation. The main gap is bus frequency for cross-suburban trips.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to get from Ascot Vale to the CBD?**
By train, about 15 to 20 minutes to Flagstaff. By tram (Route 57), about 25 to 35 minutes. By bike, about 20 to 25 minutes via the Maribyrnong River trail.

**What train station is in Ascot Vale?**
Showgrounds station on the Sunbury line is the primary station. Ascot Vale station and Newmarket station also serve parts of the suburb.

**Does Ascot Vale have tram access?**
Yes. Route 57 runs along Maribyrnong Road through the suburb. Route 59 is also accessible from nearby Moonee Ponds.

**Can you cycle to the CBD from Ascot Vale?**
Yes. The Maribyrnong River trail provides a traffic-separated route, connecting to the Capital City Trail near Flemington. About 20 to 25 minutes to the CBD.
