# Our Writers

MELBZ is written by people who live here. Not a content farm. Not AI-generated listicles. Every article is written by a named local contributor who knows their patch — the cafes that quietly close at 2pm, the tram routes that run late, the rent prices that the brochures conveniently leave out.

Our writers are assigned suburbs and beats based on where they live and what they know. If Marcus Cole writes about Richmond property prices, it's because he lives there and watches the auction results every Saturday. If Dani Reyes reviews a Fitzroy cafe, she's been there at least twice.
