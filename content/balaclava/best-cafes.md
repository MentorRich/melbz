# Best Cafes & Brunch in Balaclava 2026

Balaclava's cafe scene has evolved into one of Melbourne's most exciting culinary destinations. This inner-suburb, with its rich Jewish heritage and multicultural influences, offers a diverse range of breakfast and brunch experiences that go well beyond the typical smashed avo on toast. From traditional Eastern European bakeries to innovative modern cafes, Balaclava serves up something for every palate.

## 1. Milk the Cow Balaclava

**Address:** 155 Carlisle Street, Balaclava VIC 3183

Milk the Cow brings their signature cheese and wine expertise to breakfast with a sophisticated twist. Their weekend brunch menu features artisanal cheese boards alongside freshly baked pastries, making it perfect for those who prefer savoury morning options. The cafe's commitment to local producers shines through in their carefully curated coffee selection and house-made preserves.

## 2. Las Chicas

**Address:** 203 Carlisle Street, Balaclava VIC 3183

A Balaclava institution, Las Chicas continues to set the standard for brunch in the area. Their Middle Eastern-inspired breakfast menu includes standout dishes like shakshuka with house-made labneh and za'atar-spiced eggs. The spacious courtyard provides a perfect setting for weekend catch-ups, while their efficient service ensures you're never waiting too long for your morning coffee.

## 3. The Little Pocket

**Address:** 168 Carlisle Street, Balaclava VIC 3183

This cozy corner cafe focuses on wholesome, nourishing breakfast options. Their menu features everything from gluten-free buckwheat pancakes to hearty breakfast bowls packed with seasonal vegetables. The Little Pocket's commitment to sustainability extends to their packaging and sourcing, making it a favourite among environmentally conscious locals.

## 4. Glicks Cafe

**Address:** 127 Acland Street, St Kilda VIC 3182

While technically across the border in St Kilda, Glicks deserves a mention for its proximity to Balaclava and its cultural significance. This kosher bakery and cafe has been serving traditional Jewish delicacies since 1969. Their challah French toast and smoked salmon bagels remain local favourites, drawing crowds from across Melbourne.

## 5. Third Roast

**Address:** 181 Carlisle Street, Balaclava VIC 3183

Third Roast has quickly become a local favourite since opening, known for its exceptional coffee and innovative brunch dishes. Their menu changes seasonally, but always features creative takes on breakfast classics. The smashed peas with whipped feta and poached eggs has become something of a signature dish, while their vegan options are equally impressive.

## 6. Fitzrovia

**Address:** 169 Fitzroy Street, St Kilda VIC 3182

Another nearby venue worth the short walk from Balaclava, Fitzrovia offers a bright, airy space perfect for weekend brunching. Their all-day breakfast menu includes everything from traditional eggs Benedict to more adventurous options like kimchi scrambled eggs. The bakery section is particularly noteworthy, with fresh pastries and bread baked daily.

## 7. Monk Bodhi Dharma

**Address:** 202 Carlisle Street, Balaclava VIC 3183

This specialty coffee roaster and cafe has built a loyal following for its expertly brewed coffee and thoughtful plant-based menu. Their breakfast offerings include creative dishes like sweet potato and black bean hash, while their famous avocado on toast comes with unique toppings like dukkah and pickled radish. The cafe's commitment to ethical sourcing extends from their coffee beans to their produce.

## 8. The Beatt

**Address:** 187 Carlisle Street, Balaclava VIC 3183

The Beatt combines a health-focused cafe with a fitness studio, creating a unique wellness destination. Their breakfast menu features nutrient-dense options like açai bowls, protein pancakes, and superfood smoothies. The bright, modern interior makes it an ideal spot for post-workout recovery meals or a healthy start to the day.

## The Balaclava Cafe Scene

What makes Balaclava's cafe scene special is its authenticity and diversity. Unlike some Melbourne suburbs where cafes can feel formulaic, Balaclava's venues each have their own distinct personality and culinary focus. The suburb's strong Jewish community influence is evident in many cafes, with excellent bagels, challah, and other traditional baked goods readily available.

The Carlisle Street strip, where most of these cafes are located, has become a destination in itself. Wide footpaths and plenty of outdoor seating make it perfect for people-watching while enjoying your morning coffee. Many cafes have embraced the suburb's multicultural character, incorporating Middle Eastern, Eastern European, and modern Australian influences into their menus.

Coffee culture in Balaclava is particularly strong, with most cafes taking their brews seriously. Whether you prefer a classic flat white, a pour-over, or something more experimental, you'll find skilled baristas ready to craft your perfect cup. Several venues also roast their own beans, ensuring maximum freshness and unique flavour profiles.

For those planning a brunch outing in Balaclava, timing can be everything. While weekdays tend to be quieter, weekends see many cafes packed on weekends with locals and visitors alike. Most venues open around 7am on weekdays and 8am on weekends, with breakfast service typically continuing until around 3pm.

The suburb's excellent public transport connections, including the Balaclava train station and multiple tram routes, make it easily accessible from across Melbourne. For those driving, parking can be challenging on weekends, so consider public transport or arriving early to secure a spot.

Whether you're a local or just visiting, Balaclava's cafe scene offers something special. From traditional Jewish bakeries to innovative modern cafes, the suburb continues to evolve while maintaining its unique character. Each venue brings something different to the table, ensuring that breakfast and brunch in Balaclava never gets boring.
