Balaclava rents sit below the inner south-east median, making it one of the more accessible train-connected postcodes. Here are the 2026 numbers by property type and how they compare to neighbours.

## By Location Within Balaclava

**Carlisle Street frontage:** Slight premium for walkability. One-beds $420-$450.
**Hotham Street (near station):** Most in-demand. Two-beds $500-$560.
**Inkerman Street area:** Quieter, slightly cheaper. One-beds $380-$420.
**Brighton Road edge:** Less foot traffic, more space. Two-beds $480-$530.

## FAQ

**Why is Balaclava cheaper than St Kilda?**
Less [nightlife](/balaclava/nightlife-guide/), less beachfront premium, smaller commercial strip. The trade-off is genuine value for money with better train access.

**What is the best value in Balaclava?**
Period one-bedroom apartments near the station on Hotham Street. Solid buildings, $380-$420/week, walking distance to the train.

## The Verdict

Balaclava offers one of the best rent-to-[transport](/balaclava/transport-guide/) ratios in the inner south-east. You pay less than St Kilda and get direct train access. The period apartment stock provides affordable entry. Carlisle Street dining keeps food costs manageable. For renters comparing inner south-east options, Balaclava's numbers make a strong case.
