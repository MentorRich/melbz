## The best restaurants, suburb by suburb

### Fitzroy: The benchmark

Fitzroy sets the standard for Melbourne\u2019s modern dining scene. The restaurants here are confident, creative and often groundbreaking, spanning everything from casual bistros to degustation\u2011only counters. Brunswick Street and Gertrude Street are the main strips, but don\u2019t ignore the side streets and laneways. Our [definitive guide to Fitzroy\u2019s best restaurants](/fitzroy/best-restaurants/) covers every essential stop, with notes on vibe, price and what to order.

### Carlton: Italian soul

Carlton\u2019s heart beats along Lygon Street, where red\u2011and\u2011white checkered tablecloths share the footpath with modern enotecas and espresso bars. The Italian\u2011Australian institutions here are part of Melbourne\u2019s culinary DNA, but there\u2019s also a growing wave of contemporary kitchens that respect tradition while pushing forward. For the full picture, start with our [guide to Carlton\u2019s best restaurants](/carlton/best-restaurants/) (coming soon)\u2014we\u2019re currently updating our list for 2026.

### St Kilda: Bayside classics

St Kilda serves up seafood with a view, historic pubs with great parmas, and Acland Street\u2019s cake\u2011shop legends. The vibe is relaxed, the servings are generous, and the people\u2011watching is world\u2011class. Our St Kilda restaurant guide is in development\u2014check back soon for the must\u2011visit spots, from fish\u2011and\u2011chip shops to fine\u2011dining rooms overlooking the bay.

### Brunswick, Collingwood, Richmond and beyond

The inner\u2011north and inner\u2011east are packed with destination\u2011worthy restaurants. Brunswick\u2019s Middle Eastern bakeries, Collingwood\u2019s hipster\u2011chic cafes, Richmond\u2019s Vietnamese strip\u2014each suburb has its own culinary identity. ## How we choose

Every restaurant on this list meets three criteria:

1. **It\u2019s exceptional at what it does.** Whether that\u2019s handmade pasta, wood\u2011fired steak, or a 5 bowl of noodles.
2. **It has a clear point of view.** The best Melbourne restaurants aren\u2019t generic\u2014they have a chef\u2019s personality, a regional focus, or a story behind the food.
3. **It\u2019s consistently good.** We revisit, we monitor reviews, we talk to locals. Flashes in the pan don\u2019t make the cut.

We update this guide quarterly to reflect openings, closings and shifts in quality. The version you\u2019re reading is current for March 2026.
