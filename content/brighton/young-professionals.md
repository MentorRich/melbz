## The After-Work Scene

Let's be honest: Brighton isn't a nightlife suburb. You've got a local pub and maybe a wine bar. The trade-off is quiet streets and a shorter commute to somewhere that does have nightlife.

## The Weekend Factor

Weekends in Brighton are chill. Brunch is possible but not a competitive sport. You'll find your routine — a preferred cafe, a go-to park, and the occasional exploration of nearby strips.

## The Commute

From Brighton to the CBD: 30-45 minutes by public transport. Services are less frequent off-peak. Factor in walking time to the station. For most young professionals, the commute from Brighton is the main downside.

## Is Brighton Worth It for Young Professionals?

Brighton works for young professionals who prioritise lifestyle balance over a buzzing social scene. It's not the flashiest choice, but it's smart.
