## The After-Work Scene

Brunswick East has enough to keep you out on a weeknight. A few solid bars, a pub or two worth knowing, and the occasional late-night spot. For bigger nights, you're an Uber from the CBD.

## The Weekend Factor

Weekend brunch is a religion in Brunswick East. Saturday mornings mean queues at the top cafes, followed by market browsing, park sessions, or catching up on sleep. Sundays are slower but the coffee is still exceptional.

## The Commute

From Brunswick East to the CBD: 20-35 minutes by public transport. Trains run every 10-15 minutes during peak. Reliable but not the fastest commute. For most young professionals, the commute from Brunswick East is manageable.

## Is Brunswick East Worth It for Young Professionals?

Brunswick East works for young professionals who prioritise food and walkability over a buzzing social scene. It's not the flashiest choice, but it's smart.
