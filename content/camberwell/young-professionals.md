## The After-Work Scene

Let's be honest: Camberwell isn't a nightlife suburb. You've got a local pub and maybe a wine bar. The trade-off is quiet streets and a shorter commute to somewhere that does have nightlife.

## The Weekend Factor

Weekends in Camberwell are chill. Brunch is possible but not a competitive sport. You'll find your routine — a preferred cafe, a go-to park, and the occasional exploration of nearby strips.

## The Commute

From Camberwell to the CBD: 10-20 minutes by train or tram. Services run every 5-10 minutes during peak. This is one of the best-connected suburbs in Melbourne. For most young professionals, the commute from Camberwell is a non-issue.

## Is Camberwell Worth It for Young Professionals?

Camberwell works for young professionals who prioritise lifestyle balance over a buzzing social scene. It's not the flashiest choice, but it's smart.
