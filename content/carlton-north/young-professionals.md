## The Weekend Factor

Weekend brunch is a religion in Carlton North. Saturday mornings mean queues at the top cafes, followed by market browsing, park sessions, or catching up on sleep. Sundays are slower but the coffee is still exceptional.

## The Commute

From Carlton North to the CBD: 20-35 minutes by public transport. Trains run every 10-15 minutes during peak. Reliable but not the fastest commute. For most young professionals, the commute from Carlton North is manageable.

## Is Carlton North Worth It for Young Professionals?

Carlton North works for young professionals who prioritise food and walkability over a buzzing social scene. It's not the flashiest choice, but it's smart.
