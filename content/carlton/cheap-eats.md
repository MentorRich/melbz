Here are the 15 best cheap eats in Carlton for 2026, every single one under $20. From hand-folded dumplings and Malaysian rice bowls to Neapolitan pizza slices and Bhutanese momos, Carlton's student-driven food scene means you eat brilliantly without thinking about your bank balance. This guide covers Lygon Street, Grattan Street, Elgin Street and the laneways in between.

**What's inside:**
- **Dumpling dealers:** Aunty's Dumplings, Shanghai Village, HuTong
- **Noodle temples:** Hakata Gensuke, Lagoon Dining
- **Pizza by the slice:** DOC, Garfield Pizzeria, Leonardo's
- **Global budget bangers:** Rice Bar, Taquito, Panda Hot Pot
- **New openings 2026:** Aunty's Dumplings, Garfield Pizzeria, Florian To Go

Carlton has a secret weapon that no other Melbourne suburb can match: a permanent population of approximately 30,000 university students who demand excellent food at prices that won't bankrupt them before mid-semester exams. The result is one of the most competitive budget dining strips in Australia, where restaurants know that a $25 main means empty tables while a $15 bowl means a queue out the door.

Lygon Street gets all the attention — and fair enough, the Italian precinct has been Carlton's culinary calling card since the 1950s — but the real cheap eats action has shifted. Today, you'll find the best budget food scattered across Grattan Street's Malaysian and Chinese joints, Elgin Street's takeaway counters, and a new generation of openings bringing Korean, Japanese and Mexican flavours at prices that make the old-school Italian spots look expensive. Carlton sits right next to [Fitzroy](/fitzroy/), where the food scene leans pricier and more experimental — check our [Fitzroy restaurant guide](/fitzroy/best-restaurants/) for a splurge.

Whether you're a Melbourne Uni student between lectures, a Carlton local who refuses to cook on a Tuesday, or a visitor who wants to eat well without dropping $50, this guide has you covered. Every venue below delivers a proper meal — not a snack, not a side dish — for under $20. Most come in well under $15. Let's eat.

## 1. Aunty's Dumplings

**The vibe:** A mother-daughter operation folding 1,000 dumplings a day from a tiny Carlton shopfront. This is the real deal.

Aunty's Dumplings opened in February 2026 and immediately became one of Carlton's most exciting new cheap eats. The concept is beautifully simple: a mother and her daughter make northern Chinese dumplings by hand, every single day, and sell them at prices that seem like a clerical error. The fillings rotate but always include pork and chive, prawn, and a vegetarian option. Everything is made the day it's sold — no frozen shortcuts, no bulk prep. You can taste the difference.

Broadsheet featured Aunty's in their "lunch under $20" roundup within weeks of opening, and the queues are already building. Get in early.

**Order this:** Pork and chive dumplings (12 for $12)
**Also try:** Handmade noodle soup ($14)
**Address:** Lygon Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Tue-Sun 11:00am-8:30pm
**Price range:** $ (most dishes $10-16)
**Insider tip:** Go for the boiled dumplings over fried — they let the handmade wrappers shine.

## 2. Garfield Pizzeria

**The vibe:** The King & Godfree building roars back to life with Jamie Valmorbida's new pizza joint, complete with Pidapipo soft serve for afters.

When one of Lygon Street's most iconic buildings — the heritage-listed King & Godfree — sat dormant for 18 months, Carlton felt the loss. Garfield Pizzeria is the first of three new venues from Jamie Valmorbida that's breathing life back into the space, and it's landed perfectly. Classic and creative pizzas, natural wines, and Pidapipo soft serve in one of Carlton's most beautiful rooms. A margherita comes in under $20; a scoop of soft serve for $6 makes it a proper night out for $25.

**Order this:** Margherita pizza ($18)
**Also try:** Pidapipo soft serve ($6)
**Address:** 293 Lygon Street, Carlton VIC 3053 (King & Godfree building)
**Hours:** Wed-Sun 5:00pm-10:00pm
**Price range:** $$ (pizzas $18-26, soft serve $6-10)
**Insider tip:** Garfield is the first cab off the rank — two more venues are coming to the building in 2026. Watch this space.

## 3. Rice Bar (Not Only Rice)

**The vibe:** Lightning-fast Malaysian rice bowls for under $15 in a tiny Grattan Street spot that punches absurdly above its weight.

Rice Bar has been quietly feeding Carlton's student population for years with some of the best value Malaysian food in inner Melbourne. The Kung Pao chicken with rice ($12.90) is the iconic order — succulent marinated chicken, crispy chilli peppers, peanuts, generous vegetables, and enough heat to wake you up for your afternoon lecture. Portions are enormous for the price, the turnover is fast (meaning everything is fresh), and the flavour-to-dollar ratio is genuinely hard to beat anywhere in Melbourne.

**Order this:** Kung Pao chicken with rice ($12.90)
**Also try:** Laksa ($14.50)
**Address:** 121 Grattan Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Mon-Fri 11:00am-9:00pm, Sat-Sun 12:00pm-9:00pm
**Price range:** $ (most dishes $11-16)
**Insider tip:** Arrive before 12:30pm to beat the Melbourne Uni lunch rush. After that, expect a queue.

## 4. Hakata Gensuke Carlton

**The vibe:** Gold-standard Hakata tonkotsu ramen without the CBD queues.

If you've ever stood in a 45-minute line at Hakata Gensuke's Russell Street original, you already know the ramen is worth it. The Carlton outpost gives you the same richly cloudy tonkotsu broth — simmered for 15+ hours with pork bones until it's thick, creamy and deeply savoury — without the tourist-zone madness. A standard bowl of their signature Original Tonkotsu runs under $20 and is one of the most satisfying single-bowl meals you'll find anywhere in Melbourne's inner north.

**Order this:** Original Tonkotsu ramen ($17.80)
**Also try:** Black Tonkotsu with garlic oil ($18.80)
**Address:** 211 Lygon Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Mon-Sun 11:30am-9:30pm
**Price range:** $$ (ramen $16-20, sides $5-9)
**Insider tip:** Add a half-serve of extra noodles for $2 — the broth-to-noodle ratio rewards it.

## 5. DOC Espresso

**The vibe:** Neapolitan pizza done simply and brilliantly in a buzzy Lygon Street spot that's been a Carlton staple for years.

DOC doesn't need a gimmick. The pizza dough is made daily, stretched by hand, topped with best-in-class ingredients and fired in a woodfired oven until the crust blisters and chars in all the right places. A Margherita — tomato, fior di latte, basil, olive oil — comes in at $18 and is genuinely one of the best pizzas in Melbourne. The secret is restraint: DOC doesn't overload toppings, letting each ingredient sing. It gets packed on weekends, but that's Carlton for you.

**Order this:** Margherita ($18)
**Also try:** Focaccia di Recco with stracchino ($16)
**Address:** 295 Drummond Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Mon-Sun 12:00pm-10:00pm
**Price range:** $$ (pizzas $18-28)
**Insider tip:** Sit at the bar counter if you're solo — you'll get served faster and the pizza theatre is right in front of you.

## 6. Taquito

**The vibe:** Hand-pressed tortillas, house-grown chillies, and authentic Mexican tacos in a colourful Lygon Street cantina.

Taquito is the real thing. They make their own tortillas in-house using traditional techniques, grow their own chillies and herbs, and serve tacos that would hold up in Mexico City. At around $6-8 per taco, you can build a feast of three different fillings — al pastor, carnitas, and a veggie option — for under $20, with change left over for a house-made agua fresca. The mezcal bottle shop attached to the restaurant is a bonus for anyone who wants to continue the evening.

**Order this:** Al pastor taco x3 ($18-24 for 3)
**Also try:** House-made agua fresca ($6)
**Address:** 182 Lygon Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Tue-Sun 5:30pm-10:00pm
**Price range:** $ (tacos $6-8 each)
**Insider tip:** Wednesday is mezcal night — cheaper pours and the vibe gets loose.

## 7. Florian To Go

**The vibe:** The beloved Florian team's new takeaway window in Rathdowne Village — grab-and-go sandwiches, pastries and coffee from one of Carlton's best kitchens.

When the Florian team opened a takeaway shopfront right next door to the original cafe in February 2026, the neighbourhood breathed a collective sigh of relief. Now you can grab a $16 porchetta roll on Iris the Bakery bread or a hot sopressa sandwich on Austro Bakery focaccia without waiting for a table at the always-packed sit-down restaurant. It's premium ingredients at unpremium prices, and the sandwich game is strong enough to rival Prahran's Fannys Sannys.

**Order this:** Crispy porchetta roll with gem lettuce, dijonnaise and salsa verde ($16)
**Also try:** Hot sopressa on focaccia ($16)
**Address:** Rathdowne Street, Carlton North VIC 3054
**Hours:** Mon-Fri 7:00am-3:00pm, Sat-Sun 8:00am-3:00pm
**Price range:** $ (sandwiches $16, coffee $4-6)
**Insider tip:** Arrive before 11am on weekdays — the porchetta roll sells out by lunch most days.

## 8. Panda Hot Pot

**The vibe:** DIY hot pot under a 1.5-tonne steel dragon with 80+ ingredients to choose from. Chaos in the best possible way.

Australia's first outpost of the international Panda Hot Pot chain landed in Carlton with all the subtlety of, well, a 1.5-tonne steel dragon mounted on the ceiling. The lunch special is where the value lives — a set hot pot base with a selection of meats, vegetables and noodles for under $20 per person. Choose your broth (the Sichuan mala is face-meltingly good), pick your ingredients from a menu of 80+ options, and let the bubbling pot on your table do the rest. It's communal, it's messy, it's perfect for groups.

**Order this:** Lunch special with Sichuan mala base ($18)
**Also try:** Wagyu beef slices ($5 add-on)
**Address:** Swanston Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Mon-Sun 11:30am-10:30pm
**Price range:** $-$$ (lunch specials $16-20, dinner $25-40)
**Insider tip:** The lunch specials are genuinely half the price of dinner. Plan accordingly.

## 9. Leonardo's Pizza Palace

**The vibe:** Low-lit, late-night pizza diner where you'll accidentally stay three hours and leave dustier than planned.

Leonardo's is not a place for restraint. The room is dark, the music is loud, the cocktails are strong, and the pizza arrives in thick, chewy slices that soak up whatever else you're drinking. The pepperoni is a classic — crispy-edged cups of pork, pools of orange oil, proper mozzarella — and the jalapeno number with fermented pepper and bechamel is the kind of spicy wake-up call you need at 11pm. Individual pizzas start around $18, and the late-night energy is free.

**Order this:** Pepperoni pizza ($19)
**Also try:** Jalapeno with fermented pepper and bechamel ($21)
**Address:** 36 Grattan Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Tue-Sun 5:00pm-late
**Price range:** $$ (pizzas $18-25, cocktails $18-22)
**Insider tip:** Come after 10pm on a Friday for the full Leonardo's experience — the DJ starts, the lights drop, and the pizzas keep coming.

## 10. Lagoon Dining

**The vibe:** Sit at the black granite bar and watch Cantonese masters work through charcoal-roasted char siu, XO rice noodles and shredded potato that'll change your life.

Lagoon Dining is technically a proper restaurant, but the lunch menu pricing puts several dishes firmly in cheap-eats territory. The hot and sour shredded potato ($14) is a textural masterpiece — thin julienned strips, crunchy and slick with vinegar heat. The cured pork belly with rolled rice noodles in XO sauce ($16) is rich, fragrant and absurdly generous. Come at lunch, order two dishes, and you'll walk out wondering how you spent less than $20 on food this good.

**Order this:** Hot and sour shredded potato ($14)
**Also try:** Cured pork belly with XO rice noodle rolls ($16)
**Address:** 17 Lygon Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Mon-Sun 12:00pm-3:00pm, 5:30pm-10:00pm
**Price range:** $-$$ (lunch dishes $12-18, dinner mains $22-38)
**Insider tip:** The lunch menu is where the value hides. Dinner prices jump significantly — plan your visit for midday.

## 11. Tiamo

**The vibe:** The oldest Italian restaurant on Lygon Street, serving enormous pasta portions at prices that haven't caught up with inflation.

Tiamo has been feeding Carlton since the 1980s, and the formula hasn't changed: big portions of honest Italian food at prices that seem locked in a time warp. A plate of spaghetti bolognese comes in under $17, the portions are genuinely massive, and the BYO policy means you can bring a $10 bottle of red from the bottle shop next door and have a proper Italian dinner for under $30 total. The decor is charmingly stuck in 1992 — wooden booths, chalkboard menus, Italian football scarves on the walls — and that's exactly the point.

**Order this:** Spaghetti bolognese ($16.50)
**Also try:** Veal parmigiana ($19.50)
**Address:** 303 Lygon Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Mon-Sun 7:00am-11:00pm
**Price range:** $ (pasta $14-18, mains $17-22)
**Insider tip:** It's BYO with no corkage fee. Bring your own wine and this becomes one of the cheapest proper Italian dinners in Melbourne.

## 12. The Green Man's Arms

**The vibe:** A heritage pub that's secretly one of Melbourne's best plant-based restaurants, with an eggplant schnitzel that converts carnivores.

Don't let the classic pub facade fool you. The Green Man's Arms runs an entirely plant-based menu that's so good you forget there's no meat. The eggplant "schnitzel" ($16) is the signature — crumbed, fried until golden, served with the same sides you'd expect from a traditional pub parma. The fried cauliflower is another standout. Pair it with one of the diverse craft beers on tap and you've got a genuinely excellent pub meal for under $20 that happens to be completely vegan.

**Order this:** Eggplant schnitzel ($16)
**Also try:** Fried cauliflower with house sauce ($14)
**Address:** 164 Lygon Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Mon-Sun 12:00pm-10:00pm
**Price range:** $ (mains $14-20, beers $9-14)
**Insider tip:** The beer list is excellent and changes regularly. Ask the bar staff for a recommendation — they actually know what they're talking about.

## 13. Capitano

**The vibe:** Deep-dish pizza squares and creative Italian-American fusion from the Bar Liberty crew, in a fun room that doesn't take itself too seriously.

Capitano brings something different to Carlton's Italian dining scene: deep-dish pizza "squares" and fermented-base "rounds" that lean heavily on North American influences while keeping an Italian soul. The squares are thick, cheesy, satisfying slabs of pizza that justify every calorie. A square starts around $16, and one square plus a house wine ($12) puts you comfortably under $30 for a very solid evening. The wine list is almost entirely Italian and priced fairly for the neighbourhood.

**Order this:** Pepperoni deep-dish square ($18)
**Also try:** Fermented-base round with seasonal toppings ($19)
**Address:** 421 Rathdowne Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Wed-Sun 5:30pm-10:00pm
**Price range:** $$ (pizza $16-24, wine from $12/glass)
**Insider tip:** The squares are the move here. The fermented bases are more adventurous, but the squares are what you'll crave at 2am.

## 14. Shanghai Village

**The vibe:** No-frills BYO Chinese with the kind of xiao long bao that make people drive across Melbourne.

Shanghai Village has been quietly producing some of Carlton's best Chinese food for decades. The xiao long bao (soup dumplings) are the reason people come — thin, delicate wrappers holding a burst of hot, savoury broth and pork filling. An order of 10 comes in around $14, and paired with a plate of pan-fried pork buns ($12), you've got a feast for under $20. It's BYO (no corkage on wine), the service is fast and efficient, and the food is consistently excellent. No pretension, just proper Shanghai cooking.

**Order this:** Xiao long bao (10 pieces, $14)
**Also try:** Pan-fried pork buns ($12)
**Address:** 112 Little Bourke Street (and a Carlton outpost on Lygon Street)
**Hours:** Mon-Sun 11:30am-10:00pm
**Price range:** $ (most dishes $12-18)
**Insider tip:** Go BYO. Bring a bottle of riesling — the acidity cuts through the rich dumpling fillings perfectly.

## 15. Abla's

**The vibe:** A Melbourne institution serving generous Lebanese banquets that have comforted generations. Come hungry, leave impossibly full.

Abla's is a Carlton icon — a family-run Lebanese restaurant that's been operating since the 1970s and has introduced more Melburnians to kibbeh, tabouli and fattoush than any other venue in the city. While the banquet options push above $20 per person, the individual mezze plates and wraps sit firmly in budget territory. A falafel plate ($15), a serve of kibbeh ($14), or a mixed plate of dips with bread ($12) will fill you up without breaking the bank. The warmth of the service is worth the visit alone.

**Order this:** Falafel plate ($15)
**Also try:** Kibbeh with yoghurt ($14)
**Address:** 109 Elgin Street, Carlton VIC 3053
**Hours:** Tue-Sat 12:00pm-3:00pm, 6:00pm-10:00pm
**Price range:** $-$$ (mezze $10-16, banquets $35-55pp)
**Insider tip:** If you're in a group, the banquet is actually exceptional value — but for solo cheap eats, stick to the mezze plates.

## The Verdict

Carlton's cheap eats scene is genuinely one of Melbourne's best. The combination of a massive student population, Lygon Street competition, and a constant stream of new openings (Aunty's Dumplings, Garfield Pizzeria, and Florian To Go all landed in 2026 alone) means the quality-to-price ratio stays razor sharp.

The move: start with dumplings at Aunty's, detour to Rice Bar for a $13 Malaysian bowl, grab a slice at DOC, and finish with Pidapipo soft serve at Garfield. Total damage: under $40 for four meals across four cuisines. Try doing that in South Yarra.
