# Collingwood, Melbourne
![Cover Image](https://images.pexels.com/photos/pexels-image.jpg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1260&h=750)

## Overview
Collingwood is a dynamic inner-city suburb in Melbourne, known for its industrial heritage, cutting-edge creative scene, and high-end lifestyle. With a postcode of 3066, it's a hub for foodies, hipsters, and creative travelers seeking an authentic urban experience.

## Insights
- **Vibe Score**: 9/10 for food, 9/10 for nightlife, 5/10 for affordability, 9/10 for transport, and 10/10 for culture.
- **Best For**: Hipsters, foodies, and creative travelers looking for an authentic, high-energy urban experience.

## Property
### Rent Prices
- 1-bedroom median: $520/week
- 2-bedroom median: $710/week
- 3-bedroom median: $950/week
- Unit median: $620/week
- House median: $1050/week
- **Trend**: Up 6.5% year-over-year.

### Cost of Living
- Meal (cheap): $22
- Meal (mid-range): $45
- Coffee: $5.50
- Transport pass: $165
- Gym membership: $75

## Transport
### Train
- Station: Collingwood Station
- Lines: Hurstbridge Line, Mernda Line
- CBD travel time: 10 minutes
- Frequency: Every 10-15 minutes

### Tram
- Routes: Route 86, Route 11
- Key stops: Smith St/Gertrude St, Collingwood Town Hall

### Bus
- Routes: Route 246, Route 402
- **Note**: Provides orbital connectivity to surrounding suburbs like Fitzroy and North Melbourne.

### Parking
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- Street rate: $4.50/hr
- Car parks: Ace Parking, Wilson Parking

### Cycling
- **Bike lanes**: Yes
- **CBD route**: Direct access via Wellington Street or Gipps Street bike lanes.
