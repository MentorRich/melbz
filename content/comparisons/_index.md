Choosing where to live in Melbourne often comes down to two or three suburbs that tick most of your boxes. This section puts those suburbs head-to-head so you can stop scrolling realestate.com.au at 1 a.m. and actually make a decision.

## How We Compare

Every comparison follows the same format. We look at median one-bedroom rent, commute time to the CBD, the local food scene, nightlife, family-friendliness, and overall value for money. Each article includes a quick-reference comparison table and ends with a clear verdict — we pick winners for students, young professionals, families, nightlife seekers, and budget-conscious renters.
