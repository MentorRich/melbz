## The Docklands Property Market in 2026

Docklands is almost entirely an apartment market. There are no houses, no townhouses, and no land sales. The median apartment price sits around $550K–$650K, which is below comparable CBD and [Southbank](/southbank/) apartments. That price gap reflects the suburb's still-developing reputation, the high supply of apartments, and the mixed track record of off-the-plan purchases.

## What You Can Buy

**One-bedroom apartments** — The entry point. $380K–$500K depending on views, floor level, and building quality. Harbour-view one-bedrooms command a $50K–$80K premium over city-facing or internal units.

**Two-bedroom apartments** — The most common purchase. $550K–$800K. This is where harbour views, building quality, and body corporate fees create the widest price range. A two-bedroom in a well-managed tower at NewQuay with water views is a fundamentally different product to a two-bedroom in an older tower with internal views and a troubled body corp.

**Three-bedroom apartments and penthouses** — The premium end. $800K–$1.5M+. These attract downsizers and professionals who've committed to waterfront living.

## The Body Corporate Question

Body corporate fees in Docklands range from $4,000 to $8,000+ per year. Buildings with pools, gyms, concierge, and extensive common areas charge the most. Some older towers have deferred maintenance that's about to land as special levies.

**Before you buy:** Request the body corporate financial statements, the maintenance plan, and the minutes from recent meetings. Underfunded sinking funds and upcoming special levies can cost tens of thousands. This is the single most important due diligence step in Docklands.

## The Off-the-Plan Legacy

Many Docklands apartments were sold off-the-plan in the 2000s and 2010s at prices that haven't been recovered. Some early investors are still underwater. For current buyers, this creates opportunity — you can buy at prices below what the original owners paid, in buildings that now have established communities and proven maintenance records.

The lesson: don't buy off-the-plan in Docklands. Buy established, inspect the building, and check the body corporate.

## The Verdict

Docklands offers genuine value for harbour-view apartment living in a CBD-adjacent location. The risks are specific and manageable: body corporate fees, building quality variation, and the suburb's ongoing development. Do your due diligence, inspect thoroughly, and buy in a well-managed building.
