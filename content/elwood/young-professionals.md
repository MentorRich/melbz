## The Weekend Factor

Weekends in Elwood are chill. Brunch is possible but not a competitive sport. You'll find your routine — a preferred cafe, a go-to park, and the occasional exploration of nearby strips.

## The Commute

From Elwood to the CBD: 20-35 minutes by public transport. Trains run every 10-15 minutes during peak. Reliable but not the fastest commute. For most young professionals, the commute from Elwood is manageable.

## Is Elwood Worth It for Young Professionals?

Elwood works for young professionals who prioritise lifestyle balance over a buzzing social scene. It's not the flashiest choice, but it's smart.
