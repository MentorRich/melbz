## The Weekend Factor

Weekends in Flemington are chill. Brunch is possible but not a competitive sport. You'll find your routine — a preferred cafe, a go-to park, and the occasional exploration of nearby strips.

## The Commute

From Flemington to the CBD: 20-35 minutes by public transport. Trains run every 10-15 minutes during peak. Reliable but not the fastest commute. For most young professionals, the commute from Flemington is manageable.

## Is Flemington Worth It for Young Professionals?

Flemington suits young professionals who are over the inner-city premium and want space, quiet, and savings. The social trade-off is real — make sure your social life doesn't depend on having bars within walking distance.
