*Updated 16 March 2026 | Yuki Tanaka reporting*

Footscray might be Melbourne's most underrated food destination. Just 9km west of the CBD, this multicultural suburb boasts one of the city's most diverse and delicious Asian food scenes, with Vietnamese, Ethiopian, Chinese, and Thai restaurants competing for your tastebuds and your dollars.

Unlike more polished dining precincts, Footscray's food scene is raw, authentic, and refreshingly unpretentious. You'll find no Instagram-fluencers here, just locals filling up on incredible food at prices that haven't been inflated by hype.

## The Vietnamese Scene

Footscray's Vietnamese presence is significant, though less concentrated than Richmond's Victoria Street. The restaurants here tend to be more diverse, with Vietnamese, Laotian, and Cambodian influences all present.

**Pho:** The pho in Footscray is authentic and affordable. A large bowl will set you back $12-14, and the broth is rich and aromatic. Several shops along Hopkins Street and Leeds Street serve excellent pho that rivals anything in Richmond.

**Banh Mi:** Footscray's banh mi shops are a bargain at $6-8 for a filled baguette. The Vietnamese bakeries here import their bread fresh daily, ensuring a crisp exterior and pillowy interior.

## The Ethiopian Option

Footscray is one of Melbourne's few suburbs with a significant Ethiopian presence. The injera, doro wat, and vegetarian platters are authentic and reasonably priced.

## The Chinese and Thai Presence

Chinese and Thai restaurants round out the Asian offerings, with several excellent spots for dim sum, noodles, and curry.

## The Rankings

1. Vietnamese restaurants on Hopkins Street
2. Ethiopian spots on Leeds Street
3. Chinese and Thai along Footscray Road

## The Verdict

Footscray's Asian food scene is diverse, affordable, and authentic. It's not the most polished dining destination, but it's one of the most rewarding for food lovers who know where to look.
