## The After-Work Scene

Footscray has enough to keep you out on a weeknight. A few solid bars, a pub or two worth knowing, and the occasional late-night spot. For bigger nights, you're an Uber from the CBD.

## The Weekend Factor

Weekend brunch is a religion in Footscray. Saturday mornings mean queues at the top cafes, followed by market browsing, park sessions, or catching up on sleep. Sundays are slower but the coffee is still exceptional.

## The Commute

From Footscray to the CBD: 10-20 minutes by train or tram. Services run every 5-10 minutes during peak. This is one of the best-connected suburbs in Melbourne. For most young professionals, the commute from Footscray is a non-issue.

## Is Footscray Worth It for Young Professionals?

Footscray works for young professionals who prioritise food and walkability over a buzzing social scene. It's not the flashiest choice, but it's smart.
