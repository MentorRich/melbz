## Why Fuel Prices Are So High Right Now

The Iran situation is the main driver. Here's the chain:

1. **Iran conflict escalated in February 2026** — shipping lanes in the Strait of Hormuz disrupted
2. **Global oil supply dropped ~4%** — enough to spike Brent crude above $100/barrel
3. **Australian fuel follows global crude** — with a 2-3 week lag
4. **The AUD hasn't helped** — sitting around $0.63 USD means we're paying more per barrel

This isn't a temporary blip. Analysts expect elevated prices through at least Q2 2026.

## The Melbourne Fuel Cycle — How to Time Your Fill

Melbourne runs on a roughly **7-10 day fuel price cycle**:

- **Cheapest days:** Tuesday and Wednesday (bottom of the cycle)
- **Most expensive:** Friday and Saturday (peak of the cycle)
- **Difference:** Up to 20-30c per litre between peak and trough

On a 50L tank, timing your fill correctly saves **$10-$15 every single time**.

## 7 Ways to Save on Fuel in Melbourne

### 1. Use a Fuel Price App
**FuelMap Australia** and **PetrolSpy** show real-time prices at every station. Takes 30 seconds to check before you fill up.

### 2. Fill Up on Tuesday or Wednesday
The cycle is predictable. Don't fight it. Fill mid-week, every week.

### 3. Costco Docklands
If you have a Costco membership ($65/year), their Docklands bowser is consistently **10-15c cheaper** than surrounding stations. On a 50L fill, that's $5-7.50 saved. The membership pays for itself in ~10 fills.

### 4. Head West
Footscray, Sunshine, and the western corridor consistently have Melbourne's cheapest fuel. If you're passing through, fill up there.

### 5. Lock in With 7-Eleven Fuel Lock
The 7-Eleven app lets you lock in a price when it's low and redeem later. No catch, it actually works.

### 6. Supermarket Shopper Dockets
Woolworths and Coles 4c/L discount dockets stack up. On a $2.18 base price, every cent matters.

### 7. Consider Your Commute
If you're choosing between suburbs and you drive daily, the fuel price difference between Footscray ($2.04) and Toorak ($2.29) is **$12.50 per tank**. Over a year of weekly fills, that's **$650**.

## What About Electric?

If you're seriously looking at reducing fuel costs, Melbourne's EV charging network has expanded significantly in 2026. The economics work out if you're spending $80+/week on petrol. But that's a different article.

## The Bottom Line

Fuel prices suck right now. The Iran conflict isn't resolving anytime soon, and Melbourne drivers are feeling it. Your best moves:

- **Fill up mid-week** (saves $10-$15 per fill)
- **Use a price app** (saves $5-$10 per fill)
- **Know your suburb** (western suburbs consistently cheapest)
- **Don't panic buy** — stations re-stock regularly, hoarding doesn't help
