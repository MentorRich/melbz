# Best Cafes in Hawthorn Melbourne 2026

Hawthorn takes its coffee seriously — this is Melbourne after all. The cafe culture here reflects the neighbourhood: polished without being pretentious, driven by quality rather than hype. Glenferrie Road and Burwood Road carry the bulk of the options, with a few gems on Auburn Road worth the detour.

---

**1. Axil Coffee Roasters — 322 Burwood Road, Hawthorn VIC 3122**

Axil is one of Melbourne's most respected specialty coffee roasters, and the Hawthorn outpost on Burwood Road is where many locals start their day. The single-origin filter coffees rotate regularly and the baristas know their stuff — ask for a recommendation and you'll get a genuine answer, not a sales pitch. The flat white ($5.00) is consistently excellent. The food menu holds its own too: the breakfast bowl with poached eggs, avocado, and dukkah is a standout. Expect a queue on Saturday mornings, but it moves fast. The Burwood Road location is an easy walk from Hawthorn station.

---

**2. Porgie + Mr Jones — 291 Auburn Road, Hawthorn VIC 3122**

The brunch institution on Auburn Road. Porgie + Mr Jones has been doing reliable, well-executed brunch since before brunch became a competitive sport in Melbourne. The menu doesn't chase trends — it nails the classics. Eggs Benedict with properly made hollandaise, a big breakfast that's actually big, and pastries from their own kitchen. Coffee is strong and consistent. The space is light-filled with indoor and outdoor seating. Weekday mornings are the sweet spot — you'll get a table immediately and the service is unhurried. Weekends draw crowds from across the inner east.

---

**3. Short Straw — 743 Glenferrie Road, Hawthorn VIC 3122**

A compact cafe on the Glenferrie Road strip near Swinburne University that's become a favourite with students and locals alike. The coffee is above average — they use a Melbourne-roasted blend that produces a clean, balanced espresso. The food is simple but well-made: toasties, banana bread, and a rotating lunch special. What makes Short Straw work is the atmosphere — it's small enough to feel personal, the staff remember your order after a few visits, and the prices haven't gone ridiculous despite the Glenferrie Road address. Good for a quick flat white or a longer sit with a laptop.

---

**4. Rustica Canteen — Glenferrie Road, Hawthorn VIC 3122**

The pastry game at Rustica is the draw. Croissants that shatter when you bite them, sourdough that's properly fermented, and a cabinet of baked goods that makes the walk past impossible. The coffee is solid — not quite specialty-level but well-made and consistent. It's the kind of place you pop into for a takeaway pastry and coffee on your way to Glenferrie station, or sit in for a quiet weekday breakfast. The Glenferrie Road foot traffic means it stays busy, but turnover is fast.

---

**5. Lux Foundry — 29 Church Street, Hawthorn VIC 3122**

A slightly different vibe from the Glenferrie Road strip. Lux Foundry on Church Street occupies a converted warehouse space with exposed brick, high ceilings, and the kind of natural light that makes every photo look better than reality. The menu is broader than most cafes — proper lunch options alongside the morning coffee and brunch staples. The coffee is good, the portions are generous, and the space works equally well for a solo work session, a catch-up with friends, or a family weekend brunch. The Church Street location means it's quieter than the main strip cafes.

---

## FAQ

**What's the best coffee in Hawthorn?**
Axil Coffee Roasters on Burwood Road is the specialist pick. For everyday reliability, Short Straw and Porgie + Mr Jones both deliver consistently.

**Which Hawthorn cafe is best for working from?**
Lux Foundry has the space and WiFi. Short Straw works for shorter sessions. Avoid the Glenferrie Road cafes on weekday lunchtimes when the Swinburne student rush hits.

**Are Hawthorn cafes family-friendly?**
Most are family-tolerant rather than explicitly family-friendly. Porgie + Mr Jones handles families well with outdoor seating and high chairs. Lux Foundry's larger space means a pram won't be in anyone's way.

## The Verdict

Hawthorn's cafe scene is refined and reliable. You won't find the experimental edge of Collingwood or the Instagram-bait of South Yarra, but what you get is consistently good coffee within a short walk from anywhere in the suburb. Axil brings the specialty standard, Porgie + Mr Jones owns the brunch game, Short Straw delivers value on Glenferrie Road, and Lux Foundry gives you space on Church Street. Five cafes, five different moods — and not a bad flat white among them.
