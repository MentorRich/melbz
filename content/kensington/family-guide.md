Thinking about raising kids in Kensington? Here is what parents actually need to know. At 4km from the CBD on the Craigieburn and Sunbury lines, Kensington offers families an inner-city lifestyle with village-scale community. You trade backyard size for proximity and walkability.

**Family Friendliness Grade: B+**

## Schools

**Kensington Primary School** on McCracken Street is the main local primary and is well-regarded by families in the area. Enrolment demand is strong — check your zone before assuming you have a place. **St Michael's Primary** (Catholic) on Macaulay Road provides an additional option.

For secondary, families typically look at schools in Flemington, North Melbourne, or the CBD — all accessible within 10 to 15 minutes by train. Selective-entry schools in the CBD are particularly convenient given Kensington's fast train connection.

## Parks & Green Spaces

**JJ Holland Park** on Bellair Street is the main family park. It has a playground with equipment for different age groups, open grass, walking paths, and a community garden. The park is well-maintained by Moonee Valley Council and is the social hub for Kensington families on weekends.

**Kensington Recreation Reserve** on Altona Street has sporting ovals and additional open space. The reserve hosts weekend sport and community events.

**Macaulay Road pocket parks** and smaller reserves throughout the residential streets provide quick after-school play options.

The suburb's compact size means you are never more than a 10-minute walk from a park.

## Safety for Families

Kensington feels safe for families. Bellair Street is well-lit and populated throughout the day and evening. The residential streets between Bellair Street and Macaulay Road are quiet and well-maintained. The community is a mix of young professionals and families, which keeps the streets active during daylight hours.

Standard Melbourne precautions apply at night, particularly walking near the industrial areas at the suburb's edges.

## Kid-Friendly Eating

Bellair Street [cafes](/kensington/best-cafes/) are genuinely family-friendly. High chairs, kids' portions, and outdoor seating with enough space for a pram. The general atmosphere is relaxed about children — this is a community that sees families daily. Several cafes have become known as family meeting points on weekends.

## Weekend Activities

Weekends in Kensington are well-served. **JJ Holland Park** is the Saturday morning destination for families. The playground fills up, the cafes on Bellair Street do a brisk brunch trade, and the community garden attracts curious kids.

**Local markets** at the Kensington Town Hall and surrounding venues run periodically. The proximity to the **Queen Victoria Market** (accessible by Flemington Bridge tram stop or a short cycle) gives families a major weekend destination within 10 minutes.

**Kensington Library** runs storytime sessions and school holiday programs. Local sporting clubs at the recreation reserve have junior programs.

## The Commute Factor

Kensington station is on both the Craigieburn and Sunbury lines, with trains to the CBD taking about 8 to 12 minutes. That is a genuine advantage for working parents — one of the fastest commutes in suburban Melbourne. Flemington Bridge tram stop on Route 57 provides an alternative.

See our [Kensington Transport Guide](/kensington/transport-guide/) for the full breakdown.

## The Family Verdict

Kensington works well for families who prioritise inner-city access and village community over backyard space. JJ Holland Park, Bellair Street cafes, and an 8 to 12 minute train to the CBD create a practical family setup. You will share the suburb with a younger crowd, but that keeps the cafes good and the streets active. With one-bedroom rents around $400 per week, it is a premium choice — but families who make it work tend to love it.

## FAQ

**Is Kensington good for families?**
Yes, particularly for families who prioritise proximity to the CBD and a walkable village lifestyle. JJ Holland Park, Kensington Primary School, and the Bellair Street strip provide the essentials.

**What schools are in Kensington?**
Kensington Primary School on McCracken Street is the main public primary. St Michael's Primary (Catholic) on Macaulay Road is also local. Secondary students typically travel to schools in Flemington, North Melbourne, or the CBD.

**Are there playgrounds in Kensington?**
JJ Holland Park on Bellair Street has the main playground. Kensington Recreation Reserve and smaller pocket parks also have play equipment.

**How safe is Kensington for families?**
Kensington is considered safe for families. Bellair Street is well-lit and active, residential streets are quiet, and the compact size means you are always close to other people and activity.
