Melbourne CBD after dark is a different city. The suits leave, the laneway bars light up, and the city remembers why it is Australia's nightlife capital.

## The Bar Scene

### Rooftop Bars

**Rooftop Bar** - *Curtin House, Swanston Street* - The original Melbourne rooftop experience. Astroturf, city views, and a crowd that ranges from after-work drinks to late-night chaos. Open-air cinema in summer.

**Siglo** - *Spring Street* - Cigar bar overlooking Parliament House. This is where you go when you want to feel like you have made it. Expensive, sophisticated, genuinely impressive.

### Hidden Bars

**Bar Americano** - *Presgrave Place* - Six seats. Standing room for maybe 10. The cocktails are some of the best in Australia.

**Eau de Vie** - *Malthouse Lane* - Speakeasy vibes done right. Dark, intimate, and the cocktail menu reads like a novel.

**Beneath Driver Lane** - *Driver Lane* - Underground wine bar with a curated list. Moody lighting, excellent cheese boards.

### Pub Revival

**The Royal Melbourne Hotel** - *Bourke Street* - Classic pub with a thoughtful refresh. Good beer list, decent pub food, and a crowd that actually wants to talk to strangers.

## Live Music

**Cherry Bar** - *AC/DC Lane* - Rock and roll institution. Small, loud, sweaty, perfect.

**The Toff in Town** - *Curtin House* - Eclectic programming from jazz to electronic to comedy. The train carriage interior is still one of Melbourne's best bar designs.

## Late Night Eats

- **Stalactites** (Lonsdale Street): Gyros at 3am. A Melbourne rite of passage.
- **Chinatown** (Little Bourke Street): The whole strip comes alive after midnight.
- **HuTong Dumpling Bar**: Late-night dumplings worth the midnight queue.

## What Night to Go Out

- **Thursday**: The new Friday. Best bar atmosphere with fewer crowds.
- **Friday**: Peak everything. Book ahead or accept chaos.
- **Saturday**: More tourists, but the late-night scene is strongest.
- **Sunday**: Session Sundays at certain venues. Surprisingly good.

## Getting Home

Night Rider buses run Friday and Saturday nights. Uber surge pricing peaks around 2am. Plan ahead or embrace the tram network.
