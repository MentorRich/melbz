## The Weekend Factor

Weekend brunch is a religion in Melbourne CBD. Saturday mornings mean queues at the top cafes, followed by market browsing, park sessions, or catching up on sleep. Sundays are slower but the coffee is still exceptional.

## The Commute

From Melbourne CBD to the CBD: 10-20 minutes by train or tram. Services run every 5-10 minutes during peak. This is one of the best-connected suburbs in Melbourne. For most young professionals, the commute from Melbourne CBD is a non-issue.

## Is Melbourne CBD Worth It for Young Professionals?

Yes. Melbourne CBD is one of Melbourne's best suburbs for young professionals. The nightlife, food, and commute all work. The cost is the only friction point — and if you can handle it, you'll love it here.
