## How to Keep Costs Down in Melbourne

1. **Choose your suburb strategically** — 10 minutes further from the CBD can save significant rent
2. **Share housing** — splitting a 2+ bedroom place is the most effective cost reducer
3. **Use PT** — saves on car costs, parking, and the stress of driving
4. **Cook at home** — Melbourne's food scene is great, but eating out daily adds up fast
5. **Find your local spots** — the best value food and coffee is often off the main strip
