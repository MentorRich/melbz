Here's the thing about dating in Northcote: it doesn't try too hard. And that's exactly why it works. While other suburbs are busy constructing elaborate "experience dining" concepts with dry ice and edible napkins, Northcote just quietly offers some of the most genuinely romantic — and honestly fun — date night options in Melbourne.

Whether you're on a first date trying to gauge if this person is worth a second, or you're five years deep and trying to remember what it felt like to actually leave the house together, Northcote has you sorted.

## 1. The First Date: Keep It Low-Key at Oh Loretta!

**Budget:** $40-$70 for two (wine + share plates)

Oh Loretta! nails the first date formula. Plump couches, fairy-lit courtyard, a wine list that naturally sparks conversation. The veg-led menu is share-friendly, which means you're not awkwardly deciding between a $28 pasta and the $32 fish. Order a bottle of something orange and a few plates. Let the conversation do the rest.

The courtyard out back is genuinely one of the most relaxed date spots in Melbourne. No pressure, no pretension, just good wine and a vibe that says "I'm interesting but I'm not trying too hard."

**Where:** 282 High Street, Northcote
**Tip:** Book ahead for a courtyard table on Friday or Saturday. Walk-ins at the bar are fine midweek.

## 2. The "I'm Trying to Impress" Date: The Estelle

**Budget:** $80-$120 for two (a la carte) or $95pp for the set menu

Chef Scott Pickett's Northcote bistro. The set menu is the smart play — you both eat the same thing, which eliminates the awkward "you order first" dance. The wine list is deep. The food is beautifully plated, technically excellent, and the kind of thing you'll both still be talking about over breakfast.

**Where:** 245-247 High Street, Northcote
**Tip:** The $95pp set menu is the best value fine-ish dining in the inner north. Book at least a week ahead for weekends.

## 3. The "We've Been Together Forever" Date: Mesob Ethiopian + Northcote Social Club

**Budget:** $50-$80 for two

Start with dinner at Mesob Ethiopian — a shared platter of doro wat, misir wat, and injera that forces you to eat with your hands. Sharing food from the same plate while tearing bread together is oddly intimate. Then walk five minutes down High Street to Northcote Social Club. Grab a table on the deck, order a couple of pots, and let the Friday night buzz wash over you.

**Where:** Mesob — 303 High Street / NSC — 301 High Street
**Tip:** Mesob does live jazz on weekends. Time your dinner for a Friday and you'll get dinner + live music + pub drinks for under $100 total.

## 4. The "Movie and Dinner" Combo: Palace Westgarth + Ruckers Hill

**Budget:** $60-$90 for two

The classic Northcote date night. Palace Westgarth is a 100+ year-old art deco cinema with five screens, a licensed bar, and a courtyard where you can have a pre-movie drink. The programming leans interesting — foreign films, arthouse, retrospectives alongside big releases.

Walk three minutes downhill to Ruckers Hill for dinner. An ex-Bistro Thierry chef runs the kitchen, serving French-leaning bistro fare at prices that would be half of what you'd pay in South Yarra.

**The maths:** Two cinema tickets ($36-$44) + dinner for two ($40-$60) + pre-movie drink ($16-$20) = $92-$124 for a full evening. Tuesday cinema brings it under $80.

## 5. The "We're Foodies" Date: Va Penne + High Note Bar

**Budget:** $70-$100 for two

Start at Va Penne for Sicilian-style dinner — home-style pastas, a bread basket that'll test your willpower, cannoli that'll make you briefly consider moving to Palermo. Then walk eight minutes to High Note — the wine bar inside a heritage-listed theatre. Lo-fi wines, DJs spinning vinyl, and a sound system that once lived in the Sydney Opera House.

**Where:** Va Penne — 371 High Street / High Note — 238 High Street
**Tip:** High Note can be hard to find — look for the heritage theatre entrance and follow the stairs down.

## 6. The Budget Date: 300 Grams + Samuel Pepys

**Budget:** $25-$40 for two

Not every date needs a three-course production. Grab two Cocowhip sundaes ($8 each) from 300 Grams, walk to Samuel Pepys (boutique bottle shop and wine bar opposite Palace Westgarth), pick up a glass of something sparkling, and sit out front watching the world go by through the cinema windows.

**Where:** 300 Grams — 304A High Street / Samuel Pepys — opposite Palace Westgarth

## Getting Home Safe

- **86 tram** runs until about 1:30am on weekends — runs the full length of High Street
- **Northcote and Merri stations** (Hurstbridge/Mernda line) — last trains around midnight on weekends
- **Rideshare:** High Street / Station Street intersection is the best pickup spot
- **Walking:** High Street is busy and well-lit on Friday/Saturday. Separation Street and Clarke Street are safe cross-streets between venues.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's the cheapest date night in Northcote?**
Yuni's Kitchen with BYO wine — two mains, a side, and a bottle you brought from home for about $50 total for two. Or the 300 Grams + Samuel Pepys combo above for under $60.

**Where should I go for a first date?**
Oh Loretta! The courtyard, the wine list, and the share plates format all work in your favour. Low pressure, high charm.

**Is there anything to do after dinner?**
High Note wine bar is open late. Northcote Social Club has gigs most nights. Bar Democracy does wine flights on Wednesdays.

## The Verdict

Northcote does date night the way it does everything else: without trying too hard, without charging too much, and with more charm than it probably realises. The formula is simple — pick a place on High Street, order something good, and actually talk to each other. The suburb does the rest.
