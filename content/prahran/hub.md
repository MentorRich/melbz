# Prahran, Melbourne
![Cover Image](https://images.pexels.com/photos/3760174/pexels-photo-3760174.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1260&h=750)

## Overview

Prahran is a sophisticated inner-city suburb located in the heart of Melbourne, known for its eclectic mix of high-end fashion boutiques, historic food markets, and legendary nightlife scene. With a postcode of 3181, Prahran offers a unique blend of old-world charm and modern attractions, making it a popular destination for both locals and tourists.

## Location and Transport

Prahran is situated approximately 5 kilometers southeast of Melbourne's Central Business District (CBD), with easy access to public transportation. The suburb is served by the [Prahran Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prahran_railway_station), which offers regular train services on the [Sandringham Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandringham_line). Additionally, several tram routes, including [Route 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tram_route_6_(Melbourne)), [Route 72](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tram_route_72_(Melbourne)), and [Route 78](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tram_route_78_(Melbourne)), connect Prahran to surrounding suburbs and the CBD.

For those driving, Prahran offers several parking options, including the [Prahran Central Car Park](https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/parking), [Greville Street Car Park](https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/parking), and [King Street Car Park](https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/parking). However, parking can be challenging, especially during peak hours.
