## Schools in Prahran: What Parents Actually Need to Know

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions you will make when moving to a suburb — and one of the least transparent. Real estate agents mention "great school zone" without telling you which schools, how they perform, or what the waitlist looks like. This guide covers the actual options in and around Prahran for 2026, with fees, reputations, and the details that matter.

## Primary Schools

### Stonnington Primary School

**Location:** Greville Street | **Type:** Public | **Level:** Prep-6

The local public primary. Small community feel, walking distance from Chapel Street. Strong parent involvement. Recently upgraded facilities.

### Melbourne Grammar School (Grimwade House)

**Location:** Domain Road, South Yarra (adjacent) | **Type:** Private | **Level:** Prep-6

One of Melbourne's most prestigious private primary schools. Historic campus near the Botanic Gardens. Fees in the $30,000+ range.

### Christ Church Grammar School

**Location:** South Yarra (adjacent) | **Type:** Private | **Level:** Prep-6

Well-regarded Anglican school with strong pastoral care. Fees around $22,000-28,000.

## Secondary Schools

### Prahran High School

**Location:** Orrong Road | **Type:** Public | **Level:** 7-12

Re-opened in 2019 after decades of closure. Modern facilities, vertical house model. Growing reputation. The local zoned option for Prahran families.

### Melbourne Grammar School

**Location:** Domain Road, South Yarra (adjacent) | **Type:** Private | **Level:** 7-12

Elite private boys school. Exceptional VCE results, extensive co-curricular programs. Old-money prestige. Fees $38,000+.

### Wesley College (St Kilda Road Campus)

**Location:** St Kilda Road (nearby) | **Type:** Private | **Level:** 7-12

Progressive private school with strong arts and sports programs. Co-educational. Fees around $35,000.

## The Education Landscape

Prahran is well-served by schools given its inner-city location. The reopening of Prahran High School filled a critical gap for local families who wanted a public secondary option without sending children across the city. Private schools in adjacent South Yarra are within walking or cycling distance.

## Childcare and Early Learning

Childcare in Prahran ranges from $120-160 per day. Several centres operate along Commercial Road and Chapel Street. Waitlists are shorter than CBD or South Yarra — typically 3-9 months.

## Enrolment Tips

For public schools, enrolment zones determine which school your child attends by default. Check the Find My School website (findmyschool.vic.gov.au) for current zone boundaries — they change periodically. For private and Catholic schools, applications typically open 12-18 months before the intended start year, and some prestigious schools have waitlists that begin at birth.

Moving to a suburb for the school zone? Confirm the boundaries before signing a lease. Streets can sit on zone edges, and the difference of one block can change your zoned school entirely.

## What to Consider

- **Public vs Private:** Public schools in Prahran are genuinely good. Do not assume private means better — visit both and compare.
- **Before and After School Care:** Availability varies. Popular programs fill quickly. Ask about this during school tours.
- **Travel:** Consider how your child will get to school. Walking distance matters more than you think, especially in primary years.
- **Community:** The school community often becomes your social network. Visit on a school day, talk to parents at pickup, and trust your gut.
