## The Best Date Night in Preston

Preston isn't trying to be the next Fitzroy for date nights. It's not competing with South Yarra's cocktail bars or the CBD's fine dining scene. What Preston does offer is something better for many couples: genuine, affordable, interesting date night options where you won't need a second mortgage and you won't feel like you're in a theme park designed for tourists.

Here's where to take your date in Preston in 2026, and what makes each option worth considering.

## Benzina Cantina — High Street

**Best for:** Mexican food with a rooftop that feels like a mini getaway

Benzina Cantina is one of Preston's best date night options for several reasons: the food is excellent, the drinks are well-made, and the rooftop bar turns a simple dinner into something that feels special. The neon-lit space has cactus-lined interiors, a spacious dining hall, and a rooftop that catches the afternoon sun and stays warm into the evening.

The tacos are the main event — pork belly with pickled slaw, spiced chicken with chipotle, mixed veg with black beans. The burritos are substantial. The margaritas are properly made, not just tequila-and-limeade. And the rooftop? On a warm evening with a couple of margaritas and a plate of tacos, you'll feel like you've been transported somewhere else — without the cost of a flight.

Pro tip: the rooftop is small and fills up fast on weekends. Go early or be prepared to wait. Weeknights are less crowded but equally good.

## Dexter — High Street

**Best for:** Fire-cooked modern Australian that feels special without being stuffy

Dexter is the kind of restaurant that works perfectly for a date night when you want to impress but not overwhelm. The food is modern Australian with a focus on fire — meat and vegetables kissed by flame, served with seasonal accompaniments that change regularly. The menu is concise, which means you won't spend 20 minutes deciding what to order, and the execution is consistent.

The wine list is tight and well-chosen, the cocktails are competent, and the atmosphere is relaxed without being casual. You're not in a fine dining room with white tablecloths, but you're also not in a pub where you can't hear yourself think.

Book ahead for Friday and Saturday nights. Weeknights are more likely to have walk-in availability.

## Oliva Social — High Street

**Best for:** Cocktail bar sophistication with a community feel

Oliva Social sits at 102-104 High Street and brings a touch of European sophistication to Preston's date night scene. The high ceilings, polished concrete floors, and fairy-lit courtyard create a space that feels more like a cocktail bar than a neighbourhood pub. The Chinotto Connection (whiskey, Chinotto San Pellegrino, lemon juice, honey) is their signature and it's worth trying.

The food menu covers pizzas, tapas, and grazing platters — nothing too heavy, nothing too light. The prices are reasonable, which means you can have a proper night out without the bill being a topic of conversation on the way home.

The courtyard is where Oliva Social really shines for dates. It's intimate without being cramped, and the fairy lights create that "we're somewhere special" feeling without the forced romance of a themed restaurant.

## Tavolata — High Street

**Best for:** Pasta date nights that feel like a proper occasion

Tavolata is Dexter's sibling, doing handmade pasta in a room that's more casual than Dexter but more refined than a standard trattoria. The pasta dishes rotate with the seasons, but expect combinations like cacio e pepe, ragu-based dishes, and seasonal specials that showcase whatever's good at the market that week.

The portions are generous, the prices are reasonable, and the atmosphere is the kind of relaxed that lets you linger over a second glass of wine without anyone giving you the look. It's the kind of place where you can have a proper conversation without shouting, which is more important for a date night than you might think.

## Hardout Bar — Plenty Road

**Best for:** Low-key dates with good music and no pressure

Hardout Bar is the kind of place where you can have a proper conversation without shouting over music, but the music is good enough that you notice it's there. The drinks list champions local and independent producers, the vinyl DJs spin on weekends, and the atmosphere is the kind of warm that makes you want to stay for hours.

The outdoor area is small but perfectly positioned for a relaxed evening. The indoor space has mismatched furniture and a bookshelf curated by Black Spark Cultural Centre, which gives it character without being try-hard.

This is the date night spot for couples who want to actually talk to each other, not just be in the same room looking at their phones.

## Rebel Rebel — Plenty Road

**Best for:** Dive bar dates that feel like a secret

Rebel Rebel is named after the David Bowie song and lives up to the name in the best possible way. Dim red lighting. Vintage photos on the walls. Records stacked barside. No pretension, no theme, just a genuine dive bar that's become a local favourite.

The drinks list is straightforward — spirits, beers, wine, and a couple of cocktails that prioritise flavour over presentation. In winter, they do mulled wine and hot toddies that are worth braving the cold for. The outdoor space is intimate — fairy lights, candles, just enough room for a handful of tables.

This is the date night spot for couples who want something different from the standard restaurant-bar combination. It's the kind of place where you feel like you've discovered something, even if everyone in Preston already knows about it.

## The Keys — Murray Road

**Best for:** Active dates and groups that want options

The Keys is what happens when someone builds a venue that's essentially an adult entertainment centre. 45 beer taps, 12 bowling lanes, a gaming arcade, a dance floor, and a beer garden for 600 people. It sounds excessive. It is. And it's brilliant for the right kind of date.

If you're the couple that likes to do things together — play games, compete, move around — The Keys gives you options. Start with drinks and food at the main bar, move to bowling for a bit of friendly competition, then head to the arcade or the dance floor. The variety means you're not stuck in the same environment for three hours.

The food is pub-style — pizzas, burgers, loaded fries — and the drinks list is comprehensive. Book ahead if you want bowling lanes.

## Getting There and Getting Home

Preston is well-connected by public transport. The 86 tram runs along High Street until late. The Mernda train line stops at Preston Station. Rideshare availability is good along the main roads. If you're out late, the Night Network buses cover parts of Preston — check the PTV app for current routes.

Parking is manageable compared to the inner suburbs. High Street itself can be tricky on weekend evenings, but the side streets and the carparks near the market are usually fine.

Preston Police Station is at 260 Hotham Street. If you or someone you're with needs help, call 000.

## Why Preston Works for Date Nights

Preston's date night advantage is value. You can have a full evening — dinner, drinks, maybe some entertainment — for what it would cost for a single course in the inner south. The quality is genuinely high, but the prices acknowledge that you're in Preston, not Toorak.

The variety is another advantage. If you're dating someone new, you can try a different venue every week without leaving the suburb. Mexican at Benzina, pasta at Tavolata, cocktails at Oliva, bowling at The Keys, pho at the market if you want something casual. Preston covers more date night bases than most suburbs its size.

The final advantage: authenticity. Preston's date night spots feel like they're built for the community, not for Instagram tourists. The staff are friendly, the atmosphere is relaxed, and nobody's judging you for staying late or leaving early. You can just enjoy the evening without the performance anxiety that sometimes comes with date nights in trendier suburbs.

## What We Skipped and Why

We focused on venues that make for proper date nights — places where you can have a conversation, enjoy good food and drink, and spend a couple of hours without feeling rushed. We left out the noisy bars, the venues with TVs everywhere, and the places that are more about being seen than actually connecting.

We also skipped the high-end fine dining options because Preston genuinely doesn't have many — and that's okay. If you want fine dining, you're better off in the CBD or [Northcote](/northcote/), which has some excellent chef-driven spots within 20 minutes of Preston.
