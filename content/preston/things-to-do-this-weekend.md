## The Best Things To Do This Weekend in Preston

Preston's weekend scene isn't about tourist attractions. It's about the rhythm of a multicultural suburb with a food and bar scene worth exploring. A weekend in Preston should include good food, good coffee, a market visit, and time spent in a bar where the bartender knows the regulars.

## Saturday: Start at the Market

**Preston Market** is the suburb's weekend anchor. Open Wednesday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, but Saturday is the main event. The market runs along Cramer Street and the laneways behind High Street.

**Go early.** The market gets crowded from 10am onwards. Arrive at 8-9am for space, fresher produce, and shorter queues.

**What to eat:**
- Gozleme from the Turkish stalls ($10-12)
- Fresh juice from the fruit vendors ($6-8)
- Lebanese pastries from the bakeries ($3-5 each)
- Vietnamese banh mi ($10-12)
- Chinese BBQ plates ($12-15)

Budget: $30-50 for a full market meal and coffee.

## Saturday: Brunch After the Market

**Near the market:** **Skinny's** on High Street — American-style breakfast sandwiches and solid coffee.

**Something different:** **Arepa Days** on Dundas Place — Colombian arepas with scrambled eggs, cheese, or pulled pork.

**Sit-down experience:** **George Jones** on Murray Road — ricotta hotcakes, eggs benedict, excellent coffee. Book ahead.

Budget: $20-35 per person.

## Saturday: Afternoon Drinks

- **Hardout Bar** (Plenty Road) — craft beer, local wine, vinyl spinning
- **The Keys** (Murray Road) — bowling, arcade games, 45 beer taps
- **Oliva Social** (102-104 High Street) — cocktails in the fairy-lit courtyard

## Saturday: Dinner Options

**Something nice:** **Dexter** (fire-cooked modern Australian) or **Tavolata** (handmade pasta), both on High Street. Book ahead.

**Mexican:** **Benzina Cantina** — rooftop tacos and margaritas.

**Vietnamese:** **Pho Hung** or **Lam Lam** on High Street. Both under $20.

**Pub dinner:** **The Olympic Hotel** bistro on Bell Street.

Budget: $25-50 per person.

## Saturday Night

- **Oliva Social** for cocktails in the courtyard
- **Surly's** on High Street for local brews
- **Rebel Rebel** on Plenty Road for dive bar vibes
- **The Keys** for bowling or dancing
- **Hardout Bar** for a session that extends into the early hours

Most Preston bars open until 1-2am on weekends. The 86 tram runs until about 12:30-1am.

## Sunday: Recovery

Sunday market is quieter than Saturday — better for a relaxed wander. Same food options, fewer crowds. **Moon Rabbit** on High Street for a gentle jaffle and flat white. **The Olympic Hotel** for a Sunday pub lunch.

## Family-Friendly Weekend

- **Preston Market** — kids love the bustle, food samples, and buskers
- **The Keys** on Murray Road — bowling lanes and arcade before 6pm
- **Moon Rabbit** and **Skinny's** — kid-friendly during the day
- Local parks — Darebin council-maintained playgrounds

## FAQ

**What's the best thing to do in Preston on a Saturday?**
Start at Preston Market early (8-9am), brunch at Skinny's or Arepa Days, afternoon drinks at Hardout Bar, dinner at Dexter or Benzina Cantina.

**Is Preston Market open on Sundays?**
Yes. Wednesday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. Sunday is quieter and better for a relaxed visit.

**What should I budget for a weekend in Preston?**
Budget weekend: $120 per person. Mid-range: $175. Splurge: $240.

## The Verdict

Preston works best when you don't over-plan. Pick a central spot — High Street near Murray Road is the sweet spot — start with the market, and see where the day takes you. The suburb is dense enough with good food and drink that you can wander and discover without a rigid itinerary. From $14 pho to fire-cooked modern Australian, from $10 gozleme to cocktails in a fairy-lit courtyard, Preston covers more weekend ground than most inner-north suburbs.
