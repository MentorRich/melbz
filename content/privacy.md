# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 18 March 2026*

MELBZ is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit melbz.com.au.


## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Email address** — when you subscribe to our newsletter
- **Contact information** — when you use our contact form
- **Communications** — when you email us directly

### Information We Collect Automatically
- **Analytics data** — pages you visit, time on site, referring URLs
- **Cookies** — essential cookies for site functionality, optional cookies for analytics
- **Device information** — browser type, operating system, IP address


## How We Use Your Information

### Improving Our Content
We use analytics to understand which articles perform best and what content our readers want. This helps us improve MELBZ and cover the suburbs you care about.

### Email Communications
If you subscribe to our newsletter, we'll send you:
- Weekly suburb briefings
- New article alerts
- Occasional updates about MELBZ

You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email.

### Advertising
We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your interests. See Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

## Australian Privacy Act 1988

MELBZ complies with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We handle personal information in accordance with Australian privacy law.

### Your Rights
Under Australian law, you have the right to:
- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Correct any inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information (where possible)


## Children's Privacy

MELBZ does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our content is general audience and not targeted at children.
