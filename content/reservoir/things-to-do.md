## What Is There to Do in Reservoir?

Reservoir sits 14km north of the CBD on the Mernda train line, and it has more going on than most people expect. Edwardes Lake, the Broadway shopping strip, a strong multicultural food scene, and genuinely usable parks give the suburb a solid base of things to do year-round. With median one-bedroom rents around $320 per week, you have enough left over to actually enjoy the neighbourhood.

## Outdoor Activities

**Edwardes Lake Park** — The centrepiece of Reservoir's outdoor life. The lake circuit is a 2.5km walking and cycling loop that locals use daily. Morning joggers, dog walkers, families with prams, and weekend picnickers all share the space. The park has BBQ facilities, playgrounds, and open grass areas for sport.

**Walking and Cycling** — Beyond the lake, Reservoir has flat, wide streets that suit cycling. You can ride south along High Street into Preston and connect to the Merri Creek trail for a longer route into Northcote and Clifton Hill. The Upfield bike path is also accessible from nearby Coburg.

**Weekend Sport** — Cricket in summer, footy in winter, and year-round fitness groups at Reservoir Leisure Reserve on Cheddar Road. Local sporting clubs are active and welcoming to newcomers. The Reservoir District Football Club has been part of the community for generations.

## Food and Drink Experiences

**Cafe Hopping on Broadway** — Melbourne's favourite pastime works well in Reservoir. Start at the station end of Broadway and work your way east. You will pass cafes, bakeries, and brunch spots that range from classic Australian to Italian-influenced.

**Multicultural Food Trail** — Reservoir's Italian, Vietnamese, Middle Eastern, and Greek communities mean you can eat a different cuisine every night of the week without leaving the suburb. See our [Reservoir Best Restaurants guide](/reservoir/best-restaurants/) for the full picture.

**Market Days** — Local markets and community food events pop up periodically at Edwardes Lake Park and along Broadway. Fresh produce, artisan food, and a neighbourhood atmosphere. Check the Darebin Council events calendar.

## Culture and Creative

**Darebin Arts and Entertainment Centre** — Located in nearby Preston, this venue hosts theatre, comedy, live music, and exhibitions throughout the year. Accessible by one train stop south from Reservoir station.

**Live Music** — Several pubs along Broadway book local acts on Thursday through Saturday nights. The scene is low-key but consistent — you can usually find something on without planning ahead.

**Community Events** — Darebin Council runs seasonal events, multicultural festivals, and neighbourhood programs. The Darebin Community and Kite Festival is a highlight.

## Family-Friendly Activities

- **Edwardes Lake playgrounds** — Well-maintained play areas with equipment for different age groups
- **Reservoir Library** on Broadway — Kids' readings, school holiday programs, free WiFi, and a quiet study space
- **Reservoir Leisure Centre** — Swimming pool access on Cheddar Road, with lessons and casual swim options
- **Nature walks** — The lake circuit is pram-friendly and flat enough for small legs

## Rainy Day Options

Melbourne weather means you need indoor plans. Reservoir has:
- **Reservoir Library** — Free WiFi, quiet space, community programs, and a solid kids' section
- **[Cafes](/reservoir/best-cafes/) on Broadway** — Grab a book and settle in for a couple of hours
- **Northland Shopping Centre** — A short drive or bus ride to Northland for cinema, retail, and food court options
- **Cooking at home** — Hit the Broadway delis and grocers for ingredients and make a project of it

## Free Things to Do

1. Walk the Edwardes Lake circuit — 2.5km of flat, scenic path
2. Browse Broadway's independent shops and delis
3. People-watch from a bench outside one of the Broadway cafes
4. Explore the residential streets between Cheddar Road and Broadway — the post-war architecture tells Reservoir's migration story
5. Join a free community fitness group at the local parks
6. Visit Reservoir Library for events, books, and community programs

## FAQ

**What is there to do in Reservoir on weekends?**
The Edwardes Lake circuit is the go-to outdoor activity. Beyond that, Broadway's cafes and restaurants are busy on weekends, and seasonal markets and community events pop up regularly. For bigger outings, Preston and Northcote are one train stop south.

**Is Reservoir good for families?**
Yes. Edwardes Lake Park, the Reservoir Leisure Centre pool, the local library, and a strong network of schools and sporting clubs make it a solid family suburb at a more affordable price point than inner-city alternatives.

**What is the main shopping area in Reservoir?**
Broadway is the main strip, running east from Reservoir station. It has cafes, restaurants, grocers, a Coles supermarket, specialty shops, and essential services. Northland Shopping Centre is also accessible by bus.

**How far is Reservoir from the Melbourne CBD?**
Reservoir is 14km north of the CBD. By train on the Mernda line, the trip to Melbourne Central takes about 25 to 30 minutes during peak hours.
