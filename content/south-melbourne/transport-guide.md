South Melbourne's transport game scored **A-** in our latest review. Here's what that actually means for your daily commute and weekend plans.

## Getting to the CBD

From South Melbourne to the CBD: 10-20 minutes by train or tram. Services run every 5-10 minutes during peak. This is one of the best-connected suburbs in Melbourne.

## The Tram & Train Routes

Check PTV.vic.gov.au for current timetables. The main routes serving South Melbourne connect to the CBD loop and key interchange stations.

## Cycling

South Melbourne is genuinely cycleable. Protected lanes on key routes, flat terrain, and under 10km to the CBD. Bike parking is decent at stations.

## Driving & Parking

Parking in South Melbourne is competitive. Street parking requires permits in most residential areas. If you're renting, check whether your building has a car space — they're not guaranteed.

## The Verdict

South Melbourne gets a **A-** for transport because it has excellent multi-modal options and fast CBD access. That's a serious advantage — transport is one of the main reasons people choose this suburb.
