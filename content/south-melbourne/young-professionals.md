## The After-Work Scene

South Melbourne has enough to keep you out on a weeknight. A few solid bars, a pub or two worth knowing, and the occasional late-night spot. For bigger nights, you're an Uber from the CBD.

## The Weekend Factor

Weekend brunch is a religion in South Melbourne. Saturday mornings mean queues at the top cafes, followed by market browsing, park sessions, or catching up on sleep. Sundays are slower but the coffee is still exceptional.

## The Commute

From South Melbourne to the CBD: 10-20 minutes by train or tram. Services run every 5-10 minutes during peak. This is one of the best-connected suburbs in Melbourne. For most young professionals, the commute from South Melbourne is a non-issue.

## Is South Melbourne Worth It for Young Professionals?

South Melbourne works for young professionals who prioritise food and walkability over a buzzing social scene. It's not the flashiest choice, but it's smart.
