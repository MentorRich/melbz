## Top Late-Night Eats in South Yarra

### 1. Tipo 00
Address: 164-166 Domville Street, South Yarra VIC 3141
Tipo 00 is a popular spot for Italian food, and for good reason. Their pasta dishes are made fresh daily, and their pizza menu is extensive. They offer a range of options, including vegan and gluten-free, and are open until 11pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

### 2. Embla
Address: 154-156 Toorak Road, South Yarra VIC 3141
Embla is a modern Australian restaurant with a focus on seasonal ingredients. Their menu changes regularly, but they often have a range of small plates and entrees available late at night. They're open until 12am on Fridays and Saturdays.

### 3. The Everleigh
Address: 2/50-54 Toorak Road, South Yarra VIC 3141
The Everleigh is a stylish bar and restaurant with a focus on craft cocktails and seasonal small plates. They're open until 1am on Fridays and Saturdays, making it a great spot for a late-night feed.

### 4. Supernormal
Address: 180-182 Toorak Road, South Yarra VIC 3141
Supernormal is a modern Asian restaurant with a range of small plates and entrees. They're open until 11pm on Fridays and Saturdays, and offer a great selection of Asian-inspired dishes.

### 5. Lune Croissanterie
Address: 155 Greville Street, South Yarra VIC 3141
Lune Croissanterie is a popular spot for breakfast and brunch, but they're also open late on Fridays and Saturdays. Their menu features a range of croissants, sandwiches, and salads, and they're open until 10pm on weekends.

### 6. Pellegrini's Espresso Bar
Address: 133-135 Greville Street, South Yarra VIC 3141
Pellegrini's Espresso Bar is a classic Italian cafe with a focus on coffee and desserts. They're open until 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays, and offer a range of espresso-based drinks and Italian treats.

### 7. Bistro 1967
Address: 114-116 Domville Street, South Yarra VIC 3141
