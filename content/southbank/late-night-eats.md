## Late Night Eats in Southbank

Southbank has one genuine advantage for late-night dining: Crown Casino and its surrounding complex keep things open when the rest of Melbourne shuts down. Here are the actual options worth your time after 10pm.

## Shujinko — 35-37 Riverside Quay

**Open:** 24 hours Friday-Saturday, until 2am other nights

The essential late-night Southbank venue. Proper tonkotsu ramen (#1 Black $17.90) at any hour. The broth is genuinely well-made, the noodles are proper, and the 24-hour weekend schedule makes it Southbank's most reliable after-midnight option. Located on Riverside Quay near **Southbank Boulevard**.

## Rockpool Bar & Grill — Crown, 8 Whiteman Street

**Kitchen open until:** 11:30pm

The late-night bar menu offers a more accessible way to experience Rockpool's world-class beef without the full fine-dining commitment. Located in the Crown complex off **Clarendon Street**. Not cheap, but genuinely good.

## Bistro Guillaume — Crown, 8 Whiteman Street

**Kitchen open until:** 11pm

French bistro cooking at Crown. The onion soup is a perfect late-night warmer after a walk along the river. More refined than most late-night options, but the kitchen stays open late enough to be useful.

## Ponyfish Island — Under the Pedestrian Bridge

**Open until:** 1am

Not a full kitchen, but drinks and bar snacks on a floating platform under the Southgate pedestrian bridge. The views of the CBD skyline at night are worth the visit alone. One of Melbourne's most unique late-night drinking spots.

## The Crown Food Court

**Various hours, some until midnight+**

Crown's food court has been upgraded significantly. It's no longer just chain outlets — several venues serve genuine quality at late hours. The advantage is variety and late opening. Not destination dining, but functional when everything else has closed.

## FAQ

**What's open 24 hours in Southbank?**
Shujinko on Riverside Quay is open 24 hours Friday-Saturday. Crown Casino itself operates 24/7 with various food options.

**Where's the best late-night food in Southbank?**
Shujinko for ramen. Rockpool's bar menu for quality. Bistro Guillaume for French bistro.

**Is there cheap late-night food in Southbank?**
Shujinko ramen at $17.90 is the best value. Crown food court has some affordable options.

## The Verdict

Southbank's late-night scene relies heavily on the Crown complex and Shujinko. That's both a strength (they're open when nothing else is) and a limitation (the variety is thin outside Crown). Shujinko's 24-hour weekend ramen is genuinely excellent and fills a gap that no other Southbank venue covers. For anything else after midnight, the Crown precinct on **Whiteman Street** and **Clarendon Street** is your only realistic option.
