## Southbank After Dark

Southbank transforms at night. During the day it's office workers and tourists. After dark, it becomes Melbourne's premium entertainment district — drinks are expensive, crowds are dressed up, and the vibe is unmistakably glitzy.

This is not a suburb for budget drinking or dive bars. Southbank exists to entertain and impress. Accept those terms and you can have a genuinely excellent night out.

## Lui Bar — Level 55, Eureka Tower, Riverside Quay

Melbourne's most spectacular bar view. Floor-to-ceiling windows looking out over the entire skyline. Cocktails $25-30, sophisticated and well-executed. Book ahead. Dress code applies.

## Ponyfish Island — Under the Pedestrian Bridge

Floating bar on the Yarra underneath the Southgate bridge. Tiny, quirky, stunning views both directions. Straightforward drinks, reasonable prices by Southbank standards. Open until 1am. Best on warm evenings.

## Hopscotch — 4 Riverside Quay

Extensive craft beer and whisky selection with riverside outdoor seating near **Southbank Boulevard**. Schooners $13-16. Pub-level food. Good for a more casual evening than the glitzy alternatives.

## Crown Entertainment Complex

Multiple bars, clubs, and lounges across Crown's complex on **Whiteman Street** and **Clarendon Street**. Quality varies from genuinely excellent cocktail bars to generic hotel lounges. The advantage: they stay open later than almost anything else. Crown also has live entertainment, the gaming floor (if that's your thing), and late-night dining.

## The Promenade After Dark

The Southbank Promenade along the river is Melbourne's most popular evening walking route. Street performers, lit-up [restaurants](/southbank/best-restaurants/), and the CBD skyline reflected in the water. Free and genuinely beautiful. Start at **Sturt Street** near the Arts Centre and walk west toward Crown.

## FAQ

**What time do Southbank bars close?**
Most close around midnight-1am. Crown venues stay open later — some operate 24/7.

**Is Southbank nightlife expensive?**
Yes. Cocktails $20-30, craft beer $13-16, wine $15-22. This is Melbourne's premium entertainment district.

**What's the best bar in Southbank for a special occasion?**
Lui Bar at Level 55, Eureka Tower. The view alone justifies the cocktail price.

## The Verdict

Southbank's nightlife is premium, view-driven, and concentrated around the Promenade and Crown. It's not a place for casual pub sessions or dive bar adventures — for that, cross the river to the [CBD](/melbourne-cbd/) or catch tram 96 to [St Kilda](/st-kilda/). But for a spectacular cocktail at Lui Bar, a quirky drink on Ponyfish Island, or a craft beer at Hopscotch watching the river, Southbank delivers an evening experience that few Melbourne suburbs can match. The Promenade walk alone — free, beautiful, and available every night — is one of Melbourne's best after-dark activities.
