# Melbourne Student Suburb Guide

Melbourne has over 300,000 uni and TAFE students spread across dozens of suburbs. Picking where to live is one of the biggest decisions you'll make — and most people get it wrong because they either overpay for a shoebox in the CBD or end up 90 minutes from campus in a suburb with nothing going on.

This guide exists to fix that. Every article is written by people who actually lived in these suburbs as students, not real estate agents trying to sell you something.
