## Every Melbourne Suburb, Covered by Locals

Melbourne is not one city. It is 321 suburbs arguing about which one is best. MELBZ covers them all — from the inner-city laneways where a flat white costs more than your hourly parking, to the outer suburbs where you can actually park in the first place.

This is the complete directory. Every suburb below has its own page with local intelligence: what the area is actually like, what it costs to live there, where to eat and drink, how to get around, and whether the vibe matches what the real estate agent told you.

**How to use this page:** Find your suburb or a suburb you are considering. Click through for the full local guide. If you are comparing options, check our [suburb comparisons](/comparisons/) and [rankings](/rankings/) for head-to-head data.

## How We Cover Suburbs

Every suburb page on MELBZ is written by someone who has spent real time there. We do not copy census data into a template and call it a guide. Each page includes:

- **The honest vibe** — what it actually feels like to walk around on a Tuesday afternoon, not just a sunny Saturday
- **Real costs** — current rental prices, grocery costs, and the price of a coffee on the main strip
- **Food and drink** — specific venues with opinions, not a list of everything on Google Maps
- **Transport** — actual commute times tested during peak hour, not the optimistic Google estimate
- **Who lives here** — the real demographic, not the aspirational one from the council website
