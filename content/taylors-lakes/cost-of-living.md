## Is Taylors Lakes Expensive?

Taylors Lakes sits somewhere in the middle of Melbourne's cost spectrum. It's not cheap, but it's not going to break the bank either — assuming you're realistic about expectations.

The cost of living here reflects what the suburb offers. Location, community, access to amenities — you get what you pay for, and in Taylors Lakes, you're generally getting good value for the price point.

## Renting in Taylors Lakes

The rental market in Taylors Lakes is active with a reasonable range of options at different price points. There's a mix of apartments, units, townhouses, and the occasional freestanding house.

Studio and one-bedroom apartments are the entry point. Two-bedders suit couples and sharers.

Share houses are an option for younger renters. They're common in Taylors Lakes and a practical way to live in the suburb without the full rent burden.

Compared to the Melbourne metro average, Taylors Lakes is roughly on par or slightly below — it represents reasonable value for what you get.

## Buying in Taylors Lakes

The property market in Taylors Lakes has opportunities for first-home buyers, particularly in the apartment and townhouse market.

The market has moved over the past few years, like everywhere in Melbourne. What was considered expensive five years ago now looks like good buying in hindsight.

## Day-to-Day Costs

**Coffee:** Melbourne being Melbourne, you'll pay standard cafe prices. A flat white runs the same here as most inner suburbs. There are cheaper options and fancier ones.

**Groceries:** Supermarket access is good — you've got your major chains plus smaller independent shops. Prices are standard Melbourne. If you shop smart and cook at home, the grocery bill is manageable.

**Eating out:** Taylors Lakes has options across the price range. You can get a solid weeknight dinner without it being a major financial event. Friday night at the nicer spots will cost you more, obviously.

**Transport:** If you're using PT, a Myki covers your daily commute at standard Melbourne zone pricing. If you drive, factor in potential parking costs.

## Tips for Keeping Costs Down in Taylors Lakes

1. **Cook at home most nights** — the grocery options are good, and you'll save hundreds per month compared to eating out
2. **Use PT when you can** — saves on parking, petrol, and rego costs
3. **Find your local spots** — the touristy-looking cafes often charge more than the local favourite around the corner
4. **Markets and local shops** — often cheaper than the big supermarkets for fresh produce
5. **Share if you can** — a two-bedder split with a mate in Taylors Lakes costs less than a studio on your own in a flasher suburb
