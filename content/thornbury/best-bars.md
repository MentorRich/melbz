Thornbury's bar scene runs along High Street between Darebin Road and Dundas Street, and it's one of the inner north's best-kept secrets. No cover charges, no dress codes, no bottle service — just proper drinks in venues that serve locals first. Here are the bars worth knowing.

## 1. Carwyn Cellars Back Room

**The vibe:** Melbourne's unofficial craft beer parliament, hidden behind one of the city's best bottle shops.

Carwyn Cellars at 877 High Street is primarily a bottle shop, but the back room bar is a pub in every sense that matters: communal tables, 20-odd rotating taps of Victorian craft beer, and a crowd that ranges from industry insiders to locals wanting a good pint. Weekly tap takeovers bring brewers in to talk you through each pour.

**Order this:** A tasting paddle of four ($16)
**Address:** 877 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Mon-Thu 12-10pm, Fri-Sat 12-11pm, Sun 12-9pm
**Insider tip:** Wednesday $10 pint nights on selected taps are Thornbury's best mid-week deal.

## 2. Franklin's Bar

**The vibe:** A converted warehouse with a 3am licence, woodfired pizza, and hospitality that remembers your name.

Franklin's at 517 High Street straddles the line between bar and pub with ease. High ceilings, exposed brick, and enough room that it never feels cramped even on a Saturday. The cocktail list is solid, the Victorian beer selection is curated, and the woodfired pizzas prevent regrettable late-night food decisions. The 3am licence makes this where Thornbury nights go to extend themselves.

**Order this:** Margherita pizza and a Franklin's spritz ($20 + $18)
**Address:** 517 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Mon-Thu 4pm-1am, Fri-Sat 4pm-3am, Sun 4pm-11pm
**Insider tip:** The back corner booth is the best seat. Get there before 8pm Friday to claim it.

## 3. Gigi Rooftop

**The vibe:** Sunset Negronis above High Street with views across the northern suburbs.

Perched above Umberto Espresso Bar at 676 High Street, Gigi Rooftop is Thornbury's answer to overpriced city rooftop bars. The cocktails lean Italian — Aperol Spritz, Negroni, Pina Colada Punch — and the food comes up from Umberto's kitchen. Limited seating means everyone's slightly squashed together, which in Melbourne code means "making friends."

**Order this:** Negroni with a cheesy 'nduja soldier ($22 + $7)
**Address:** 676 High St, Thornbury (upstairs)
**Hours:** Thu-Sat 5-10pm, Sun 4-9pm
**Insider tip:** Arrive before 6:30pm for a railing seat. After that you're in the middle.

## 4. Capers

**The vibe:** Greek neighbourhood wine bar with retro furniture and wines you won't find elsewhere.

Capers at 877 High Street draws on Thornbury's post-war Greek heritage. The pickled calamari is the signature dish, the wine list leans Greek — Xinomavro and Assyrtiko alongside the usual suspects — and the retro decor is genuinely charming, not Instagram-ironic. It's one of those places that feels like a secret even though everyone knows about it.

**Order this:** Pickled calamari with a glass of Xinomavro ($22 + $14)
**Address:** 877 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Tue-Sat 12pm-11pm, Sun-Mon 12pm-10pm
**Insider tip:** Sunday arvo with a spritz in the sun is one of Thornbury's quiet pleasures.

## 5. Pallino Bar & Bocce

**The vibe:** A 1950s espresso bar turned spritz-and-bocce joint on High Street.

Pallino occupies a genuine 1950s espresso bar space and has added bocce courts and an Italian-leaning drinks list. Owner Louise Zelencich built this for her neighbours, and it shows. The bocce is real, the Aperol Spritz is cold, and the retro setting is the actual bones of the building, not a design agency creation.

**Order this:** A Spritz and a game of bocce ($18)
**Address:** High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Wed-Sun 12-10pm
**Insider tip:** Book the bocce court for groups of 6+. Friday night becomes an event without the event price tag.

## 6. Barton Fink

**The vibe:** Morning coffee spot that transforms into a serious cocktail bar by evening.

Barton Fink on High Street does the day-to-night transition better than most. Morning coffee is specialty-grade, evening cocktails are well-crafted, and the window seats with natural light make it a surprisingly good solo spot. They run occasional cocktail masterclasses that sell out fast.

**Order this:** An old fashioned ($20)
**Address:** High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** From 7:30am daily (cafe); bar from 5pm Wed-Sat
**Insider tip:** The contrast between morning barista and evening mixologist is worth witnessing in the same visit.

## 7. Nasty's

**The vibe:** A dive bar with a drinks list short enough to fit on a coaster. No cocktails, no problems.

Nasty's is the cheapest night out on the High Street strip. The drinks list is deliberately tight: beer, wine, spirits. No cocktail menu, no mixology, no pretension. A pint for $9 in 2026 Thornbury is practically charity. The crowd is locals who've been coming for years and newcomers who instantly feel like regulars.

**Order this:** A schooner of whatever's cold ($9)
**Address:** High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Thu-Sat 5pm-late
**Insider tip:** Cash moves faster at the bar on busy nights.

## Getting Home

The 86 tram runs along High Street until approximately 1am (later on weekends via Night Network). Thornbury station on the Mernda line is a short walk from most venues. Rideshare pickup zones are along High Street — avoid hailing from side streets.

## The Verdict

Thornbury's bar scene doesn't need gimmicks. Start at Carwyn for the craft education, hit Gigi for sunset drinks, play bocce at Pallino, swing by Capers for Greek wines, and end at Nasty's for the $9 pints. That's a proper night out for $80-120 per person including food.

## FAQ

**What's the best bar for craft beer in Thornbury?**
Carwyn Cellars, no contest. Twenty rotating taps and the bottle shop pricing is better than retail.

**Are there late-night bars in Thornbury?**
Franklin's has a 3am licence on Friday and Saturday. Most other bars close by 1am.

**Do Thornbury bars have cover charges?**
No. Not even on Saturday night. If a bar is charging a cover, you're in the wrong bar.
