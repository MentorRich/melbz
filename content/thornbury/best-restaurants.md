Thornbury's restaurant scene runs along High Street between Darebin Road and Dundas Street, and it's quietly become one of Melbourne's best dining strips. The cuisine is eclectic — Calabrian pasta, Yorkshire pies, Greek wine bars, vegan pub food, woodfired pizza — and the prices haven't been inflated by a Broadsheet feature. Here's where to eat.

## 1. Umberto Espresso Bar

**The vibe:** Your nonna's kitchen if your nonna was Calabrian and had a rooftop bar upstairs.

Set inside a former shoe shop on High Street — the old signage still reads "Arthur's Shoes" — Umberto is a love letter to the owner's Calabrian-born father. Handmade pasta arrives on enamelled plates, the wine list makes you want a second bottle before the first is empty, and the orecchiette with broccoli and anchovies is the dish regulars argue about.

**Order this:** Orecchiette with broccoli and anchovies ($26)
**Address:** 676 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Wed-Mon 8am-3pm, 5:30-10pm; Tue closed
**Insider tip:** Duck upstairs to Gigi Rooftop for a Negroni after dinner. Same owners, sunset views.

## 2. Northern Git

**The vibe:** A Yorkshire pub in Melbourne serving proper British food with zero pretension.

The owner-chef is from Yorkshire, and the menu reads like a proper British boozer: pork crackling with apple sauce, bubble and squeak, pies with mushy peas and gravy, and a Sunday roast that draws crowds from across the inner north. The space is compact — a handful of wooden tables, a small bar, and genuine warmth.

**Order this:** Pie of the day with mushy peas and gravy ($19)
**Address:** 766A High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Wed-Sun 11:30am-9pm; Mon-Tue closed
**Insider tip:** The weekday lunch pie special at $15 is the best value meal on High Street. Arrive by 1pm — they sell out.

## 3. Farro Pizzeria

**The vibe:** Homely Italian pizzeria with woodfired spelt bases and inclusive options.

Farro's woodfired margherita at $19 is a proper pizza — blistered base, fresh mozzarella, San Marzano tomato sauce, and that slight char that separates woodfired from everything else. Gluten-free, vegan, and vegetarian options are genuine, not afterthoughts.

**Order this:** Woodfired margherita ($19)
**Address:** 578 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Tue-Sun 5:30-10pm; Mon closed
**Insider tip:** Two margheritas and a focaccia ($46) is dinner for two under $50. In 2026 Melbourne, that's remarkable.

## 4. Sandro Pasta & Wine

**The vibe:** Convivial Italian wine bar where handmade pasta is the headline act.

Sandro's at 633 High Street does handmade pasta that cooks in 3-4 minutes and comes with a wine list that makes lingering dangerous. Eat in or take home — packets of fresh pasta run $14-16 and are the suburb's best budget dinner hack.

**Order this:** Handmade pasta of the day ($22) with a glass of Italian red ($13)
**Address:** 633 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Wed-Sun 12-10pm; Mon-Tue closed
**Insider tip:** Ask the staff which sauce pairs with which pasta. They'll talk you through it like a proper Italian nonna.

## 5. Ballard's

**The vibe:** All-vegan pub food that doesn't preach — it just serves good meals.

Ballard's at 871 High Street occupies a unique position: a pub where everything is plant-based but nobody makes a fuss about it. The house-made gnocchi is excellent, the beer-battered options satisfy pub-food cravings, and the beer garden catches afternoon sun.

**Order this:** House-made gnocchi with a Victorian pale ale ($14 + $9)
**Address:** 871 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Tue-Sat 12-10pm, Sun 12-9pm; Mon closed
**Insider tip:** Weekday happy hour 4-6pm: $8 pints and half-price small bites.

## 6. Franklin's Bar

**The vibe:** Warehouse bar with woodfired pizza that prevents regrettable late-night food decisions.

Franklin's at 517 High Street does solid woodfired pizzas alongside a proper cocktail list. The 3am licence on weekends means the kitchen runs late, and the margherita at $20 is the best pizza you'll eat after midnight in the inner north.

**Order this:** Margherita pizza and a Franklin's spritz ($20 + $18)
**Address:** 517 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Mon-Thu 4pm-1am, Fri-Sat 4pm-3am, Sun 4pm-11pm
**Insider tip:** Early weeknight pizzas with a colleague are an underrated Thornbury move.

## 7. Capers

**The vibe:** Greek neighbourhood bar with retro furniture, real food, and wines you won't find elsewhere.

Capers draws on Thornbury's post-war Greek heritage. The pickled calamari is the signature, the vegan moussaka is surprisingly rich, and the wine list features Greek varietals — Xinomavro and Assyrtiko — alongside more familiar bottles.

**Order this:** Pickled calamari with a glass of Xinomavro ($22 + $14)
**Address:** 877 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Tue-Sat 12pm-11pm, Sun-Mon 12pm-10pm
**Insider tip:** Sunday arvo with a spritz is one of Thornbury's quiet pleasures.

## 8. Casa Sicilia Caffe

**The vibe:** Sicilian cafe culture — arancini the size of your fist and espresso that resets your nervous system.

One of the newer spots on the strip, Casa Sicilia at 835 High Street brings proper Sicilian breakfast to Thornbury. Brioche and granita for the morning, four types of golden-fried arancini for later. Small, busy on weekends, and the arancini sell out fast.

**Order this:** Arancini trio ($15)
**Address:** 835 High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Wed-Mon 7:30am-3:30pm; Tue closed
**Insider tip:** Go early Saturday. By 10am the arancini are gone.

## 9. Greek Street Food

**The vibe:** Permanent souva spot serving stacked Greek plates with zero frills.

Greek Street Food started as a food truck and now operates from a permanent High Street location. The chicken souvlaki plate at $17 includes skewers, salad, pita, and enough tzatziki to drown a small village.

**Order this:** Chicken souvlaki plate with extra tzatziki ($17)
**Address:** High St, Thornbury
**Hours:** Tue-Sun 11:30am-9pm; Mon closed
**Insider tip:** The lamb skewers at $19 are worth the two-dollar stretch.

## The Verdict

Thornbury's restaurant strip on High Street is one of Melbourne's most underrated. Umberto for Calabrian pasta, Northern Git for Yorkshire pies, Farro for woodfired pizza, and Ballard's for vegan pub food — all within walking distance of each other and the 86 tram. Weeknight dining is the sweet spot: walk-in friendly, quieter, and better service.
