## Where the Money Actually Goes

**Groceries:** Standard supermarket territory. There's a Woolies or Coles accessible, plus a couple of smaller shops. Budget $80-$120/week for one person.

**Eating out:** The average meal out in Thornbury runs $18-$25 for lunch and $30-$50 for dinner. Check our [cheap eats guide](/thornbury/cheap-eats/) for the spots that don't punish your wallet.

**Transport:** A full-price Myki costs $10 daily cap, $50 weekly cap in Zone 1-2. If you're in Thornbury, you're firmly in Zone 1, which keeps your Myki costs predictable. An annual Myki pass is the smart move if you commute daily.

## The Hidden Costs Nobody Warns You About

1. **Parking permits** — If you drive, council parking permits run $50-$150/year depending on the area
2. **Brunch culture tax** — You will spend more on breakfast than you budget for. It's Melbourne law.
3. **The "quick drink after work" budget** — A pint in Thornbury averages $12-$14. Cocktails run $18-$24.
4. **Laundry** — If your apartment doesn't have a washing machine, coin laundry runs $8-$12 per load

## Is Thornbury Worth the Cost?

Thornbury offers genuine value. A Vibe Score of 79 at these prices is uncommon. The food scene (B+) and transport (B+) punch above the price point. This is one of Melbourne's better deals.
