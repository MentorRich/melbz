## The After-Work Scene

Thornbury has enough to keep you out on a weeknight. A few solid bars, a pub or two worth knowing, and the occasional late-night spot. For bigger nights, you're an Uber from the CBD.

## The Weekend Factor

Weekends in Thornbury are chill. Brunch is possible but not a competitive sport. You'll find your routine — a preferred cafe, a go-to park, and the occasional exploration of nearby strips.

## The Commute

From Thornbury to the CBD: 20-35 minutes by public transport. Trains run every 10-15 minutes during peak. Reliable but not the fastest commute. For most young professionals, the commute from Thornbury is manageable.

## Is Thornbury Worth It for Young Professionals?

Thornbury works for young professionals who prioritise transport and value over a buzzing social scene. It's not the flashiest choice, but it's smart.
