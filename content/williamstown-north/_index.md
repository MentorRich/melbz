## Who Lives in Williamstown North?

Williamstown North draws a particular crowd. The demographics lean towards the kind of people who value what this suburb specifically offers. You'll find a mix — and it's the mix that makes it work.

## Housing in Williamstown North

The housing stock in Williamstown North tells the story of how the suburb has evolved. You'll find a mix of older homes that have been here for decades alongside newer build quality — townhouses, apartments, and the occasional knockdown-rebuild that sticks out on the street.

If you're renting, there's decent variety depending on your budget. Share houses exist for those starting out. Units and apartments work for professionals. Families will need to hunt a bit harder for standalone homes with a backyard, but they're out there.

## Eating and Drinking in Williamstown North

The food scene in Williamstown North reflects the suburb's personality.
