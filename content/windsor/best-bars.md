Windsor's bar scene runs the full length of Chapel Street from High Street down to Dandenong Road, with a few hidden gems tucked into laneways that most visitors walk past. This is not the polished cocktail-bar strip of [South Yarra](/south-yarra/) — Windsor's bars are grittier, more personal, and priced for people who actually live here.

Here are the bars worth knowing about in 2026.

### 1. Jungle Boy

**Address:** 301a Chapel Street, Windsor (behind Boston Sub)
**Hours:** 5pm-2am Wed-Sun
**Budget:** $20-$26 cocktails

The entrance is a fridge door inside Boston Sub sandwich shop. Push through and you are in a plant-filled tiki bar with tropical cocktails in ceramic mugs, rum-heavy drinks with fresh fruit and house syrups, and a vibe that feels like a holiday. Capacity is around 40, so it stays intimate. No food menu — eat before you arrive.

**Why it matters:** It is the bar that put Windsor on Melbourne's cocktail map. The hidden entrance is part of the experience.

### 2. Her Bar

**Address:** 2 Windsor Place, Windsor
**Hours:** 5pm-2am Tue-Sun
**Budget:** $20-$26 cocktails

Tucked into Windsor Place, a laneway most people walk past without noticing. Dark wood, warm lighting, and a cocktail program built around the concept of dualities — sweet and bitter, light and dark. The bartenders will custom-make a drink based on your preferences if you tell them what you like.

Perfect for date night or a quiet catch-up. No food, just drinks.

### 3. Borsch, Vodka & Tears

**Address:** 133a Chapel Street, Windsor
**Hours:** 12pm-2am Wed-Mon, closed Tuesday
**Budget:** $18-$24 cocktails, $14-$28 food

Over 15 years on Chapel Street and still going strong. The vodka list runs past 100 varieties, including house infusions. The borsch and pierogi are legitimate Eastern European fare. The atmosphere is warm and bohemian — mismatched furniture, candlelight, the kind of place where you come for one drink and stay for four.

The chilli-infused vodka is exceptional. Start there.

### 4. Better Days Bar

**Address:** 332 Chapel Street, Windsor
**Hours:** 4pm-2am daily
**Budget:** $18-$24 cocktails, $12-$18 food

Cocktails and board games (and an N64). The cocktail menu is thoughtful, the board game collection is extensive, and the retro console setup means you can settle in for hours. Food covers pizza, tapas, and cheese boards designed for grazing. Thursday night board game tournaments with prizes are a weekly fixture.

### 5. Hoo Haa Bar

**Address:** 264a Chapel Street, Windsor (upstairs)
**Hours:** 5pm-3am Thu-Sat, 5pm-1am Wed and Sun
**Budget:** $18-$24 cocktails, $3 cover after 10pm Thu-Sat

Three levels: ground floor has the pop-up restaurant (currently Kuku Mama, pan-African), middle level is the cocktail bar, upstairs is the dance floor with DJs spinning house, disco, and mainstream. Come for dinner at Kuku Mama and skip the cover charge, then stay for cocktails and dancing.

### 6. The Osborne Rooftop & Bar

**Address:** 545 Chapel Street, Windsor (south end)
**Hours:** 4pm-2am Fri-Sat, 4pm-12am Sun-Thu
**Budget:** $20-$26 cocktails, $12-$14 beers

Rooftop drinking at the south end of Chapel Street with views across Windsor's rooftops to the city skyline on clear evenings. The cocktail list is seasonal, the food is solid enough for dinner, and Sunday arvo sessions with DJs and golden-hour light are the move. Weather-dependent — when it is good, it is very good.

## When to go

**Weeknights (Tue-Thu):** The sweet spot. Fewer crowds, better service, bartenders have time to chat and make recommendations. Better Days and Her Bar are particularly good on quiet nights.

**Friday:** The strip fills up from 6pm. Arrive early or expect to wait at Jungle Boy and Hoo Haa. The Wolf's bifold doors on Chapel Street create a street-party atmosphere.

**Saturday:** Peak night. Everything is busy. Book dinner first, then bar-hop south along Chapel Street.

**Sunday:** Underrated. The Osborne rooftop does afternoon DJ sessions. Borsch, Vodka & Tears is relaxed and conversational.

## FAQ

**What is the best hidden bar in Windsor?** Jungle Boy behind the fridge door in Boston Sub. Her Bar in Windsor Place laneway is also genuinely hidden.

**Are Windsor bars cheaper than South Yarra?** Yes. Cocktails average $20-$24 versus $24-$30 in South Yarra. Beer prices are comparable.

**What time do Windsor bars close?** Most close at 1-2am. Hoo Haa goes to 3am Thursday-Saturday. Circus nightclub runs until 5am on weekends.

## Verdict

Windsor's bar scene works because it serves locals first. These are not Instagram destinations you visit once — they are places you go weekly. The variety runs from hidden tiki bars to rooftop cocktails to bohemian vodka joints, all within walking distance on Chapel Street.
