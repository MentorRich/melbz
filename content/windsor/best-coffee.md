## The Best Coffee in Windsor

Windsor does not mess around with its coffee. This is the stretch of Chapel Street where the tourism crowds thin out and the real Melbourne kicks in — the one that actually cares about extraction times and milk texture. Forget the flashy storefronts up in [South Yarra](/south-yarra/); the Windsor coffee scene is where substance beats style, and a $4.50 flat white from a no-name roaster will genuinely ruin you for everything else.

Here is where to get the best cup in Windsor right now.

### 1. Fourth Chapter

**Address:** 272 Chapel Street, Windsor

Fourth Chapter is the kind of cafe that makes you feel slightly guilty for ever drinking instant. The fit-out is minimal — polished concrete, big windows, not a single unnecessary decoration — but the coffee program is anything but. They roast in-house using a Probat, and the single-origin filter rotates weekly. A flat white runs $4.80, a batch brew $5.50, and the pour-over is $6.50 for something genuinely special.

The baristas here take their craft seriously without being pretentious about it. Ask them what they are pulling and they will tell you about the Ethiopian natural they have been dialing in, not with arrogance but with enthusiasm. The weekend brunch menu is solid too — the smashed avo with dukkah on sourdough is $19.

**Insider tip:** Come on a weekday before 9am and you will get the place practically to yourself. By 10:30 on weekends, you are queuing.

### 2. Mr Mister

**Address:** 228 Chapel Street, Windsor

Mr Mister has been a Chapel Street stalwart since before "specialty coffee" was a term people threw around at dinner parties. The espresso blend is chocolatey and reliable. A standard coffee is $4.50, and the all-day breakfast runs until 3pm. The corn fritters with avocado salsa and poached eggs ($19.50) are the order that keeps regulars coming back.

**Insider tip:** Their iced latte in summer uses a cold brew base rather than just pouring hot coffee over ice. It makes a genuine difference.

### 3. Staple

**Address:** 173 Chapel Street, Windsor

Staple occupies that sweet spot between "serious about coffee" and "does not take itself too seriously." The espresso is pulled on a La Marzocca and the milk is always silky. A flat white is $4.50 and a breakfast roll with scrambled eggs, bacon, and house-made relish is $14. This is a neighbourhood cafe in the truest sense — the staff know regulars by name.

**Insider tip:** They do a $3.50 short black that hits harder than most long blacks elsewhere.

### 4. Franklin Windsor

**Address:** 177 Chapel Street, Windsor

The coffee program uses Five Senses beans, and the execution is consistent — well-extracted, well-textured, served quickly. Flat whites are $4.80. The food is the real draw: the ricotta hotcakes with honeycomb butter ($21) and the breakfast ramen with a soft egg and crispy shallots ($22).

**Insider tip:** There is usually a queue on weekends from 9:30 onwards. Book ahead or come after 12:30.

### 5. High Society

**Address:** 246 Chapel Street, Windsor

The cafe doubles as a florist, which means you are drinking your coffee surrounded by native banksias and eucalyptus arrangements. The $4.50 flat white is consistently smooth. The Vietnamese-style iced coffee ($7.50) with condensed milk is properly strong. The outdoor seating area is one of the best on Chapel Street — shady, spacious, removed from foot traffic.

### 6. Cheeky Monkey

**Address:** 298 Chapel Street, Windsor

The reliable workhorse. $4.20 for a flat white, $15 for eggs Benedict on brioche. Espresso is punchy, portions generous, service fast. Wednesday mornings they do a two-coffee-and-toast special for $12 — not widely advertised but regulars know.

## Coffee in Windsor: What You Actually Need to Know

Windsor's coffee scene is concentrated along Chapel Street, with a few outliers on the side streets near High Street and Albert Street. The average flat white sits around $4.50 — roughly in line with Melbourne's inner south but noticeably cheaper than South Yarra five minutes up the road.

Most Windsor [cafes](/windsor/best-cafes/) use locally roasted beans, with Five Senses, Proud Mary, and several micro-roasters represented. You will not find many chain coffee brands here.

## FAQ

**What is the cheapest good coffee in Windsor?** Cheeky Monkey at $4.20 for a flat white. Staple's $3.50 short black if you want maximum caffeine per dollar.

**Where is the best single-origin coffee in Windsor?** Fourth Chapter, which roasts in-house and rotates single-origin filter weekly.

**Best cafe to work from in Windsor?** Mr Mister has the most space and best WiFi. Fourth Chapter is good on weekday mornings.

## Verdict

Six cafes within a 10-minute walk, all pulling serious coffee, all priced between $4.20 and $4.80 for a flat white. Windsor's coffee scene rewards loyalty — find your spot, become a regular, and enjoy the best daily ritual in Melbourne's inner south.
