## The Weekend Factor

Weekend brunch is a religion in Windsor. Saturday mornings mean queues at the top cafes, followed by market browsing, park sessions, or catching up on sleep. Sundays are slower but the coffee is still exceptional.

## The Commute

From Windsor to the CBD: 10-20 minutes by train or tram. Services run every 5-10 minutes during peak. This is one of the best-connected suburbs in Melbourne. For most young professionals, the commute from Windsor is a non-issue.

## Is Windsor Worth It for Young Professionals?

Windsor works for young professionals who prioritise food and walkability over a buzzing social scene. It's not the flashiest choice, but it's smart.
