## Where Is Yallambie?

Yallambie sits in Melbourne's North East region. It's the kind of suburb where you can feel the neighbourhood's personality the minute you step off public transport. Getting to the city from here isn't a drama — you've got options depending on where exactly you are in the suburb.

The suburb borders Viewbank, Macleod, Bundoora. That matters because your nearby options for food, coffee, and weekend plans extend well beyond Yallambie's own postcode boundaries.

## Who Lives in Yallambie?

Yallambie draws a particular crowd. The demographics lean towards the kind of people who value what this suburb specifically offers. You'll find a mix — and it's the mix that makes it work.

## Housing in Yallambie

The housing stock in Yallambie tells the story of how the suburb has evolved. You'll find a mix of older homes that have been here for decades alongside newer build quality — townhouses, apartments, and the occasional knockdown-rebuild that sticks out on the street.

If you're renting, there's decent variety depending on your budget. Share houses exist for those starting out. Units and apartments work for professionals. Families will need to hunt a bit harder for standalone homes with a backyard, but they're out there.

## Eating and Drinking in Yallambie

The food scene in Yallambie reflects the suburb's personality.
