Yarraville's transport game scored **B+** in our latest review. Here's what that actually means for your daily commute and weekend plans.

## Getting to the CBD

From Yarraville to the CBD: 20-35 minutes by public transport. Trains run every 10-15 minutes during peak. Reliable but not the fastest commute.

## The Tram & Train Routes

Check PTV.vic.gov.au for current timetables. The main routes serving Yarraville connect to the CBD loop and key interchange stations.

## Cycling

Yarraville is genuinely cycleable. Protected lanes on key routes, flat terrain, and under 10km to the CBD. Bike parking is decent at stations.

## Driving & Parking

Parking in Yarraville is generally manageable compared to inner-city suburbs. Most houses have driveways or garages. Street parking is available but can tighten near commercial strips.

## The Verdict

Yarraville gets a **B+** for transport because it covers the basics well — regular trains and bus connections. Functional. Not amazing, not a dealbreaker. You can make it work.
