# CONTENT QUALITY GATE — 7/10 Minimum to Publish

## Score Every Article Before Publishing

| Criteria | Points | Check |
|----------|:------:|-------|
| Hook quality | /2 | First 2 sentences create specific curiosity gap or pattern interrupt. Would this stop a scroll? |
| Specificity | /2 | Real venue names, real addresses, real prices ($14 laksa, $5.50 flat white), real tram routes |
| Emotional trigger | /1 | At least one moment of pride, outrage, amusement, nostalgia, or FOMO |
| Honest negative | /1 | At least one "what we skipped and why" or honest criticism. Not everything is amazing. |
| Open loop close | /1 | Ends with teaser to another MELBZ page. No dead ends. Ever. |
| Voice check | /1 | Could this article describe any city? If yes = FAIL. Every sentence must only work for Melbourne. |
| Insider value | /1 | At least one tip not on Google (off-menu item, specific timing, local knowledge) |
| Length | /1 | Minimum 750 words. Target 1,200+. Under 750 = auto-fail. |
| **TOTAL** | **/10** | **Must score 7+ to publish** |

## If Below 7:
1. Rewrite the hook (most common failure point)
2. Add specific prices and addresses
3. Add the contrarian/honest-negative section
4. Add an open loop close linking to another page
5. Re-score. Still below 7? Assign to a different persona for full rewrite.

## BANNED PHRASES — Instant -1 Per Occurrence
"hidden gem", "vibrant community", "something for everyone", "nestled in", "boasts", "a plethora of", "in recent years", "foodie paradise", "up-and-coming", "a stone's throw from", "diverse dining options", "this [suburb] institution", "the heart of [suburb]"
