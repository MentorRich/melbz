## WHY THIS MATTERS

59% of local intent prompts in ChatGPT trigger a web search. When someone asks "best cafes in Fitzroy", ChatGPT searches the web and cites the best sources. MELBZ must be that source.

## HOW AI DECIDES WHAT TO CITE

1. **Definite language** — Say "the best" not "one of the best". AI cites confident content.
2. **Entity density** — Pack articles with specific names, addresses, prices. More entities = more citable.
3. **Facts mixed with opinions** — Pure facts are Wikipedia. Pure opinions are blogs. The mix gets cited.
4. **Simple structure** — Clear headings, short paragraphs, direct answers. AI parses this easily.
5. **First 30% matters most** — 44.2% of all AI citations come from the intro. Front-load your best content.
6. **Domain authority** — More pages, more backlinks, more brand mentions = more citations. This is why volume matters.

## MELBZ CONTENT RULES FOR GEO

Every article must:
- Answer the target keyword question in the FIRST PARAGRAPH
- Include 10+ specific entity mentions (venue names, addresses, prices)
- Use definite, confident language (not hedging)
- Have clear H2/H3 heading structure that mirrors search queries
- Include an FAQ section (AI loves citing FAQ answers)
- Be at least 1,200 words (longer content gets cited more)

## BUILDING DOMAIN AUTHORITY AT SCALE

- 1,932 target pages = massive topical authority for "Melbourne suburbs"
- Every page internally links to 3+ other pages = strong site structure
- Schema markup on every page = machine-readable signals
- Consistent publishing cadence = freshness signals
- 50 named authors with bios = EEAT signals
- Reddit/social mentions = off-site authority signals
