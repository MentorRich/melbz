# HEADLINE FORMULAS — Tested, Psychology-Backed

## Every headline must score 3+ out of 5:
- [ ] Contains a SPECIFIC detail (number, suburb, price, venue)
- [ ] Creates a CURIOSITY GAP (makes you need to click)
- [ ] Triggers an EMOTION (pride, outrage, amusement, FOMO)
- [ ] Signals FRESHNESS (2026, this week, just opened, just closed)
- [ ] Speaks to IDENTITY (your suburb, your type of person)

---

## CURIOSITY + SPECIFICITY
- "The $9 Taco in Collingwood That's Making Smith Street Chefs Nervous"
- "14 Cafes in Carlton Where the Barista Will Judge Your Order (Ranked)"
- "This Brunswick Restaurant Has a Secret Menu Item That Costs $4"

## IDENTITY + PROVOCATION
- "If You Think South Yarra Is Overrated, You Haven't Been to These 7 Places"
- "The Definitive Proof That Northcote Has Better Coffee Than Fitzroy"
- "You're Either a Lygon Street Person or a Smith Street Person. Choose."

## FOMO + URGENCY
- "5 Melbourne Spots Closing This Month You Need to Visit Before They're Gone"
- "This Popup in Prahran Only Has 3 Weekends Left"
- "The New Opening in Richmond That Already Has a 2-Hour Wait"

## DATA + SURPRISE
- "Fitzroy's Vibe Score Just Hit 94. Here's Exactly Why."
- "Rent in Footscray Dropped 12% — Is It Finally Footscray's Moment?"

## DEBATE + TRIBAL
- "Melbourne's 10 Most Overrated Restaurants (Fight Us)"
- "Fitzroy vs Collingwood: A Definitive, Completely Biased Comparison"
- "The Suburbs Melbourne Snobs Won't Admit Are Better Than Toorak"

## BANNED HEADLINE PATTERNS
- "Best [X] in Melbourne" (too generic)
- "Everything You Need to Know About [X]" (boring)
- "A Complete Guide to [X]" (no tension)
- "Top [X] [Things] in [Place]" (feels AI-generated)
