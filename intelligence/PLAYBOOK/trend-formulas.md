# TREND-CHASING CONTENT FORMULAS
## 6 Formulas for Turning Discoveries Into Traffic

---

## Formula 1: THE SPIKE RIDER
**When:** A search term is spiking (event, viral moment, news)
**Speed:** Publish within 4 HOURS
**Headline:** "[Trending Topic]: What Melbourne Locals Actually Need to Know"
**Localise:** Turn a national/global trend into a Melbourne suburb story
**Example:** AFL Grand Final trending → "Best Pubs to Watch the Grand Final in [Every Tier 1 Suburb]"

## Formula 2: THE FIRST MOVER
**When:** New venue opens before competitors cover it
**Speed:** Publish within 24 HOURS
**Headline:** "First Look: [Venue] Just Opened in [Suburb] — Here's the Verdict"
**Include:** Prices, what to order, who it's for, honest first impression
**Follow-up:** Add venue to suburb's best-of guide within 48 hours

## Formula 3: THE FOMO BOMB
**When:** Beloved venue closing
**Speed:** Publish within 48 HOURS, BEFORE closing date
**Headline:** "[Venue] Is Closing After [X] Years — Here's Why [Suburb] Will Never Be the Same"
**Emotion:** Nostalgia, loss, urgency. "Go before it's gone."
**Follow-up:** Monthly "RIP" roundup: "Every Melbourne Venue We Lost in [Month] 2026"

## Formula 4: THE SEASONAL LOCK
**When:** Predictable seasonal content (publish 7 days before event)
**Headline:** "[Event/Season] 2026: The Only Guide You Actually Need"
**URL:** Evergreen: /events/[event-slug]/ (update annually, keep SEO)
**Include:** Dates, prices, insider tips, food recs nearby

## Formula 5: THE COMPETITOR COUNTER
**When:** Broadsheet/Urban List publishes city-wide roundup
**Speed:** Publish suburb-specific versions within 24 HOURS
**Example:** Broadsheet: "Best New Restaurants Melbourne" → MELBZ: "Best New Restaurants Collingwood", "Best New Restaurants Carlton", etc.
**Advantage:** We own long-tail suburb searches they're too broad for

## Formula 6: THE REDDIT PULSE
**When:** r/melbourne has a hot post about suburbs/food
**Headline:** Directly address the Reddit discussion with data and authority
**Example:** Reddit: "Is South Yarra overrated?" (500+ upvotes) → MELBZ: "Is South Yarra Overrated? We Ran the Numbers."
**Include:** Vibe Score data, specific evidence, opinionated conclusion
