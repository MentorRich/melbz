## PRIMARY SOURCES (scan every day)

### 1. Google Trends Australia
- URL: https://trends.google.com/trends/trendingsearches/daily?geo=AU
- What to look for: Any Melbourne-related spikes. Food/restaurant/suburb terms rising.
- Action: If a Melbourne term is spiking, check if we have a page for it. If not, create within 4 hours.

### 2. Broadsheet Melbourne
- URL: https://www.broadsheet.com.au/melbourne
- What to look for: New articles about venues, suburbs, food trends.
- Action: Log to competitor-watch.json. Create a deeper suburb-level MELBZ version within 24 hours.

### 3. Urban List Melbourne
- URL: https://www.theurbanlist.com/melbourne
- What to look for: Same as Broadsheet. They publish "best of" lists frequently.
- Action: Same — beat them with suburb depth.

### 4. Sitchu Melbourne
- URL: https://sitchu.com.au/melbourne
- What to look for: Food trend pieces, new opening coverage.
- Action: Log trends to trend-scanner-log.json. Create trend content.

### 5. TimeOut Melbourne
- URL: https://www.timeout.com/melbourne
- What to look for: Events, things to do, weekend guides.
- Action: Create MELBZ versions with suburb-specific angles.

### 6. Reddit r/melbourne
- URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/melbourne/ (sort by Hot)
- What to look for: Top 5 posts. Any about suburbs, food, rent, events, complaints.
- Action: Hot discussions = content goldmines. "Is South Yarra overrated?" → we write the definitive answer.

### 7. Google News "Melbourne"
- Search: "Melbourne restaurant" OR "Melbourne food" OR "Melbourne suburb"
- What to look for: Breaking news, new openings, closures, events, property market.
- Action: Time-sensitive content within 4-24 hours.

### 8. Melbourne City Council Events
- URL: https://whatson.melbourne.vic.gov.au/
- What to look for: Events in the next 7-30 days.
- Action: Preview guides published 7 days before event. Update seasonal-calendar.json.

### 9. Domain.com.au Melbourne
- Search: "melbourne suburb rent" OR "melbourne property market"
- What to look for: Rent changes, median price shifts, new development approvals.
- Action: Suburb-specific property updates. Tom Kelly persona.

### 10. Instagram #melbournefood #melbournecafe
- What to look for: Venues being tagged heavily that we haven't covered.
- Action: If a venue has 50+ tagged posts and no MELBZ mention, it's a gap.

## TREND SIGNAL STRENGTH

When logging a discovery, rate the signal:

- 🔴 CRITICAL — Publish within 4 hours (trending NOW, event TODAY, venue closing THIS WEEK)
- 🟠 HIGH — Publish within 24 hours (competitor just published, new opening, rising keyword)
- 🟡 MEDIUM — Publish within 72 hours (emerging trend, seasonal opportunity approaching)
- 🟢 LOW — Queue for batch generation (evergreen keyword gap, Tier 3/4 suburb content)
