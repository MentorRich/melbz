About This Tool

The MELBZ Suburb Comparison Tool lets you evaluate multiple Melbourne suburbs across the factors that matter most when choosing where to live. Each metric is scored on a consistent scale so you can make meaningful comparisons.

  • Rent Data: Q1 2026 advertised weekly rents across major platforms
  • Walk Score: Walkability based on density of amenities, footpath quality, block length
  • Transit Score: Public transport frequency and coverage
  • Safety: Community perception and reported incident data (1-10 scale)
  • Nightlife: Density of bars, live music, and late-night venues
  • Family Rating: Schools, parks, safety, and family-oriented amenities
  • Cafe Density: Concentration of independent coffee spots
