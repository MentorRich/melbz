The Drinking Scene in Ascot Vale

Ascot Vale sits 6km northwest of the CBD, with Showgrounds station providing the main train access and the Maribyrnong River forming the western boundary. The bar scene here is small but purposeful — a handful of pubs and wine spots along Union Road and the surrounding streets that cater to a mix of young professionals, families, and long-time locals.

Flemington Racecourse is practically next door, and the racecourse crowds spill into Ascot Vale’s bars on race days. Outside of those events, the drinking scene is relaxed and neighbourhood-focused.

Where to Drink in Ascot Vale

Union Road — The main commercial strip has a few bars and pubs alongside the cafes and restaurants. Union Road is walkable and has the most concentration of after-dark options. A couple of wine bars have opened in recent years, adding some sophistication to what was previously a pub-only scene.

The Local Pubs — Ascot Vale’s pubs are genuine locals — the kind of places where the regulars have their own stool and the bartender starts pouring before you order. Some have been renovated with craft beer taps and improved food. Others remain firmly old-school.

Wine Bars — Small, curated wine lists with a few snack options. These spots along or near Union Road are suited to couples and small groups. They tend to close by 10pm on weeknights but stay open later on weekends.

Race Day Overflow — When Flemington Racecourse hosts events (Melbourne Cup, Spring Racing Carnival), Ascot Vale’s bars benefit from the overflow. The atmosphere on those days is noticeably different — busier, louder, and more dressed-up. If you want a quiet drink, avoid race weekends.

Maribyrnong River precinct — The riverside area along the Maribyrnong has a few venues that combine food and drinks with river views. Not strictly Ascot Vale, but within easy walking distance and worth knowing about.

When to Go

After work (5-7pm) — Union Road bars are at their best. Relaxed, uncrowded, locals unwinding.

Friday night — Union Road fills up from 6:30pm. Get in early or have a backup plan. The atmosphere is social and friendly.

Saturday — Peak social time. Bars are full by 8pm. The mix of young professionals and established locals creates a good energy.

Sunday session — Some of Ascot Vale’s pubs do proper Sunday sessions — live music, discounted pots, and a completely relaxed vibe.

What Makes Ascot Vale Different

Ascot Vale is close enough to the city (6km) to draw from the inner-city energy, but far enough out that it keeps its own identity. The bars here serve the people who live here — you will see the same faces weekly. With one-bedroom rents around $370 per week, the crowd includes young professionals priced out of Kensington or Flemington, alongside families and long-term residents.

The Flemington Racecourse connection gives the suburb a social event calendar that most suburbs at this price point do not have.

Tips

  1. Walk Union Road first — get a feel for what is open and what appeals
  2. Race day awareness — check the Flemington calendar if you prefer quieter sessions
  3. Weeknight drinking is underrated — fewer crowds, better service
  4. Ask the bartender — they know what is worth ordering
  5. Walk to the river — the Maribyrnong riverside has a few venues worth exploring after dark

FAQ

What are the best bars near Showgrounds station? Union Road is a short walk from Showgrounds station and has the most bar and pub options. The Maribyrnong River precinct is also walkable from the station.

Is Ascot Vale good for nightlife? Ascot Vale is good for relaxed weeknight drinks and weekend pub sessions. For late-night nightlife, the CBD is about 15 to 20 minutes by train, and nearby Kensington and Flemington offer additional options.

How much does a night out in Ascot Vale cost? A few drinks and a bar snack will run $50 to $80 per person. Wine by the glass costs $14 to $18, beers $9 to $14. Comparable to other inner-northwest suburbs.

Does Ascot Vale get busy on race days? Yes. During the Spring Racing Carnival and Melbourne Cup, the suburb’s bars fill up with Flemington Racecourse overflow. Book ahead or arrive early on those days.
