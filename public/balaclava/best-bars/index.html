Best Bars in Balaclava: Carlisle Street Drinks and Rooftop Sessions

D
Dani Reyes Mar 22, 2026
Balaclava

Balaclava’s bar scene is compact but characterful. The drinking happens mainly along Carlisle Street and in the venues tucked around the station precinct on Hotham Street. It is not St Kilda volume, but the quality-to-pretension ratio is excellent.

1. Carlisle Wine Bar

Carlisle Street, Balaclava

A natural wine bar that has become Carlisle Street’s go-to for a proper glass. The list leans toward minimal-intervention Australian and European producers, with knowledgeable staff who will guide you without condescending. Cheese and charcuterie boards complement the wine. Intimate, low-lit, and perfect for a Tuesday evening.

2. The Local Taphouse (nearby St Kilda East)

184 Carlisle Street, St Kilda East

Technically just over the border into St Kilda East, but every Balaclava local claims it. One of Melbourne’s best craft beer bars with 30+ taps rotating through local and international brews. The rooftop has views toward the city. Tuesday trivia is an institution. Craft beers from $10, good pub food alongside.

3. The Balaclava Hotel

Corner Carlisle and Hotham Streets, Balaclava

The pub that anchors the suburb’s main intersection. Renovated but not gentrified beyond recognition — it still feels like a neighbourhood local. Front bar for a quick pot, beer garden for longer sessions. Live music some weekends. Walking distance from the station.

FAQ

Is Balaclava good for nightlife? It has enough for casual weeknight drinks and weekend sessions. For bigger nights, St Kilda is a short walk or one train stop away. The CBD is 17 minutes by train.

What is the best bar for a date in Balaclava? Carlisle Wine Bar for intimate, considered drinking. The Local Taphouse rooftop for something more social.

Are there late-night bars in Balaclava? Limited options within the suburb itself. The Local Taphouse and some Carlisle Street venues stay open late on weekends. St Kilda nightlife is a 10-minute walk.

The Verdict

Balaclava’s bar scene is three strong options rather than a dozen average ones. Carlisle Wine Bar for natural wine, The Local Taphouse for craft beer depth, and The Balaclava Hotel for a no-frills local. All three sit within walking distance of each other along Carlisle Street and Hotham Street. For the full nightlife picture, see our nightlife guide and best pubs.
