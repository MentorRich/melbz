Late Night Eats in Balaclava: Where to Eat After 10pm

March 18, 2026

As a dedicated follower of the perfect crust and a connoisseur of Italian dining across Melbourne’s suburbs, I’m excited to share my guide to late-night eats in Balaclava. This Melbourne suburb has a lot to offer, even when the sun goes down. Whether you’re a local or just visiting, there’s no need to go hungry after 10 pm. Here’s where to find the best late-night food in Balaclava.

Venue Addresses & Details

  • Las Chicas — 203 Carlisle St, Balaclava VIC 3183 ($$). Open: Mon-Sun until 10:00 PM. Tip: The courtyard is heated, making it a great spot for a late evening drink and snack.
  • Ilona Staller — 281 Carlisle St, Balaclava VIC 3183 ($$). Open: Tue-Sun until 10:30 PM. Tip: The pasta specials are consistently excellent for a late dinner.
  • Batch Espresso — 323 Carlisle St, Balaclava VIC 3183 ($). Open: Mon-Sun until 9:00 PM. Tip: Great for a quick, high-quality bite if you are catching a late train at Balaclava station.
  • Babu Ji — 4-6 Grey St, St Kilda VIC 3182 ($$). Open: Mon-Sun until 10:00 PM. Tip: Located just on the border of Balaclava/St Kilda, it is the go-to for late-night spice cravings.
  • Monarch Cakes — 103 Acland St, St Kilda VIC 3182 ($). Open: Mon-Sun until 9:30 PM. Tip: Grab a slice of their famous Polish baked cheesecake for a late-night treat.

Top Late-Night Eats in Balaclava

1. Tipo 00

123 Smith Street, Melbourne VIC 3000 Phone: (03) 9015 3292

Tipo 00 is a popular spot for Italian food, and for good reason. Their kitchen is open late on weekends, serving up delicious pasta dishes, pizzas, and more. Try their famous Tipo 00 pizza, made with high-quality ingredients and a perfectly cooked crust.

2. Giovanni’s Italian Restaurant

232-234 Barkly Street, Balaclava VIC 3182 Phone: (03) 9529 3133

Giovanni’s Italian Restaurant is a local favorite, serving up traditional Italian dishes with a modern twist. Their kitchen is open late on weekends, making it a great spot for a late-night meal.

3. Balaclava Bakery

76-78 Barkly Street, Balaclava VIC 3182 Phone: (03) 9529 1100

Balaclava Bakery is a great spot for a late-night snack or light meal. They offer a range of freshly baked goods, including bread, pastries, and sandwiches.

The Melbourne Pantry is a popular spot for breakfast and brunch, but they also serve a great dinner menu late into the night. Try their seasonal dishes, made with fresh, local ingredients.
