By Location Within Balaclava

Carlisle Street frontage: Slight premium for walkability. One-beds $420-$450. Hotham Street (near station): Most in-demand. Two-beds $500-$560. Inkerman Street area: Quieter, slightly cheaper. One-beds $380-$420. Brighton Road edge: Less foot traffic, more space. Two-beds $480-$530.

FAQ

Why is Balaclava cheaper than St Kilda? Less nightlife, less beachfront premium, smaller commercial strip. The trade-off is genuine value for money with better train access.

What is the best value in Balaclava? Period one-bedroom apartments near the station on Hotham Street. Solid buildings, $380-$420/week, walking distance to the train.

The Verdict

Balaclava offers one of the best rent-to-transport ratios in the inner south-east. You pay less than St Kilda and get direct train access. The period apartment stock provides affordable entry. Carlisle Street dining keeps food costs manageable. For renters comparing inner south-east options, Balaclava’s numbers make a strong case.
