Best Bars in Brunswick East 2026: Lygon Street's After-Dark Scene

K
Kai Thompson Mar 22, 2026
Brunswick-East

Brunswick East’s bar scene punches above its weight. While Brunswick has the volume and Fitzroy has the reputation, this suburb’s Lygon Street strip has assembled a collection of bars that range from warehouse beer gardens to intimate wine spots — all without the crowds or the attitude.

Here are six bars worth knowing about.

1. Howler

The vibe: Converted warehouse with a beer garden, live music venue, and bar. Brunswick East’s cultural anchor.

Howler is the venue that put Brunswick East’s evening scene on the map. The warehouse space hosts live music several nights a week, the beer garden is one of the best in the inner north (heated in winter, leafy in summer), and the bar serves craft beers and cocktails without the markup you’d pay in Fitzroy.

Order this: A local craft pale ale ($10) and whatever band is playing Address: 7-11 Dawson Street, Brunswick East VIC 3057 Hours: Wed-Sun from 4pm (check for gig times)

2. Lomond Hotel

The vibe: Restored 1880s pub that’s evolved into one of Brunswick East’s best all-round venues.

The Lomond has been operating since 1888, and the current iteration is its best yet. Craft taps rotate regularly, the wine list has depth, and the beer garden catches afternoon sun. The food is proper pub fare elevated — think gourmet parmas and seasonal share plates.

Order this: Whatever’s on the craft tap ($9-$11) and the parma ($24) Address: 223 Nicholson Street, Brunswick East VIC 3057 Hours: Mon-Thu 12pm-11pm, Fri-Sat 12pm-1am, Sun 12pm-10pm

3. Le Clec

The vibe: Lebanese-inspired wine bar on Lygon Street. Natural wines, mezze, and a vibe that makes you stay longer than planned.

Le Clec brings something different to the strip — a curated natural wine list paired with Lebanese-inspired small plates. The space is intimate and warm, the staff know their wines, and the mezze sharing plates ($14-$18 each) are genuinely excellent.

Order this: A glass of natural orange wine and the mezze board for two ($35) Address: Lygon Street, Brunswick East VIC 3057 Hours: Wed-Sun from 5pm

4. Retreat Hotel

The vibe: Classic neighbourhood pub with a massive beer garden and live music on weekends.

The Retreat is a Brunswick East institution — the kind of pub where regulars have their usual seat and the barman starts pouring before you order. The beer garden is huge and the live music on weekends draws crowds from across the inner north.

Order this: A pot of the house lager ($6) and a spot in the beer garden Address: 280 Sydney Road (border of Brunswick/Brunswick East) Hours: Daily from 12pm

5. Two Pot Screamer

The vibe: Dive bar with character. Mario Kart, cheap drinks, and zero pretension.

Two Pot Screamer is the kind of bar that doesn’t exist in newer suburbs. It’s small, a bit rough around the edges, and has a Mario Kart setup that’s become legendary in the inner north. Drinks are cheap, the crowd is friendly, and nobody’s taking flash photography of their cocktail.

Order this: Whatever’s cheapest on the board Address: Lygon Street, Brunswick East VIC 3057 Hours: Daily from 4pm

6. Mr Mister

The vibe: Small cocktail bar with considered drinks and a moody atmosphere.

Mr Mister does cocktails properly — tight menu, skilled bartenders, quality spirits. The space is small (maybe 30 seats) which means it feels intimate rather than empty. Good for dates and small groups who want to drink well.

Order this: An Espresso Martini ($20) or whatever the bartender recommends Address: Lygon Street, Brunswick East VIC 3057 Hours: Thu-Sat from 5pm

FAQ

Does Brunswick East have good nightlife? Yes — better than most people realise. Howler provides live music, the Lomond is an excellent pub, and the bar strip on Lygon Street has genuine variety.

What’s the latest bars stay open? Howler and Lomond Hotel run until 1am on weekends. Most other bars close by 11pm-midnight.

How does Brunswick East compare to Brunswick for bars? Brunswick has more bars and more variety. Brunswick East has a smaller, more curated scene with less crowding and lower prices.

The Verdict

Brunswick East’s bar scene works because it’s local-first. Howler is the standout for live music and atmosphere. The Lomond Hotel is one of Melbourne’s best restored heritage pubs. And the smaller bars on Lygon Street — Le Clec, Two Pot Screamer, Mr Mister — each bring something different without trying to compete with Fitzroy. For a great night that doesn’t require battling crowds, Brunswick East delivers.
