Best Pubs in Brunswick — 2026 Local Guide

K
Kai Chen Mar 22, 2026
Brunswick

Brunswick’s pub scene has survived everything Melbourne has thrown at it: lockout laws, COVID, gentrification, and the slow realisation that a pint now costs more than a coffee used to. Through it all, Sydney Road has kept its pub identity intact — a strip of drinking institutions where the beer garden is king, the parma is sacred, and live music still matters more than the cocktail menu.

This is not a list of bars pretending to be pubs. These are seven proper Brunswick pubs — the ones where you can watch the footy, eat a $15 meal, and stumble home on the Upfield line without feeling like you have overcommitted.

1. The Retreat Hotel — The Undisputed King

The vibe: Brunswick’s most iconic pub, where the live music is legendary, the beer garden is enormous, and the reputation extends well beyond the suburb’s borders.

The Retreat Hotel on Sydney Road has been a Brunswick institution for longer than most of its current patrons have been alive. The back room hosts live music almost every night of the week — from punk to jazz to everything in between — and the front bar is the kind of place where strangers become friends over a shared table and a $7 pint. The food is solid pub grub at honest prices, and the crowd is a perfect Brunswick cross-section: students, families, tradies, and people who have been coming here since the 90s.

Order this: The parma ($19 on parma nights) — thick, crispy, and it comes with chips and salad. The tap list leans local with plenty of Victorian craft options alongside the essentials.

Address: 276 Sydney Road, Brunswick Hours: Daily, 11am-late Insider tip: Monday night is jazz. Wednesday is the popular parma deal. The back room fills fast for headline acts — check their program online and book if it is a band you know. The Retreat is a two-minute walk from Anstey station on the Upfield line, making it the easiest pub to reach by train.

2. The Cornish Arms — The Vegan Pub That Converted Everyone

The vibe: A sunny, welcoming pub with one of Melbourne’s best vegan menus and a rooftop bar that catches the afternoon sun perfectly.

The Cornish Arms is the pub that proved “vegan pub food” does not have to mean a sad salad while your mates eat parmas. The entire menu is plant-based, and it is genuinely excellent — burgers that hold together, wings with actual crunch, and enough variety that the meat-eaters at the table stop complaining after the first bite. The rooftop bar is one of Brunswick’s best outdoor drinking spots, with views up and down Sydney Road.

Order this: The buffalo cauliflower wings ($16) — crispy, spicy, and the dish that converted the skeptics. Or the classic burger ($18) with house-made pickles and cashew aioli.

Address: 223 Sydney Road, Brunswick Hours: Daily, noon-late Insider tip: The rooftop fills fast on warm evenings — arrive by 5pm on Fridays for a seat. Wednesday night’s $15 vegan parma deal is genuinely one of the best value meals in Brunswick.

3. The Bergy Seltzer — The Live Music Engine

The vibe: A small, loud, gloriously chaotic pub where the music never stops and the crowd never pretends to be anything they are not.

The Bergy Seltzer on Sydney Road is not a quiet pint kind of pub. It is a pub where the band starts at 9pm, the dance floor fills by 10pm, and you have somehow made three new friends and lost your jacket by midnight. The beer selection is excellent, the kitchen runs late, and the Monday comedy night has a cult following that some people travel across Melbourne for.

Order this: Whatever is on tap — the selection rotates through Victorian craft breweries and the staff know their stuff. The $10 Wednesday wing deal is legendary.

Address: 333 Sydney Road, Brunswick Hours: Daily, 4pm-3am (kitchen until midnight) Insider tip: Monday comedy is free and starts around 8pm — arrive early for a seat. The smart move is to make the Bergy your first stop of the night: arrive at 7pm, get a table, order wings, and let the night build around you.

4. Brunswick Green — The Cosy Back-Street Winner

The vibe: A tucked-away pub on the corner of Sydney Road and Brunswick Road that is cosier than the big names but just as good.

Brunswick Green does not have the reputation of The Retreat or the rooftop of the Cornish Arms, and that is exactly why the regulars love it. It is a proper neighbourhood pub: good taps, decent food, a fire in winter, and a crowd that actually lives in the area. The beer garden is compact but comfortable, and the live music schedule is strong without overwhelming the bar.

Order this: A pot of whatever local craft is on special (usually $6-8) and the chicken schnitzel ($17) — reliable, under twenty bucks, and comes with the right amount of chips.

Address: Corner of Sydney Road and Brunswick Road, Brunswick Hours: Daily, 11am-late Insider tip: The fire in winter makes Brunswick Green one of the cosiest spots in the inner north. It is the best “first pub” of the night — low-key enough for a weeknight drink, good enough to keep you there.

5. Union Hotel Brunswick — The Newcomer That Earned Its Place

The vibe: A freshly renovated pub on Sydney Road that manages to feel modern without losing its neighbourhood soul.

The Union Hotel has had a few incarnations over the years, but the current version has found its groove. The food menu is a step above standard pub fare — think smoked meats, house-made slaws, and a Sunday roast that sells out regularly. The beer garden is generous, the staff are fast, and the vibe is young-professional-meets-long-term-local in a way that works.

Order this: The Sunday roast ($22) — it is the best pub roast in Brunswick and only available on Sundays. The house-smoked beef brisket ($19) is the weekday winner.

Address: Sydney Road, Brunswick Hours: Daily, 11am-late Insider tip: Slightly further north on Sydney Road than most pub guides cover, which means less tourist-heavy and more local. A natural halfway point if you are heading toward Coburg for dinner afterwards.

6. The Brunswick Hotel — The Strip’s Workhorse

The vibe: A classic Sydney Road pub that does everything well and nothing fancy.

The Brunswick Hotel is the reliable option on a strip full of characters. It does not have the live music reputation of The Retreat or the vegan credentials of the Cornish Arms. What it has is a solid tap list, a decent bistro, and the kind of no-drama atmosphere that makes it the right choice for a Tuesday night pint when you do not want to think too hard about where you are drinking.

Order this: The burger and chips ($16) — straightforward and well-made. A pot of Melbourne Bitter ($6) if you want to keep it traditional.

Address: Sydney Road, Brunswick Hours: Daily, 11am-late Insider tip: Two minutes from Brunswick station on the Upfield line — no excuses for being late. The best “I just need a quiet pint” pub on the strip.

7. Waxflower — The Hidden Bar-Pub Hybrid

The vibe: A bar-pub hybrid tucked off Sydney Road that combines outdoor seating, quality drinks, and a vibe that is more “mate’s backyard” than “licensed venue.”

Waxflower is not technically a pub in the traditional sense — it does not have a TAB or a pokies room. But it fills the pub role perfectly: a place to sit outside with friends, drink something good, eat something decent, and not feel like you are in a venue trying too hard. The outdoor area has heaters for Melbourne’s unpredictable weather, and the drink list leans towards natural wine and craft beer.

Order this: Whatever natural wine is on special — the list rotates and the staff recommendations are always on point. The snack menu is tight but well-executed.

Address: Off Sydney Road, Brunswick (side street entrance) Hours: Wed-Mon, 4pm-late Insider tip: Waxflower is the best “second venue” of the night — the kind of place you end up after The Bergy or The Retreat when you are not ready to go home but do not want another loud pub. Also a solid starting point for a Saturday afternoon session before hitting the Sydney Road strip.

The Pub Crawl That Actually Works

For the ambitious: a one-night Brunswick pub crawl that will not bankrupt you.

  1. 6pm — Start at Brunswick Green for a quiet first pint ($7)
  2. 7:30pm — Union Hotel for a brisket roll and a craft tap ($19 + $8)
  3. 9pm — The Retreat for live music and a parma ($19)
  4. 10:30pm — The Bergy Seltzer for wings and whatever the band is playing ($10)
  5. Midnight — Cornish Arms rooftop if it is warm; Brunswick Green fire if it is not

Total spend per person: roughly $80-100. Brunswick does affordable nights out better than anywhere else in the inner north.

FAQ

What is the best pub in Brunswick? The Retreat Hotel at 276 Sydney Road. Live music almost every night, enormous beer garden, and the parma deal on Wednesdays.

Is there a vegan pub in Brunswick? Yes. The Cornish Arms at 223 Sydney Road has a fully plant-based menu. The buffalo cauliflower wings and the $15 vegan parma on Wednesdays are standouts.

Which Brunswick pub has the best live music? The Retreat Hotel for range and reputation. The Bergy Seltzer for raw energy and late-night sets. Brunswick Ballroom for ticketed headline acts in a heritage venue. See our bars guide for more.

Can you get to Brunswick pubs by train? Yes. The Upfield line has three stations in Brunswick — Jewell, Brunswick, and Anstey. The Retreat is two minutes from Anstey station. The Brunswick Hotel is two minutes from Brunswick station.

Verdict

Brunswick’s pub scene in 2026 is as strong as it has ever been. The old guard (The Retreat, Cornish Arms) have not lost a step, the smaller players (Brunswick Green, Waxflower) offer something for every mood, and the newer arrivals (Union Hotel) prove the strip still has room for fresh energy.

The Sydney Road pub strip between Brunswick Road and Glenlyon Road remains the sweet spot — seven quality pubs within a ten-minute walk. That is a density of good drinking that most Melbourne suburbs cannot match.
