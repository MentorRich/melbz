Late Night Eats in Collingwood: Where to Eat After 10pm

As a pizza and Italian writer, I’m always on the lookout for the best late-night eats in Melbourne’s suburbs. Collingwood, with its rich history and cultural significance, is a suburb that comes alive at night. Whether you’re a local or just visiting, there’s no shortage of delicious options to satisfy your late-night cravings. In this article, we’ll explore the best places to eat after 10 pm in Collingwood.

Venue Addresses & Details

  • Easey’s — 3/48 Easey St, Collingwood VIC 3066 ($$). Open: Fri-Sat until 11:00 PM. Tip: Book a seat in the train carriage for the quintessential Collingwood experience.
  • Le Bon Ton — 51 Gipps St, Collingwood VIC 3066 ($$$). Open: Fri-Sat until 1:00 AM. Tip: The late-night fried chicken and bourbon selection are legendary.
  • Shop Ramen — 329 Smith St, Collingwood VIC 3066 ($$). Open: Daily until 10:00 PM. Tip: Try the peanut ramen; it’s a local cult favorite.
  • Lazerpig — 9-11 Peel St, Collingwood VIC 3066 ($$). Open: Fri-Sat until 12:00 AM. Tip: The ‘Pork Lover’ pizza is the go-to, and the cocktails are surprisingly sophisticated.
  • Bad Frankie — 141 Greeves St, Fitzroy/Collingwood border VIC 3065 ($$). Open: Fri-Sat until 1:00 AM. Tip: Order the sweet jaffles for a late-night dessert fix.
  • Naked for Satan — 285 Brunswick St, Fitzroy/Collingwood border VIC 3065 ($$). Open: Fri-Sat until 1:00 AM. Tip: Head straight to the rooftop for the best views of the Melbourne skyline.

Top Late-Night Eats in Collingwood

1. Pizzeria Italiana

Address: 145 Smith Street, Collingwood VIC 3068 Pizzeria Italiana is a Collingwood institution, serving up delicious Neapolitan-style pizzas late into the night. Their menu features a range of traditional pizzas, including the classic Margherita and the more adventurous Prosciutto e Funghi. With its cozy atmosphere and friendly service, Pizzeria Italiana is a must-visit for any pizza lover.

2. La Taqueria

Address: 211 Smith Street, Collingwood VIC 3068 La Taqueria is a popular spot for Mexican-inspired food, and they’re open late to satisfy your cravings. Their menu features a range of tacos, burritos, and other Mexican favorites, all made with fresh and delicious ingredients. Whether you’re in the mood for something spicy or something mild, La Taqueria has got you covered.

3. The Provincial Hotel

Address: 230 Johnston Street, Collingwood VIC 3068 The Provincial Hotel is a Collingwood landmark, offering a range of food and drink options late into the night. Their menu features a range of pub-style favorites, including burgers, sandwiches, and pizzas. With its lively atmosphere and friendly service, The Provincial Hotel is a great spot to grab a bite to eat after 10 pm.

4. Mamak

Address: 316-318 Smith Street, Collingwood VIC 3068 Mamak is a popular spot for Malaysian cuisine, and they’re open late to satisfy your cravings. Their menu features a range of traditional Malaysian dishes, including noodles, curries, and rice bowls. With its cozy atmosphere and friendly service, Mamak is a great spot to try something new and exciting.

5. The Collingwood Children’s Farm Cafe

Address: 69-71 Johnston Street, Collingwood VIC 3068 The Collingwood Children’s Farm Cafe is a great spot for a late-night breakfast or brunch. Their menu features a range of delicious breakfast favorites, including eggs, pancakes, and sandwiches. With its cozy atmosphere and friendly service, The Collingwood Children’s Farm Cafe is a great spot to start your day or satisfy your late-night cravings.

6. Grampians Hotel

Address: 124-126 Gertrude Street, Fitzroy VIC 3065 (just a short walk from Collingwood) The Grampians Hotel is a popular spot for food and drink, and they’re open late to satisfy your cravings. Their menu features a range of pub-style favorites, including burgers, sandwiches, and pizzas. With its lively atmosphere and friendly service, The Grampians Hotel is a great spot to grab a bite to eat after 10 pm.

7. Papa’s Boy

Address: 395-397 Smith Street, Collingwood VIC 3068 Papa’s Boy is a popular spot for burgers and other American-inspired food, and they’re open late to satisfy your cravings. Their menu features a range of delicious burgers, sandwiches, and salads, all made with fresh and high-quality ingredients. With its cozy atmosphere and friendly service, Papa’s Boy is a great spot to grab a bite to eat after 10 pm.
