Rent Prices in Collingwood 2026

March 18, 2026

As Melbourne’s inner-city suburbs continue to evolve, Collingwood remains a sought-after destination for renters. With its rich history, cultural attractions, and proximity to the city, it’s no wonder that many are looking to call Collingwood home. In this article, we’ll explore the current state of rent prices in Collingwood, what you can expect to pay, and some of the top venues to enjoy in the area.

Understanding Rent Prices in Collingwood

The median rent price for Collingwood is around $650-$750 per week for a one-bedroom apartment, according to recent data. For a two-bedroom apartment, you can expect to pay around $800-$1,000 per week. These prices are reflective of the suburb’s popularity and its proximity to Melbourne’s CBD.

Lifestyle and Amenities

Collingwood offers a unique blend of urban living and community spirit. With its proximity to the city, residents can easily access Melbourne’s top attractions, including the National Gallery of Victoria and the Melbourne Museum. The suburb is also home to a range of parks and gardens, including Birrarung Marr and Yarra River.
