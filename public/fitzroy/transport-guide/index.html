Transport in Fitzroy 2026: Trains, Trams & the Commute Reality

Jack Reeves

Hidden Melbourne Editor • Mar 21, 2026 • 2 min read

Fitzroy

Fitzroy’s transport game scored A in our latest review. Here’s what that actually means for your daily commute and weekend plans.

Getting to the CBD

From Fitzroy to the CBD: 10-20 minutes by train or tram. Services run every 5-10 minutes during peak. This is one of the best-connected suburbs in Melbourne.

The Tram & Train Routes

Check PTV.vic.gov.au for current timetables. The main routes serving Fitzroy connect to the CBD loop and key interchange stations.

Cycling

Fitzroy is genuinely cycleable. Protected lanes on key routes, flat terrain, and under 10km to the CBD. Bike parking is decent at stations.

Driving & Parking

Parking in Fitzroy is competitive. Street parking requires permits in most residential areas. If you’re renting, check whether your building has a car space — they’re not guaranteed.

The Verdict

Fitzroy gets a A for transport because it has excellent multi-modal options and fast CBD access. That’s a serious advantage — transport is one of the main reasons people choose this suburb.
