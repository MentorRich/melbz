Footscray Transport Guide 2026: Trains, Trams and Getting Around

J
Jack Morrison Mar 22, 2026
Footscray

Footscray Transport Overview

Footscray is one of Melbourne’s best-connected suburbs for public transport. Footscray Station is a major interchange hub, not just a local stop, and tram route 82 adds another layer of connectivity along the main street. If you’re choosing a suburb based on how easily you can get to work, Footscray should be near the top of your list.

Trains: Footscray Station

Footscray Station is not a minor suburban stop. It’s a major interchange where six train lines converge:

  • Werribee line
  • Williamstown line
  • Sunbury line
  • Bendigo line (V/Line regional)
  • Ballarat line (V/Line regional)
  • Geelong line (V/Line regional)

This means you can get to Southern Cross Station (CBD) in about 8–12 minutes on a Metro train during peak hours. Services run every 5–10 minutes during peak and every 10–20 minutes off-peak. The regional V/Line services also stop at Footscray, which is useful if you commute to Geelong, Ballarat, or Bendigo — you can board here instead of fighting through Southern Cross.

The station sits at the junction of Hopkins Street and Irving Street, within easy walking distance of Footscray Market and the main commercial strips.

Tram: Route 82

Tram route 82 runs along Barkly Street, connecting Footscray to Moonee Ponds via Maribyrnong. The tram runs every 8–12 minutes during peak and every 15–20 minutes off-peak. It’s useful for reaching Highpoint Shopping Centre, Maribyrnong, and Moonee Ponds without a car.

The tram stops along Barkly Street put you within walking distance of most of the suburb’s restaurants, bars, and shops. If you live on or near Barkly Street, the tram is often more convenient than walking to the station.

Getting to the CBD

From Footscray to the CBD by train: 8–12 minutes to Southern Cross, 12–18 minutes to Flinders Street (depending on the line and time of day). During peak hours, you will get a seat most mornings if you board at Footscray, since many services originate further out on the line.

By tram, the route 82 connects to Moonee Ponds, not the CBD directly, so train is the better option for city-bound commutes.

By bike, the ride to the CBD is roughly 5km via Dynon Road or the Maribyrnong River trail connecting to the Capital City Trail. Allow 15–25 minutes depending on your route and speed.

Cycling

Footscray’s flat terrain makes cycling practical for daily commuting. The Maribyrnong River trail provides a car-free route along the river, linking to broader cycling networks heading north and south. On-road, Barkly Street and Hopkins Street have mixed traffic, so experienced commuters will be more comfortable than beginners. Nicholson Street and the quieter residential streets south of Barkly are more relaxed cycling routes.

Bike parking is available at Footscray Station and near Footscray Market.

Driving and Parking

Driving from Footscray to the CBD takes 10–20 minutes outside peak, but can blow out to 30–40 minutes during morning and evening rush on the West Gate Freeway or Dynon Road approaches.

Parking in the residential streets is generally fine — most houses have driveways or garages. Street parking near Barkly Street, Hopkins Street, and around Footscray Station is time-limited during business hours. On weekends, the area around Footscray Market fills up by mid-morning, so arrive early or walk from a side street.

Buses

Several bus routes run through Footscray, connecting to suburbs not well-served by train or tram. Routes run to Highpoint, Sunshine, Essendon, and various western suburbs. Frequency varies — some routes run every 15 minutes during peak, others every 30–40 minutes. Check PTV for current timetables.

Getting to Neighbouring Suburbs

  • Seddon — one train stop south on the Werribee or Williamstown line, or a 10-minute walk along Hopkins Street
  • Yarraville — one train stop south, or 15-minute walk
  • West Footscray — one train stop west on the Sunbury line
  • Moonee Ponds — tram 82 along Barkly Street, about 20 minutes
  • Maribyrnong — tram 82 or a short bus ride

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the train from Footscray to the CBD? About 8–12 minutes to Southern Cross Station during peak hours. Trains run every 5–10 minutes on most lines.

Does Footscray have a tram? Yes. Tram route 82 runs along Barkly Street, connecting Footscray to Moonee Ponds via Maribyrnong.

Can I cycle from Footscray to the city? Yes. The ride is about 5km and takes 15–25 minutes. The Maribyrnong River trail and Dynon Road are the main routes.

Is parking difficult in Footscray? In residential streets, parking is generally fine. Near the commercial strips and Footscray Market, it gets tight on weekends. Time limits apply on Barkly Street and Hopkins Street during business hours.

What V/Line trains stop at Footscray? Bendigo, Ballarat, and Geelong V/Line services all stop at Footscray Station, making it a convenient boarding point for regional travel.

Verdict

Footscray’s transport connections are a genuine advantage for anyone living here. The combination of a major train interchange, tram route 82, cycling-friendly terrain, and short CBD commute times puts it ahead of most Melbourne suburbs for getting around without a car. If you work in the CBD or need regular access to regional Victoria, Footscray Station makes daily life significantly easier.
