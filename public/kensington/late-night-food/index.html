Late Night Food in Kensington 2026: Where to Eat After Dark

Late Night Food in Kensington 2026: Where to Eat After Dark

Updated 16 March 2026 | 6 places tested | Ethan Cross reporting

Kensington doesn’t shout about its food scene. That’s partly the point — this is a suburb that does things at its own pace, anchored by Racecourse Road, Macaulay Road, and a handful of side streets where you’ll find Ethiopian stews, hand-pressed corn tortillas, and Turkish grills all within a 10-minute walk. But when the sun drops and the trains thin out, the options get narrower fast.

We walked every strip, ate at every spot that stays open past 9pm, and ranked what’s worth your time after dark. Here’s what actually delivered.

1. Flemington Kebab House

Address: 301 Racecourse Road, Kensington VIC 3031 Hours: Tuesday–Sunday, 11am–midnight. Closed Mondays. Price range: $8–$18 per person Cuisine: Turkish / Halal

This is the reliable 11pm option that every Kensington local already knows about. Flemington Kebab House has been turning out charcoal-grilled meats on Racecourse Road for years, and it’s still the spot most likely to have its lights on when everything else has gone dark.

The halal snack pack — chips, meat, cheese, and sauce in a styrofoam coffin — is the main event. A large HSP runs about $15 and will absolutely wreck your sleep schedule in the best way. The mixed grill plate is a better call if you want something slightly more composed: lamb chops, chicken skewers, kofta, rice, and salad for around $18. Kebab wraps sit at the $10–$12 mark.

It’s takeaway only, the queue moves fast, and the staff have the process down to a science. If you’re coming from the Flemington Bridge side of the suburb, it’s a five-minute walk from the station.

Verdict: The dependable backbone of Kensington’s late-night food. No pretensions, no frills, open when you need it.

2. Sahara Restaurant

Address: 465 Macaulay Road, Kensington VIC 3031 Hours: Sunday–Tuesday & Thursday–Saturday, 1pm–11pm. Closed Wednesdays. Price range: $15–$35 per person Cuisine: East African / Middle Eastern

Sahara has been on Macaulay Road since the early 2000s, serving East African and Mediterranean food in a low-lit, unfussy dining room. It’s one of the only sit-down restaurants in Kensington that takes orders until 10:30pm, which makes it a genuine option for dinner rather than just a post-pub feed.

The slow-cooked goat is the standout — fall-apart tender, seasoned with berberé spice, and served with injera and a side of lentils. It runs about $25 for a generous plate. The Nile perch, sautéed in spiced ghee, is another strong pick at roughly $28. For something lighter, the sambusas ($6) are crispy, well-filled, and arrive fast.

The restaurant is fully halal. Portions are generous, and you won’t leave hungry. It’s the kind of place that rewards you for going in with an open mind and a willingness to try something off the beaten track.

Verdict: Kensington’s most underrated late-night dining room. The goat alone is worth the trip.

3. La Tortilleria

Address: 72 Stubbs Street, Kensington VIC 3031 Hours: Monday–Thursday 5:30pm–9pm | Friday–Saturday 12pm–9:30pm | Sunday 12pm–9pm Price range: $12–$25 per person Cuisine: Mexican street food

La Tortilleria makes its corn tortillas from scratch. You can see the press working in the front of the kitchen, and you can taste the difference immediately — these aren’t the rubbery supermarket discs you’re used to. They’re warm, slightly sweet, and hold together even under aggressive salsa application.

On Friday and Saturday nights, this is one of the busiest spots in Kensington. The tacos are the main draw: chipotle chicken ($6 each), birria beef ($7), and a rotating seasonal option. The quesadillas are excellent value at around $10, and the tostadas with fresh ceviche are worth ordering as a starter if you’re sharing.

Everything on the menu is gluten-free, which is unusual for a Mexican restaurant and makes this a solid option for groups with dietary requirements. The space is small and colourful, with communal seating that forces you to squeeze in shoulder-to-shoulder with strangers. Part of the charm.

Verdict: The best Mexican in Kensington, hands down. Go on a Friday and arrive before 7pm unless you enjoy queues.

4. Arnold’s Wine Bar

Address: 192 Bellair Street, Kensington VIC 3031 Hours: Thursday 5pm–10pm | Friday 5pm–11pm | Saturday 12pm–11pm | Sunday 12pm–6pm. Closed Mon–Wed. Price range: $18–$40 per person Cuisine: Mexican-inspired wine bar

Arnold’s opened in late 2024 and quickly became the neighbourhood spot Kensington didn’t know it was missing. Run by husband-and-wife team Scott Eddington and Lauren Chibert — both Kensington locals — it sits on Bellair Street opposite the train station, tucked behind a cheery yellow facade.

The food is Mexican-ish, drawing on head chef Eddington’s time at Mamasita. Think tostadas with seasonal toppings, mole-drenched chicken, and small plates designed to pair with the natural wine list. A full feed for two with a couple of glasses of wine will land around $80–$100.

On Friday and Saturday nights, the kitchen runs until 11pm. The disco ball comes on. The wine pours get generous. It’s not a late-night restaurant in the traditional sense — it’s more of a slow-burn evening that stretches later than you planned. The vibe is relaxed, the crowd is local, and nobody’s in a hurry.

Verdict: The best option if you want a proper sit-down meal with good wine on a Friday or Saturday night. Book ahead — the room only holds about 30 people.

5. Hardimans Hotel

Address: 521 Macaulay Road, Kensington VIC 3031 Hours: Monday–Thursday 12pm–11pm | Friday–Saturday 12pm–1am | Sunday 12pm–11pm Price range: $15–$30 per person Cuisine: Pub food / Modern Australian

Hardimans is one of Melbourne’s most historic pubs, and it’s been feeding Kensington since long before the suburb started getting write-ups in food magazines. The kitchen turns out solid pub classics — parmas, steaks, seasonal pizzas, and share plates — with a focus on doing the basics well rather than chasing trends.

Friday and Saturday nights are when Hardimans earns its late-night stripes, staying open until 1am with full food service. The pizza menu is the move after 10pm: thin-crust numbers with proper toppings, roughly $20–$24, that hold up well against anything in the inner west. The $20 parma special on Tuesdays is one of the best value meals in the postcode.

The beer list leans local, with rotating craft taps alongside the usual suspects. It’s a proper pub — no exposed brick pretension, no cocktail list that reads like a novel. Just good food, cold drinks, and a bistro that stays open when you need it.

Verdict: The late-night pub option. If Hardimans is open and you want a sit-down meal with a beer, this is your spot.

6. Auntie Annie’s Hotel

Address: 271 Racecourse Road, Kensington VIC 3031 Hours: Thursday–Sunday evenings (check website for seasonal hours) Price range: $16–$30 per person Cuisine: Irish pub / Australian classics

Auntie Annie’s took over the old Quiet Man Irish Pub site in September 2025, after the beloved original closed on St Patrick’s Day that year. The new owners — Zengal Hospitality Group — kept the bones of the building (which was originally fabricated in Dublin and shipped to Melbourne in 1998) and gave it a fresh fitout.

The menu blends Irish staples with Australian pub classics: bangers and mash, beef and Guinness pie, fish and chips, and a Sunday roast that draws a crowd. Weekly specials keep things interesting — steak night on Wednesdays, parma night on Thursdays, and a proper Sunday roast from noon. Prices are pub-standard, with most mains sitting at $20–$28.

Live music runs Thursday through weekends, and the kitchen stays open to match. It’s still early days for Auntie Annie’s, but the neighbourhood has already warmed to having the old venue back. Worth watching as it finds its feet.

Verdict: The spiritual successor to The Quiet Man. Still settling in, but the bones are right and the food is solid.

What We Skipped and Why

Domino’s / Pizza Hut delivery. They deliver to Kensington, sure. But if you’re ordering chain pizza at 11pm, this article can’t help you. You already know what you’re doing and it’s not reading food guides.

Any venue that closes before 9pm. Kensington has several excellent daytime spots — The Premises on Bellair Street, various cafes along Macaulay Road — but they all wrap up by mid-afternoon. This guide is specifically about after dark. For daytime recommendations, check our broader Kensington food guide.

CBD late-night spots. The CBD is a 15-minute train ride and has its own deep bench of late-night dining — from Supper Inn’s Cantonese congee at 1am to the Korean BBQ joints on Little Lonsdale. But that’s a different suburb and a different article. We’ll cover CBD late-night eating in its own piece. If you want to start exploring now, our CBD late-night food guide is worth a read.

Getting There and Getting Home

Kensington station sits on the Craigieburn line, roughly 12 minutes from Flinders Street. Trains run until about 12:30am on weeknights and slightly later on Friday and Saturday. If you’re coming from the city, getting home by public transport after midnight is tight — plan accordingly.

Taxis and rideshare from the CBD run about $15–$20 and take 10–15 minutes depending on traffic. The ride home on a Saturday night from Racecourse Road is usually smooth — Kensington isn’t the kind of suburb that generates surge pricing.

The Honest Take

Kensington isn’t going to win any awards for the sheer volume of late-night dining options. You won’t find midnight dumpling houses or 24-hour diners here. What you will find is a handful of genuinely good spots — each doing one thing well — that happen to stay open past the time most of Melbourne’s kitchens are scrubbing down the benches.

The kebab house for quick takeaway. Sahara for a proper sit-down. La Tortilleria when you want the real deal on a Friday. Arnold’s when you want to make a night of it. Hardimans when you want a pub meal and a cold beer. Auntie Annie’s when you want live music with your pie and mash.

Six spots. No filler. That’s the lot.
