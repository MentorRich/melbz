Transport in Moonee Ponds 2026: Trains, Trams & the Commute Reality

J
Jack Morrison Mar 21, 2026 Updated Mar 2026
Moonee-Ponds

Moonee Ponds’s transport game scored A- in our latest review. Here is what that actually means for your daily commute and weekend plans. Sitting 7km northwest of the CBD, Moonee Ponds has one of the better transport connections in the inner-northwest.

Getting to the CBD

By train: Moonee Ponds station is on the Craigieburn line. Trains to Flagstaff take about 12 to 15 minutes during peak, running every 6 to 10 minutes. This is genuinely fast and makes Moonee Ponds one of the best-connected suburbs at its price point.

By tram: Route 59 runs along Mount Alexander Road through Moonee Ponds and into the CBD via Flemington and North Melbourne. The tram takes longer than the train (about 25 to 35 minutes to the city depending on stops), but it drops you closer to specific destinations along the route.

By car: Driving to the CBD takes 15 to 30 minutes depending on traffic. CityLink is accessible from Moonee Ponds, and Mount Alexander Road runs directly into the city. Peak hour congestion on Mount Alexander Road is real, though — the train is faster during morning rush.

Train Details

Moonee Ponds station is the primary station, located a short walk from Puckle Street. The Craigieburn line runs through North Melbourne, Flagstaff, Melbourne Central, and Flinders Street.

Peak frequency: Every 6 to 10 minutes during morning and evening peak. Off-peak: Every 15 to 20 minutes. Night Network: Hourly all-night services on Friday and Saturday nights.

The station has a commuter car park, though it fills up early on weekday mornings. Consider walking or cycling to the station if you live within 1km.

Tram Routes

Route 59 runs along Mount Alexander Road and is the main tram service for Moonee Ponds. It connects north to Airport West and south through Flemington, North Melbourne, and into the CBD (Elizabeth Street).

Tram stops along Mount Alexander Road in Moonee Ponds are well-positioned for accessing Puckle Street and the surrounding residential areas. The tram is particularly useful if your destination is along the route rather than in the City Loop.

Bus Routes

Buses fill the cross-suburban gaps that the train and tram do not cover. Key routes connect Moonee Ponds to Essendon, Ascot Vale, Highpoint Shopping Centre, and the western suburbs. Frequency varies — main routes run every 15 to 20 minutes during peak, less often on weekends.

Cycling

Moonee Ponds is good for cycling. The Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is a dedicated shared path running along the creek, connecting south to Flemington and the Maribyrnong River trails, and north to Essendon. It is flat, well-maintained, and separated from traffic.

The road cycling on Mount Alexander Road is less comfortable due to traffic, but quieter residential streets provide alternative routes. Cycling to the CBD takes about 25 to 30 minutes via the creek trail and Capital City Trail.

Driving & Parking

Parking in Moonee Ponds is manageable compared to inner-city suburbs. Most houses have driveways or garages. Street parking near Puckle Street has time restrictions during business hours but is generally available in residential streets.

CityLink access is a major advantage — you can reach the western suburbs, airport, and Geelong direction without fighting through the CBD.

The Verdict

Moonee Ponds gets an A- for transport because it has train, tram, and cycling options that all work independently. The 12 to 15 minute train to the CBD is the headline, but the Route 59 tram and the Creek Trail give you genuine alternatives. Transport is one of the main reasons people choose this suburb.

FAQ

How long does it take to get from Moonee Ponds to the CBD? By train, about 12 to 15 minutes to Flagstaff. By tram (Route 59), about 25 to 35 minutes. By car, 15 to 30 minutes depending on traffic.

What train line is Moonee Ponds on? The Craigieburn line. Trains run through North Melbourne, Flagstaff, Melbourne Central, and Flinders Street.

Does Moonee Ponds have tram access? Yes. Route 59 runs along Mount Alexander Road through Moonee Ponds, connecting to Airport West in the north and the CBD in the south.

Is there night public transport in Moonee Ponds? Yes. Night Network services run hourly all night on Friday and Saturday on the Craigieburn line.
