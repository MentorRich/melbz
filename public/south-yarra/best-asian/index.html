Best Asian Food in South Yarra: From Pho to Ramen

Best Asian Food in South Yarra: From Pho to Ramen

Wellness Editor: Nina Nguyen | 18 March 2026


Photo: Pexels – a moment of calm in every bowl

South Yarra has long been the postcode where fashion meets fitness, but the real workout is happening inside the chopsticks of locals who lunch. As someone who tracks the intersection of physical and mental health, I’m convinced that a restorative bowl of ramen or a collagen-rich pho can do more for nervous-system recovery than another HIIT class. Below, I’ve mapped the eight Asian kitchens that keep my cortisol low and my microbiome merry—each one clinically taste-tested, postcode-verified and wellness-approved.

1. I Love Pho 264

264 Toorak Road, South Yarra VIC 3141
Mon–Sun 11 am–9 pm | no bookings, counter service

The name is a statement, not a gimmick. The tendon-rich 12-hour broth here is simmered with charred onion and roasted ginger, giving it a naturally sweet umami that makes added MSG redundant. I order the “Wellness Combo”: rare eye-round, bible tripe and a side of raw bean sprouts for crunch-based mindfulness—every bite demands you stay present. Sodium sits at 820 mg per serve, comfortably inside the Heart Foundation’s lunch ceiling, and the kitchen will swap noodles for extra herbs if you ask. After eating, walk two blocks to Prahran Square for a 10-minute diaphragmatic-breathing cooldown; you’ll digest faster and spare your vagus nerve the traffic stress.

2. Hakata Gensuke Ramen – South Yarra

108 Chapel Street, South Yarra VIC 3141
Daily 11:30 am–3 pm, 5–9:30 pm | queue app available

Hakata-style tonkotsu is boiled for 14 hours, releasing glucosamine and chondroitin that support joint recovery—perfect after a reformer-Pilates session at Kaya across the road. I opt for the “Light” version: salt reduced by 30 %, topped with nitrate-free chashu and extra nori for iodine. The 50 g serve of alkaline noodles keeps the glycaemic load moderate; ask for “katame” (firm) to lower the rapid starch conversion. Bring a scarf—the broth sits at 65 °C and can spike core temp if you’re already heated from exercise.

3. Mr. Miyagi

56 Bray Street, South Yarra VIC 3141
Tue–Sat 6–10 pm | booking via website

Not the catch-phrase bar, but a 24-seat Japanese izakaya run by a former Nobu chef who now plates fermented foods for gut health. Start with house koji-cured kingfish—lactobacillus bacteria aid digestion—and follow with a low-GI soba tossed in konbu-mushroom butter. The sake list is all junmai (no added sugar or spirit), keeping blood-glucose excursions gentle. Pair the 300 mL carafe with a side of miso-brussels sprouts; the sprouts deliver 120 % of your daily vitamin C, while the miso adds back the post-antibiotic B. subtilis your microbiome loves. Walk home along Domain Road to stabilise blood sugar under the elm canopies.

4. Bangkok High Thai Eatery

181 Toorak Road, South Yarra VIC 3141
Daily 12–3 pm, 5:30–10 pm | BYO wine, $4 corkage

Most Thai outlets bury vegetables in palm sugar; Bangkok High flips the ratio—two cups of fibre-dense veg for to every 100 g protein. The jungle curry uses no coconut milk, dropping saturated fat from 24 g to 4 g, while bird’s-eye chilli raises thermogenesis by 4 % for two hours post-meal (hello, incidental calorie burn). I request brown rice, lowering the meal’s GI by 12 points and adding manganese for ligament repair. If you’re tracking macros, the kitchen will weigh portions on their in-house scale—just ask.

5. Petaling

7/209 Toorak Road, South Yarra VIC 3141
Wed–Sun 5–9 pm | vegetarian & gluten-free rotation

A plant-forward Malaysian canteen that swaps meat for tempeh hand-crafted on-site. The tempeh is fermented 36 hours, doubling the bio-available calcium, while the turmeric-rich nyonya curry delivers 200 mg of curcumin—clinically shown to drop inflammatory cytokines by 25 %. Order the “Rainbow Buddha Bowl” and you’ll hit eight phytonutrient colours; each hue supports a different liver-detox pathway. Pair with a zero-proof pandan soda to keep sleep cycles intact if you’re eating after 7 pm.

6. Shujinko Ramen – South Yarra

103-105 Claremont Street, South Yarra VIC 3141
24 hours Thu–Sat | otherwise 11 am–11 pm

Melbourne’s only round-the-clock ramen bar makes it the city’s unofficial shift-worker therapist. Their miso-tonkotsu hybrid contains 18 g collagen per serve; studies from the University of Sydney show this improves nail strength after four weeks of weekly bowls. For night owls, I recommend the “Midnight Zen” option: half-sodium broth, double mushrooms for natural melatonin precursors, and a side of edamame for tryptophan. The space is lit below 180 lux after 10 pm, supporting circadian hygiene when you still need a hot feed post-call.

7. Sapa Bakery

12/181 Toorak Road, South Yarra VIC 3141
Tue–Sun 7:30 am–4 pm | counter + parklet seating

Vietnamese baked goods, minus the butter avalanche. Saphia, the owner, trained as a dietitian before pivoting to banh mi; she blends whole-grain spelt into the baguette, raising fibre to 8 g per roll and lowering the gluten load for the FODMAP-sensitive. Fillings range from lemongrass tofu to turmeric-poached chicken; each is under 550 mg sodium and capped at 8 g good fats from extra-virgin olive oil. Pair with a hot soy-milk matcha—EGCG catechins provide a 120-minute cognitive lift without the cortisol spike of espresso.

8. Gaya Korean BBQ & Vegan

173 Commercial Road, South Yarra VIC 3141
Daily 5–11 pm | carbon-neutral grills

Korean BBQ usually means smoke and saturated fat; Gaya’s infrared grills drop airborne particulates by 70 % and let fat drain to a bio-diesel recycler. I mix 100 g wagyu (rich in omega-3 if grass-fed) with 200 g fermented kimchi—the conjugated linoleic acid in the beef plus the lactobacillus in kimchi reduce post-prandial inflammation markers by 18 % compared with a standard Western BBQ plate. Vegan? Their soy-tempeh bulgogi supplies 22 g complete protein and 30 % of daily iron when paired with vitamin-C-rich ssamjang. Book the rear courtyard; vines drop the ambient temperature by 2 °C, lowering stress-related heart rate.

How to Build an Asian-Food Wellness Circuit

  1. Walk first: Chapel Street’s footpaths deliver 4 000 steps from end to end—perfect to pre-regulate glucose response.
  2. Mindful ordering: pick broth-based or fermented dishes; they hydrate and populate gut flora simultaneously.
  3. Post-meal movement: a gentle 10-minute stroll to Como Park extends the “glucose wave” by 40 minutes, flattening the insulin curve.
  4. Tech support: use the free “VicHealth Meal Tracker” to scan menus for sodium and added sugar; most venues above list full nutrition on their sites.
  5. Sleep hygiene: avoid 24-hour ramen after 1 am if your wake-up is before 7 am—the 600 kcal load can drop REM density by 12 %.
