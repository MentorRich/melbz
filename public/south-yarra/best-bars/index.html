Best Bars in South Yarra 2026

South Yarra’s bar scene continues to evolve, offering everything from sophisticated wine bars to energetic cocktail lounges. As one of Melbourne’s most dynamic suburbs, South Yarra attracts both locals and visitors seeking quality drinks and memorable experiences.

The Bottom End

Starting with a classic, The Bottom End at 1A Little Chapel Street, South Yarra VIC 3141, remains a cornerstone of the local bar scene. This unpretentious venue has been serving up cold beers and hearty pub fare for years. The outdoor beer garden is particularly popular during warmer months, offering a relaxed atmosphere where you can catch up with friends over a pot or two.

The Cullen Hotel Bar

Located at 164 Commercial Road, South Yarra VIC 3141, The Cullen Hotel Bar brings a touch of artistic flair to South Yarra’s drinking establishments. The bar takes its name from the hotel’s namesake artist, Adam Cullen, and features rotating exhibitions alongside its extensive drinks menu. Their cocktail list changes seasonally, incorporating fresh, local ingredients that reflect Melbourne’s commitment to quality.

The Village Brasserie

Their rooftop terrace has become one of South Yarra’s most popular spots for after-work drinks, offering panoramic views of the surrounding area. The bar’s menu focuses on craft beers and innovative cocktails, with a strong emphasis on seasonal ingredients.

For those seeking a more traditional pub experience, The Windsor Castle at 292-294 High Street, Prahran VIC 3181, (just across the South Yarra border) offers a welcoming atmosphere and a solid selection of beers on tap. While technically in Prahran, this historic venue is within easy walking distance of South Yarra and remains a favourite among locals from both suburbs.
