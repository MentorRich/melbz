Best Bars in Southbank 2026

Southbank’s bar scene has evolved into a sophisticated playground where riverside views meet inventive cocktails and Melbourne’s creative spirit. From hidden laneway gems to sky-high drinking destinations, here’s where to find the best pours in this riverside precinct.

1. The Emerald Room

Level 4, 88 Queensbridge St, Southbank VIC 3006 | Price: $$$

Hidden above a nondescript office building, The Emerald Room has become Southbank’s worst-kept secret. This Art Deco-inspired cocktail bar channels 1920s glamour with emerald velvet seating and crystal chandeliers. Their signature ‘Emerald City’ cocktail changes seasonally, currently featuring local quandong and finger lime. Insider tip: Book the private booth for groups of four or more—it comes with its own dedicated bartender.

2. Riverside Spirits

Southbank Promenade, Southbank VIC 3006 | Price: $$

Right on the Yarra’s edge, this open-air bar offers Melbourne’s best river views without the tourist trap prices. Their ‘Locals Hour’ (5-7pm weekdays) features $10 cocktails and $7 pints of local craft beer. The Aperol Spritz here tastes even better watching the sunset over Flinders Street Station. Insider tip: Skip the food menu—grab dumplings from the stall across the promenade and bring them over.

3. The Eureka Bar

Level 88, 7 Riverside Quay, Southbank VIC 3006 | Price: $$$$

Melbourne’s highest bar isn’t just about the view (though it’s spectacular). This sophisticated space serves molecular cocktails that play with temperature and texture. The ‘Melbourne Fog’ arrives under a cloche filled with eucalyptus smoke. Insider tip: Book sunset sessions at least two weeks ahead—they’re snapped up by both tourists and locals.

4. Understudy

Level 3, 28 Southgate Ave, Southbank VIC 3006 | Price: $$

Behind an unmarked door in the Arts Centre Melbourne, Understudy feels like Melbourne’s best-kept secret. Theatre industry folks gather here for pre-show drinks, creating a uniquely creative atmosphere. Their wine list focuses on small Victorian producers, with glasses starting at $10. Insider tip: Show your theatre ticket for a free drink upgrade.

5. Hopscotch Southbank

Shop 4, 10/11 Riverside Quay, Southbank VIC 3006 | Price: $$

This craft beer hall brings a casual energy to Southbank’s sometimes-stuffy bar scene. With 30 rotating taps and a beer garden that catches the afternoon sun, it’s perfect for groups. Their ‘Beer Paddle’ lets you sample four different local brews for $20. Insider tip: Tuesday nights feature brewery takeovers with the brewers themselves pouring pints.

6. The Boatbuilders Yard

23 South Wharf Promenade, Southbank VIC 3006 | Price: $$

While technically across the Yarra in the CBD, this sprawling waterfront venue deserves mention for its unbeatable atmosphere. Converted from a boatbuilding workshop, it features both indoor and outdoor spaces perfect for Melbourne’s unpredictable weather. Their Aperol Spritz jugs are legendary for summer afternoons. Insider tip: The back courtyard catches the last of the afternoon sun—arrive before 3pm to snag a table.

Getting There

All these venues are within walking distance of Flinders Street Station and Southbank’s free tram zone. For riverside bars, the 1 and 3 trams stop right outside. Most venues recommend booking, especially on weekends—Southbank’s popularity means these spots fill quickly.

The Southbank Bar Scene in 2026

What makes Southbank’s bar scene special is its evolution beyond tourist traps. While the riverside strip still has its share of overpriced venues, these under the radars showcase Melbourne’s true drinking culture: quality drinks, creative concepts, and that unmistakable local energy. Whether you’re after skyline views or tucked-away cocktail dens, Southbank delivers experiences that keep both tourists and locals coming back.
