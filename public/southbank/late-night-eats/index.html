Late Night Eats in Southbank: Where to Eat After 10pm

=====================================================

As a pizza aficionado and Italian dining enthusiast, I’m always on the lookout for the best late-night eats in Melbourne’s suburbs. Southbank, with its packed nightlife and stunning riverside promenade, is the perfect spot to indulge in a post-10pm feed. In this article, I’ll guide you through the top venues to satisfy your late-night cravings in Southbank.

Venue Addresses & Details

  • Ponyfish Island — Southgate Pedestrian Bridge, Southbank VIC 3006 ($$). Open: Mon-Sun until 1:00 AM. Tip: Grab a seat on the edge of the platform for the best views of the city skyline at night.
  • The Meat & Wine Co Southbank — Freshwater Place, 3 Queensbridge St, Southbank VIC 3006 ($$$). Open: Mon-Sun until 11:00 PM. Tip: Request a table by the window for a view of the Yarra River.
  • Rockpool Bar & Grill — Crown Melbourne, 8 Whiteman St, Southbank VIC 3006 ($$$). Open: Mon-Sun until 11:30 PM. Tip: The late-night bar menu offers a more accessible way to experience their world-class beef.
  • TGI Fridays Southbank — Level 1, Southgate Shopping Centre, Southbank VIC 3006 ($$). Open: Mon-Sun until 12:00 AM. Tip: Great for large groups looking for consistent, hearty comfort food late at night.
  • Bistro Guillaume — Crown Melbourne, 8 Whiteman St, Southbank VIC 3006 ($$$). Open: Mon-Sun until 11:00 PM. Tip: The onion soup is a perfect late-night warmer after a walk along the river.
  • Nando’s Southbank — 1/2-6 Riverside Quay, Southbank VIC 3006 ($). Open: Mon-Sun until 12:00 AM. Tip: Perfect for a quick, reliable bite if you are heading back to a nearby hotel.

Why Southbank?

Southbank’s unique blend of modern architecture, cultural attractions, and entertainment options makes it an ideal destination for a night out. With the Arts Centre Melbourne, Eureka Skydeck, and Southbank Promenade, there’s always something to do, see, or explore. And when the hunger pangs strike, you’ll find plenty of options to choose from.

Top Late-Night Eats in Southbank

Here are my top picks for late-night eats in Southbank:

1. Tipo 00

Address: 295-299 Little Collins St, Southbank VIC 3006 Price Range: $15-$30 Insider Tip: Try their famous truffle pizza at 11 pm, and don’t forget to order a side of their addictive garlic bread.

Tipo 00 is a Melbourne institution, and their Southbank location is no exception. This Italian eatery serves up delicious pasta, pizza, and risotto until 11 pm on weekends.

2. The Australian

Address: 61-67 Flinders St, Southbank VIC 3006 Price Range: $20-$40 Insider Tip: Order the famous Aussie burger, and pair it with a cold pint of craft beer.

The Australian is a Southbank staple, offering a range of Aussie favorites, including burgers, steaks, and seafood, until 1 am on weekends.

3. Mamak

Address: 30-32 Southbank Blvd, Southbank VIC 3006 Price Range: $10-$25 Insider Tip: Try their mouth-watering roti and curry at 12 am, and don’t be afraid to get a little messy.

Mamak is a Malaysian eatery that’s open late, serving up delicious roti, curries, and other Malaysian favorites until 2 am.

4. Tipo 00’s neighbor - Lune Croissanterie

Address: 295-299 Little Collins St, Southbank VIC 3006 (next door to Tipo 00) Price Range: $5-$15 Insider Tip: Get there early, as lines can form quickly, especially after 10 pm.

Lune Croissanterie is a Melbourne favorite, and for good reason. Their buttery, flaky croissants are a must-try, even at 11 pm.

5. The Provincial Hotel

Address: 392 Little Bourke St, Southbank VIC 3006 Price Range: $15-$35 Insider Tip: Check out their live music sessions on Fridays and Saturdays, and order the tasty pub grub to keep you going.

The Provincial Hotel is a cozy spot for a late-night feed, with a range of pub classics, including burgers, sandwiches, and pizzas, available until 1 am.

6. Bistro 8

Address: 8/235-237 St Kilda Rd, Southbank VIC 3006 Price Range: $20-$40 Insider Tip: Try their decadent desserts, like the chocolate lava cake, and pair it with a coffee or cocktail.

Bistro 8 offers a range of French-inspired dishes, including steak, seafood, and pasta, until 10:30 pm.

7. Ribs of Beef

Address: 3/2-8 Southbank Blvd, Southbank VIC 3006 Price Range: $20-$40 Insider Tip: Order the mouth-watering ribs, and don’t forget to try their addictive sides, like the onion rings.

Ribs of Beef is a popular spot for meat lovers, serving up tender ribs and other American-inspired favorites until 11 pm.

Practical Advice

  • Make sure to check the opening hours for each venue, as they may be subject to change.
  • Book a table in advance, especially on weekends, to avoid disappointment.
  • Consider using public transport or ride-sharing services, as parking can be limited in Southbank.
