Things to Do in Windsor Melbourne — 2026 Guide

O
Oscar Wei Mar 22, 2026
Windsor

What Is There to Do in Windsor?

More than you think for a suburb that is barely 1.5 square kilometres. The Chapel Street strip between High Street and Dandenong Road packs in enough bars, restaurants, parks, and retail to fill any weekend.

Outdoor Activities

Victoria Gardens — Windsor’s main green space between Chapel Street and Punt Road. Playground for families, off-leash dog zone during certain hours, and the unofficial picnic spot for takeaway pizza from Small Print (they will deliver to the park). Saturday mornings fill with runners, dog walkers, and families.

The Chapel Street Walk — Walk the full length of Windsor’s Chapel Street strip from the Prahran border to Dandenong Road. Pop into the boutiques, check out the vintage shops, window-shop the independent stores. Allow 45 minutes to an hour if you stop for coffee.

Cycling to St Kilda — Windsor is flat and well-connected to cycling paths heading south to the bay. An easy 15-20 minute ride gets you to St Kilda beach, and the return trip is flat the whole way.

Food and Drink Experiences

The Wednesday Specials Circuit — Cheeky Monkey does two-coffee-and-toast for $12 (unadvertised). Wonderland Bar runs $7-pot happy hour 4-6pm. String them together for a $30 Wednesday.

Chapel Street Pub Crawl — Start at The Railway Hotel (since the 1920s), walk south to The Wolf (bifold doors, street atmosphere), continue to The Windsor Alehouse (true locals’ pub), and finish at Lucky Coq (pizza-and-beer deal). Four pubs, all walkable, all different characters.

Jungle Boy Cocktail Experience — Find the fridge door inside Boston Sub sandwich shop, push through, and you are in a hidden tiki bar. It is worth doing at least once.

Culture and Creative

Live Music — The Railway Hotel, The Wolf, Hoo Haa, and The Osborne all book live acts and DJs. Check their socials weekly — there is usually something on Thursday through Sunday.

Vintage Shopping — Chapel Street’s southern end has maintained a strong concentration of vintage and thrift stores. Independent fashion, vinyl shops, and bookshops with staff who have actually read the books. This is what Chapel Street was before the chain stores took over the Prahran end.

Street Art and Architecture — Walk the residential streets off Chapel — Albert Street, Union Street, High Street — for Victorian terraces, Edwardian cottages, and the occasional commissioned mural on a laneway wall.

Family-Friendly Activities

  • Victoria Gardens playground — Well-maintained, shaded, space to run
  • Weekend brunch at family-friendly cafes — Franklin, High Society, and Staple all welcome families
  • Daytime pub meals — The Railway Hotel and The Wolf are kid-friendly before 6pm
  • The Windsor Castle Sunday roast ($24) — Family-friendly Sunday lunch with all the trimmings

Rainy Day Options

  • Better Days Bar — Board games, N64 consoles, and cocktails. Thursday night tournaments
  • Borsch, Vodka & Tears — Warm, bohemian atmosphere, 100+ vodkas, proper food
  • Chapel Street shopping — Browse the independent stores, especially the vintage shops
  • Her Bar — Intimate laneway cocktail bar for quiet conversation

FAQ

What is the best free thing to do in Windsor? Walk the residential streets on Albert Street and Union Street on a Sunday morning with takeaway coffee. The Victorian streetscape is genuinely beautiful.

Is there a cinema in Windsor? Not within the suburb itself, but the Jam Factory cinemas in South Yarra are a short tram ride north on the 78.

What is on this weekend in Windsor? Check The Railway Hotel, The Wolf, Hoo Haa, and The Osborne socials for live music and DJ sets. Victoria Gardens Market sometimes runs on Saturdays.

Verdict

Windsor fits more into its 1.5 square kilometres than suburbs five times its size. The combination of Chapel Street’s food and bar scene, Victoria Gardens’ green space, vintage shopping, and live music venues means you can fill a full weekend without leaving the suburb — and most Windsor locals do exactly that.
