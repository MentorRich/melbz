How the Vibe Score Works

The Suburb Vibe Score (0–100) measures how much is happening in a suburb right now. It's calculated weekly from five signals:

  • New openings — restaurants, cafes, bars, shops that opened recently
  • Event density — gigs, markets, festivals, community events
  • Search interest — how often people Google the suburb
  • Social buzz — mentions across social media and review platforms
  • Property movement — rental demand, new listings, price shifts

A score above 80 means something genuinely interesting is happening. Below 50 means it's a quiet week. Scores update every Sunday night.
